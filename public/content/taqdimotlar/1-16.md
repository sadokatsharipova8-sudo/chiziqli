## Ta'rif

:::tarif
$\mathcal A: V \to V$ akslantirish
$$\mathcal A(\alpha\mathbf x + \beta\mathbf y) = \alpha\mathcal A\mathbf x + \beta\mathcal A\mathbf y$$
shartni qanoatlantirsa — **chiziqli almashtirish** (chiziqli operator).
:::

Natijalar: $\mathcal A\mathbf 0 = \mathbf 0$; to'g'ri chiziq to'g'ri chiziqqa (yoki nuqtaga) o'tadi; parallel chiziqlar parallel qoladi.

---

## Geometrik misollar

- $\varphi$ burchakka **burish**;
- $Ox$ o'qqa **proyeksiyalash**;
- $Ox$ ga nisbatan **simmetriya**;
- $k$ marta **gomotetiya**: $\mathcal A\mathbf x = k\mathbf x$;
- **siljish** (sdvig): $(x, y) \mapsto (x + ky, y)$.

---

## Algebraik misollar

- **Differensiallash:** $D: P_n \to P_n$, $Dp = p'$ — chiziqli: $(\alpha p + \beta q)' = \alpha p' + \beta q'$.
- **Transponirlash:** $M_{n\times n} \to M_{n\times n}$.
- Nol operator $\mathcal O\mathbf x = \mathbf 0$ va ayniy $\mathcal E\mathbf x = \mathbf x$.

---

## Kontrmisollar

- Siljitish $\mathbf x \mapsto \mathbf x + \mathbf a$ ($\mathbf a \ne \mathbf 0$) — chiziqli emas: $\mathbf 0 \mapsto \mathbf a$.
- $(x, y) \mapsto (x^2, y)$ — chiziqli emas: $2\mathbf x \mapsto (4x^2, 2y) \ne 2(x^2, y)$.
- $\mathbf x \mapsto |\mathbf x|$ — chiziqli emas.

:::savol
Qaysi akslantirish chiziqli?
- $(x, y) \mapsto (x + 1, y)$
+ $(x, y) \mapsto (2x - y, x + 3y)$
- $(x, y) \mapsto (xy, x)$
- $(x, y) \mapsto (\sin x, y)$
:::

---

## Bazis yetarli!

$\mathbf x = \sum x_j\mathbf e_j$ bo'lsa:
$$\mathcal A\mathbf x = \sum x_j\,\mathcal A\mathbf e_j.$$

Operator **bazis vektorlarining obrazlari** bilan to'liq aniqlanadi. $n$ ta vektor — $n^2$ ta son — matritsa.

---

## Operator matritsasi

:::tarif
$\mathcal A\mathbf e_j = \sum_i a_{ij}\mathbf e_i$. $j$-ustuni $\mathcal A\mathbf e_j$ ning koordinatalaridan iborat $A = (a_{ij})$ — operator **matritsasi**.
:::

:::teorema
$\mathbf y = \mathcal A\mathbf x \Rightarrow Y = AX$.
:::

Qoida: **bazis vektorlari obrazlari — ustunlarga**.

---

## Misol: burish

$\mathcal A\mathbf i = (\cos\varphi, \sin\varphi)$, $\mathcal A\mathbf j = (-\sin\varphi, \cos\varphi)$:
$$A = \begin{pmatrix} \cos\varphi & -\sin\varphi\\ \sin\varphi & \cos\varphi\end{pmatrix}.$$

$90^\circ$: $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & -1\\ 1 & 0\end{pmatrix}$; $(1, 0) \mapsto (0, 1)$, $(0, 1) \mapsto (-1, 0)$.

---

## Misol: proyeksiya va simmetriyalar

| Almashtirish | Matritsa |
|---|---|
| $Ox$ ga proyeksiya | $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0\\ 0 & 0\end{pmatrix}$ |
| $Ox$ ga nisbatan simmetriya | $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0\\ 0 & -1\end{pmatrix}$ |
| $y = x$ ga nisbatan simmetriya | $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1\\ 1 & 0\end{pmatrix}$ |
| siljish $(x + ky, y)$ | $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & k\\ 0 & 1\end{pmatrix}$ |

---

## Misol: differensiallash

$P_2$, bazis $1, t, t^2$: $D1 = 0$, $Dt = 1$, $Dt^2 = 2t$:
$$D = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 & 0\\ 0 & 0 & 2\\ 0 & 0 & 0\end{pmatrix}.$$

$p = 3 - 2t + 5t^2 \to (3, -2, 5)^T$: $DX = (-2, 10, 0)^T \to p' = -2 + 10t$ ✓

---

## Mashq: matritsa

:::savol
$\begin{pmatrix} 0 & -1\\ 1 & 0\end{pmatrix}$ qanday almashtirish?
- O'qqa nisbatan simmetriya
+ $90^\circ$ ga burish
- Siljitish
- Proyeksiya
:::

## Vektor obrazi

$A = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 2\\ 2 & -1\end{pmatrix}$, $\mathbf v = (-4;\ 1)$:
$$A\mathbf v = \begin{pmatrix} 0\cdot(-4) + 2\cdot1\\ 2\cdot(-4) + (-1)\cdot1\end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 2\\ -9\end{pmatrix}.$$

## Qo'shish va songa ko'paytirish

:::tarif
- $(\mathcal A + \mathcal B)\mathbf x = \mathcal A\mathbf x + \mathcal B\mathbf x$
- $(\lambda\mathcal A)\mathbf x = \lambda(\mathcal A\mathbf x)$
:::

:::teorema
Bir bazisda: $\mathcal A + \mathcal B \leftrightarrow A + B$, $\lambda\mathcal A \leftrightarrow \lambda A$.
:::

Operatorlar to'plami — $n^2$ o'lchamli chiziqli fazo.

---

## Ko'paytirish

:::tarif
$$(\mathcal A\mathcal B)\mathbf x = \mathcal A(\mathcal B\mathbf x)$$
— **avval $\mathcal B$, keyin $\mathcal A$**.
:::

:::teorema
$\mathcal A\mathcal B \leftrightarrow AB$.
:::

Isbot: $Y = B X$, $Z = AY = A(BX) = (AB)X$.

**Matritsalar ko'paytmasining asl ma'nosi — almashtirishlarni ketma-ket bajarish!**

---

## Ko'paytma: misol

$\mathcal A$ — $90^\circ$ burish, $\mathcal B$ — $Ox$ ga nisbatan simmetriya:
$$AB = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & -1\\ 1 & 0\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0\\ 0 & -1\end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1\\ 1 & 0\end{pmatrix}.$$

$\mathcal A\mathcal B$ — $y = x$ ga nisbatan simmetriya.

---

## Tartib muhim!

$$BA = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0\\ 0 & -1\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} 0 & -1\\ 1 & 0\end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & -1\\ -1 & 0\end{pmatrix}$$
— $y = -x$ ga nisbatan simmetriya.

$AB \ne BA$: «avval buramiz, keyin aks ettiramiz» ≠ «avval aks ettiramiz, keyin buramiz».

---

## Burishlar ko'paytmasi

$R_\alpha R_\beta = R_{\alpha + \beta}$:
$$\begin{pmatrix} \cos\alpha & -\sin\alpha\\ \sin\alpha & \cos\alpha\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} \cos\beta & -\sin\beta\\ \sin\beta & \cos\beta\end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} \cos(\alpha + \beta) & -\sin(\alpha + \beta)\\ \sin(\alpha + \beta) & \cos(\alpha + \beta)\end{pmatrix}.$$

Trigonometriyaning qo'shish formulalari — matritsa ko'paytmasidan!

---

## Mashq: kompozitsiya

:::savol
$A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 0\\ 0 & 1\end{pmatrix}$ ($Ox$ bo'ylab cho'zish), $B = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & -1\\ 1 & 0\end{pmatrix}$ (burish). Avval $B$, keyin $A$ matritsasi?
+ $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & -2\\ 1 & 0\end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & -1\\ 2 & 0\end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & -1\\ 1 & 1\end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 2\\ -1 & 0\end{pmatrix}$
> $AB$.
:::

## Darajalar

$\mathcal A^k = \underbrace{\mathcal A\cdots\mathcal A}_k$ $\leftrightarrow$ $A^k$.

- Proyeksiya: $P^2 = P$ (idempotent).
- Simmetriya: $S^2 = E$.
- Differensiallash $P_2$ da: $D^3 = O$ (nilpotent) — uch marta hosila olsak, ikkinchi darajali ko'phad nolga aylanadi.

---

## Teskari operator

:::tarif
$\mathcal A\mathcal A^{-1} = \mathcal A^{-1}\mathcal A = \mathcal E$.
:::

:::teorema
$\mathcal A^{-1}$ mavjud $\iff \det A \ne 0$ (**xosmas** operator); matritsasi $A^{-1}$; $(\mathcal A\mathcal B)^{-1} = \mathcal B^{-1}\mathcal A^{-1}$.
:::

- Burish $\varphi$ ning teskarisi — burish $-\varphi$.
- Proyeksiyaning teskarisi yo'q ($\det = 0$): ma'lumot yo'qoladi.

---

:::savol

- $\mathcal O$
+ $\mathcal P$
- $\mathcal E$
- $-\mathcal P$
:::

## Fazoda burish

$Oz$ o'qi atrofida $\varphi$ ga burish:
$$R_z = \begin{pmatrix} \cos\varphi & -\sin\varphi & 0\\ \sin\varphi & \cos\varphi & 0\\ 0 & 0 & 1\end{pmatrix}.$$

$\mathbf k$ o'zgarmaydi ($R_z\mathbf k = \mathbf k$) — burish o'qi. Samolyot va dronlarni boshqarishda uchta shunday burish ketma-ketligi ishlatiladi.

## Tipik xatolar

- Obrazlarni ustun emas, satr qilib yozish.
- $\mathcal A\mathcal B$ matritsasini $BA$ deb olish.
- Siljitishni ($\mathbf x + \mathbf a$) chiziqli deb hisoblash.
- Xos operatorga teskarisi bor deb o'ylash.

---

## Xulosa

- Chiziqli operator chiziqli kombinatsiyani saqlaydi.
- Matritsa: ustunlar — bazis vektorlari obrazlari; $Y = AX$.
- $\mathcal A + \mathcal B \leftrightarrow A + B$, $\mathcal A\mathcal B \leftrightarrow AB$.
- $\mathcal A^{-1}$ mavjud $\iff \det A \ne 0$.
