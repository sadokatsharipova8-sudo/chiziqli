## Fazo ichidagi fazo

$\mathbb R^3$ ichida «tekis» obyektlar: koordinata boshidan o'tuvchi to'g'ri chiziq va tekislik. Ular ham qo'shish va cho'zishga nisbatan yopiq — o'zlari ham chiziqli fazo.

Bunday to'plamlar — **qism fazolar**.

---

## Ta'rif

:::tarif
$V$ ning bo'sh bo'lmagan $L$ qism to'plami **qism fazo**, agar
$$\mathbf x, \mathbf y \in L\ \Rightarrow\ \alpha\mathbf x + \beta\mathbf y \in L.$$
:::

Qism fazo o'zi chiziqli fazo: aksiomalar $V$ dan meros qoladi, $\mathbf 0 = 0\cdot\mathbf x \in L$, $-\mathbf x = (-1)\mathbf x \in L$.

---

## Tekshirish rejasi

1. $\mathbf 0 \in L$? (Yo'q bo'lsa — darhol «qism fazo emas».)
2. $\mathbf x, \mathbf y \in L \Rightarrow \mathbf x + \mathbf y \in L$?
3. $\mathbf x \in L \Rightarrow \lambda\mathbf x \in L$?

---

## Misollar

- $\{\mathbf 0\}$ va $V$ — trivial qism fazolar.
- $\mathbb R^3$: boshdan o'tuvchi to'g'ri chiziqlar va tekisliklar.
- $AX = O$ yechimlari — $\mathbb R^n$ ning qism fazosi.
- $M_{n\times n}$ da simmetrik matritsalar; diagonal matritsalar.
- $P_n$ da $p(1) = 0$ shartli ko'phadlar.

---

## Kontrmisollar

- $x + y + z = 1$ — $\mathbf 0$ yo'q.
- $AX = B$, $B \ne O$ yechimlari — $\mathbf 0$ yo'q.
- Birinchi oktant $x, y, z \ge 0$ — $(-1)\mathbf x$ chiqib ketadi.
- Ikki to'g'ri chiziq birlashmasi — yig'indi chiqib ketadi.

:::savol
Qaysi biri $\mathbb R^3$ ning qism fazosi?
- $x + y + z = 1$
+ $x + y + z = 0$
- birlik sfera
- birinchi oktant
:::

---

## Misol: $AX = O$ yechimlari

$X_1$, $X_2$ — yechimlar:
$$A(\alpha X_1 + \beta X_2) = \alpha AX_1 + \beta AX_2 = O.$$

Demak yechimlar to'plami — qism fazo, o'lchami $n - r$ (FYS, 8-mavzu).

$AX = B$ ($B \ne O$) uchun esa $A(X_1 + X_2) = 2B \ne B$ — yopiq emas.

---

## Chiziqli qobiq

:::tarif
$$L(\mathbf a_1, \dots, \mathbf a_k) = \{\lambda_1\mathbf a_1 + \dots + \lambda_k\mathbf a_k\}.$$
:::

- Bu — qism fazo.
- $\mathbf a_1, \dots, \mathbf a_k$ ni o'z ichiga oluvchi **eng kichik** qism fazo.
- $\mathbb R^3$ da: $L(\mathbf a)$ — to'g'ri chiziq, $L(\mathbf a, \mathbf b)$ — tekislik (agar erkli bo'lsa).

---

## Chiziqli qobiq o'lchami

:::teorema
$$\dim L(\mathbf a_1, \dots, \mathbf a_k) = r(\mathbf a_1, \dots, \mathbf a_k).$$
Bazis — maksimal erkli qism sistema.
:::

:::misol
$L\big((1, 2, 3), (2, 1, 0), (4, 5, 6)\big)$: rang 2 — tekislik, bazis $(1, 2, 3), (2, 1, 0)$.
:::

---

## Qism fazo o'lchami

:::xossa
- $L \subset V \Rightarrow \dim L \le \dim V$; tenglik $\iff L = V$.
- $L$ ning istalgan bazisini $V$ ning bazisigacha **to'ldirish** mumkin.
:::

Masalan, $(1, 1, 0)$ ni $\mathbb R^3$ bazisigacha to'ldirish: $(1, 1, 0), (0, 1, 0), (0, 0, 1)$ (determinant $= 1 \ne 0$).

---

## Kesishma

:::tarif
$L_1 \cap L_2$ — ikkala qism fazoga tegishli vektorlar.
:::

:::teorema
Kesishma — qism fazo.
:::

Isbot: $\mathbf x, \mathbf y \in L_1 \cap L_2 \Rightarrow \alpha\mathbf x + \beta\mathbf y$ ham $L_1$ da, ham $L_2$ da.

Misol: $\mathbb R^3$ da ikki tekislik kesishmasi — to'g'ri chiziq.

---

## Yig'indi

:::tarif
$$L_1 + L_2 = \{\mathbf x_1 + \mathbf x_2 : \mathbf x_1 \in L_1,\ \mathbf x_2 \in L_2\}.$$
:::

- Yig'indi — qism fazo, $L_1$ va $L_2$ ni o'z ichiga oluvchi eng kichik qism fazo.
- $L_1 = L(\mathbf a_i)$, $L_2 = L(\mathbf b_j)$ bo'lsa: $L_1 + L_2 = L(\mathbf a_i, \mathbf b_j)$.

:::diqqat
Birlashma $L_1 \cup L_2$ umuman qism fazo **emas**!
:::

---

## Grassman formulasi

:::teorema
$$\dim(L_1 + L_2) = \dim L_1 + \dim L_2 - \dim(L_1 \cap L_2).$$
:::

To'plamlar uchun «kiritish-chiqarish» formulasiga o'xshaydi: kesishma ikki marta sanalmasligi kerak.

---

## Grassman: geometrik misol

$\mathbb R^3$ da ikki turli tekislik (boshdan o'tuvchi):
- $\dim L_1 = \dim L_2 = 2$
- $L_1 + L_2 = \mathbb R^3$
- $\dim(L_1 \cap L_2) = 2 + 2 - 3 = 1$ — to'g'ri chiziq ✓

Tekislik va unda yotmagan to'g'ri chiziq: $2 + 1 - 3 = 0$ — faqat $\mathbf 0$ da kesishadi.

---

## Hisoblash algoritmi

1. $L_1$, $L_2$ bazis vektorlarini bitta matritsaga satr qilib yozish.
2. $r$ = rang $= \dim(L_1 + L_2)$.
3. $\dim(L_1 \cap L_2) = \dim L_1 + \dim L_2 - r$.
4. Kesishma bazisi: $\sum\alpha_i\mathbf a_i = \sum\beta_j\mathbf b_j$ sistemani yechish.

---

## Misol (1)

$\mathbb R^4$: $L_1 = L(\mathbf a_1, \mathbf a_2)$, $L_2 = L(\mathbf b_1, \mathbf b_2)$:
$$\mathbf a_1 = (1, 0, 1, 0),\ \mathbf a_2 = (0, 1, 0, 1),\ \mathbf b_1 = (1, 1, 0, 0),\ \mathbf b_2 = (0, 0, 1, 1).$$

Kuzatuv: $\mathbf a_1 + \mathbf a_2 = (1, 1, 1, 1) = \mathbf b_1 + \mathbf b_2$.

---

## Misol (2)

- To'rtala vektor rangi 3 ($\mathbf a_1, \mathbf a_2, \mathbf b_1$ erkli, $\mathbf b_2 = \mathbf a_1 + \mathbf a_2 - \mathbf b_1$).
- $\dim(L_1 + L_2) = 3$.
- $\dim(L_1 \cap L_2) = 2 + 2 - 3 = 1$.
- Kesishma bazisi: $(1, 1, 1, 1)$.

---

## Mashq: Grassman

:::savol
$\mathbb R^4$ da $\dim L_1 = 3$, $\dim L_2 = 2$, $L_1 + L_2 = \mathbb R^4$. $\dim(L_1 \cap L_2)$?
- 0
+ 1
- 2
- 5
:::

---

## Mashq: minimal kesishma

:::savol
$\mathbb R^5$ da ikkita 3-o'lchamli qism fazo kesishmasining o'lchami kamida nechaga teng?
- 0
+ 1
- 2
- 3
> $\dim(L_1 \cap L_2) = 3 + 3 - \dim(L_1 + L_2) \ge 6 - 5 = 1$.
:::

---

## To'g'ri yig'indi

:::tarif
$L_1 \cap L_2 = \{\mathbf 0\}$ — **to'g'ri yig'indi**: $L_1 \oplus L_2$.
:::

:::teorema
$L = L_1 \oplus L_2 \iff$ har bir vektor **yagona** usulda $\mathbf x_1 + \mathbf x_2$ ko'rinishda yoziladi. $\dim L = \dim L_1 + \dim L_2$.
:::

---

## Yagonalik isboti

$\mathbf x_1 + \mathbf x_2 = \mathbf y_1 + \mathbf y_2$ bo'lsa,
$$\mathbf x_1 - \mathbf y_1 = \mathbf y_2 - \mathbf x_2 \in L_1 \cap L_2 = \{\mathbf 0\}.$$

Demak $\mathbf x_1 = \mathbf y_1$, $\mathbf x_2 = \mathbf y_2$.

---

## To'g'ri yig'indi: misollar

- $\mathbb R^3 = (Oxy\ \text{tekislik}) \oplus (Oz\ \text{o'qi})$.
- $M_{n\times n} = \{\text{simmetrik}\} \oplus \{\text{kososimmetrik}\}$:
$$A = \tfrac12(A + A^T) + \tfrac12(A - A^T).$$
O'lchamlar: $\frac{n(n+1)}{2} + \frac{n(n-1)}{2} = n^2$ ✓
- Funksiyalar: juft $\oplus$ toq.

---

## Tatbiq: signal = foydali + shovqin

Signallarni qayta ishlashda fazo «foydali signal» va «shovqin» qism fazolariga ajratiladi; filtrlash — signalning bitta qism fazodagi tashkil etuvchisini olish.

11–12-mavzularda bu ajratish **ortogonal** bo'ladi — eng kichik kvadratlar usuli.

---

## Kesishma bazisini topish

$L_1 = L(\mathbf a_1, \mathbf a_2)$, $L_2 = L(\mathbf b_1, \mathbf b_2)$ (yuqoridagi misol). $\mathbf x \in L_1 \cap L_2$:
$$\alpha_1\mathbf a_1 + \alpha_2\mathbf a_2 = \beta_1\mathbf b_1 + \beta_2\mathbf b_2.$$

Bu $\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \beta_1, \beta_2$ ga nisbatan bir jinsli sistema; yechim $\alpha_1 = \alpha_2 = \beta_1 = \beta_2 = t$ — kesishma $L\big((1, 1, 1, 1)\big)$ ✓

---

## Mashq: to'g'ri yig'indi

:::savol
$\mathbb R^3$ da $L_1 = L\big((1, 0, 0), (0, 1, 0)\big)$ va $L_2 = L\big((1, 1, 0)\big)$. $L_1 + L_2$ to'g'ri yig'indimi?
- Ha
+ Yo'q — $L_2 \subset L_1$
- Faqat $\mathbb R^3$ da
- Aniqlab bo'lmaydi
> $(1, 1, 0)$ — $L_1$ ning vektori; kesishma $L_2$ ning o'zi.
:::

---

## Tipik xatolar

- $\mathbf 0$ ni tekshirmasdan «qism fazo» deyish.
- Yig'indi bilan birlashmani chalkashtirish.
- $\dim(L_1 + L_2) = \dim L_1 + \dim L_2$ deb yozish (faqat to'g'ri yig'indi uchun).
- Yig'indi o'lchamini vektorlar soni deb olish (rang kerak).

---

## Xulosa

- Qism fazo — chiziqli kombinatsiyalarga nisbatan yopiq to'plam.
- $\dim L(\mathbf a_i) = r(\mathbf a_i)$.
- $L_1 \cap L_2$, $L_1 + L_2$ — qism fazolar.
- Grassman: $\dim(L_1 + L_2) = \dim L_1 + \dim L_2 - \dim(L_1 \cap L_2)$.
- To'g'ri yig'indi — yagona yoyilma.
