## Tekislikdan fazoga

| Tekislikda (7-mavzu) | Fazoda |
|---|---|
| $Ax + By + C = 0$ — to'g'ri chiziq | $Ax + By + Cz + D = 0$ — **tekislik** |
| $\mathbf n = (A;\ B)$ | $\mathbf n = (A;\ B;\ C)$ |
| nuqtadan chiziqqacha masofa | nuqtadan tekislikkacha masofa |

---

## Hayotda

- **Arxitektura:** tom qiyaligi — tekisliklar orasidagi burchak.
- **3D grafika:** har bir uchburchak — tekislik bo'lagi; yoritish normal orqali hisoblanadi.
- **Geologiya:** qatlamlarning yotish tekisligi.
- **Mashinali o'rganish:** sinflarni ajratuvchi «giper-tekislik» (SVM).

---

## Umumiy tenglama

:::teorema
Fazoda har qanday tekislik birinchi darajali tenglama bilan beriladi va aksincha:
$$Ax + By + Cz + D = 0,\qquad \mathbf n = (A;\ B;\ C) \ne \mathbf 0.$$
:::

$\mathbf n$ — tekislikka perpendikulyar **normal vektor**.

---

## Nega $\mathbf n$ perpendikulyar?

$M_1$, $M_2$ tekislikda bo'lsa, tenglamalarni ayiramiz:
$$A(x_2 - x_1) + B(y_2 - y_1) + C(z_2 - z_1) = 0,$$
ya'ni $\mathbf n\cdot\overrightarrow{M_1M_2} = 0$ — $\mathbf n$ tekislikdagi istalgan vektorga perpendikulyar.

---

## Xususiy hollar

| Shart | Tekislik |
|---|---|
| $D = 0$ | koordinata boshidan o'tadi |
| $C = 0$ | $Oz$ o'qiga parallel |
| $B = C = 0$ | $Oyz$ ga parallel: $x = \text{const}$ |
| $C = D = 0$ | $Oz$ o'qidan o'tadi |
| $z = 0$ | $Oxy$ koordinata tekisligi |

---

## Mashq: xususiy hol

:::savol
$2x - 3y + 5 = 0$ fazoda qanday tekislik?
+ $Oz$ o'qiga parallel
- $Oxy$ ga parallel
- Koordinata boshidan o'tadi
- $Ox$ o'qiga parallel
> $C = 0$.
:::

---

## Nuqta va normal bo'yicha

$M_0(x_0;\ y_0;\ z_0)$, $\mathbf n = (A;\ B;\ C)$:
$$A(x - x_0) + B(y - y_0) + C(z - z_0) = 0.$$

:::misol
$M_0(1;\ -2;\ 3)$, $\mathbf n = (2;\ 1;\ -1)$: $2(x - 1) + (y + 2) - (z - 3) = 0$, ya'ni $2x + y - z + 3 = 0$.
:::

---

## Parallel tekislik

$A(1;\ 2;\ 3)$ dan o'tuvchi va $x - y + z = 0$ ga parallel tekislik:
- normal o'sha: $(1;\ -1;\ 1)$;
- $(x - 1) - (y - 2) + (z - 3) = 0 \Rightarrow x - y + z - 2 = 0$.

---

## Uch nuqta orqali

$M, M_1, M_2, M_3$ bitta tekislikda $\iff$ $\overrightarrow{M_1M}$, $\overrightarrow{M_1M_2}$, $\overrightarrow{M_1M_3}$ komplanar (4-mavzu):
$$\begin{vmatrix} x - x_1 & y - y_1 & z - z_1\\ x_2 - x_1 & y_2 - y_1 & z_2 - z_1\\ x_3 - x_1 & y_3 - y_1 & z_3 - z_1\end{vmatrix} = 0.$$

---

## Uch nuqta: misol

$M_1(1;\ 1;\ 1)$, $M_2(2;\ 3;\ 1)$, $M_3(1;\ 2;\ 3)$:
$$\begin{vmatrix} x - 1 & y - 1 & z - 1\\ 1 & 2 & 0\\ 0 & 1 & 2\end{vmatrix} = 4(x - 1) - 2(y - 1) + (z - 1) = 0.$$

$4x - 2y + z - 3 = 0$. Tekshirish: $M_2$: $8 - 6 + 1 - 3 = 0$ ✓

---

## Kesmalarda tenglama

$$\frac xa + \frac yb + \frac zc = 1$$
— tekislik o'qlarni $(a;0;0)$, $(0;b;0)$, $(0;0;c)$ da kesadi.

:::savol
$2x - 3y + 6z - 12 = 0$ tekislik $Oz$ dan qanday kesma ajratadi?
- 6
+ 2
- −12
- 12
> $\frac x6 + \frac{y}{-4} + \frac z2 = 1$.
:::

---

## Normal tenglama

$$x\cos\alpha + y\cos\beta + z\cos\gamma - p = 0,$$
$p \ge 0$ — boshdan masofa, $(\cos\alpha, \cos\beta, \cos\gamma)$ — birlik normal.

Umumiydan: $\mu = \pm\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{A^2 + B^2 + C^2}}$ ga ko'paytiramiz, $\mu$ ishorasi $D$ ga teskari.

:::misol
$2x - y + 2z + 6 = 0$: $\mu = -\frac13$: $-\frac23x + \frac13y - \frac23z - 2 = 0$, $p = 2$.
:::

---

## Ikki tekislik: vaziyatlar

Ikki tekislik: **kesishadi** (to'g'ri chiziq bo'ylab), **parallel** yoki **ustma-ust**.

:::xossa
- Ustma-ust: $\frac{A_1}{A_2} = \frac{B_1}{B_2} = \frac{C_1}{C_2} = \frac{D_1}{D_2}$
- Parallel: $\frac{A_1}{A_2} = \frac{B_1}{B_2} = \frac{C_1}{C_2} \ne \frac{D_1}{D_2}$
- Perpendikulyar: $A_1A_2 + B_1B_2 + C_1C_2 = 0$
:::

---

## Tekisliklar orasidagi burchak

Normallar orasidagi burchak:
$$\cos\varphi = \frac{|A_1A_2 + B_1B_2 + C_1C_2|}{\sqrt{A_1^2 + B_1^2 + C_1^2}\sqrt{A_2^2 + B_2^2 + C_2^2}}.$$

:::misol
$x + 2y + 2z - 3 = 0$ va $6x + 2y - 3z + 1 = 0$: $\cos\varphi = \frac{|6 + 4 - 6|}{3\cdot7} = \frac{4}{21}$.
:::

---

## Mashq: perpendikulyarlik

:::savol
$2x - y + 3z = 1$ va $x + 5y + kz = 0$ qaysi $k$ da perpendikulyar?
+ $k = 1$
- $k = -1$
- $k = 3$
- $k = 0$
:::

---

## Mashq: parallellik

:::savol
$2x - 4y + 6z - 1 = 0$ va $x - 2y + 3z + 5 = 0$?
+ Parallel
- Ustma-ust
- Perpendikulyar
- Kesishadi
> Normallar proporsional, ozod hadlar — yo'q.
:::

## Nuqtadan tekislikkacha masofa

:::teorema
$$d = \frac{|Ax_0 + By_0 + Cz_0 + D|}{\sqrt{A^2 + B^2 + C^2}}.$$
:::

Isbot: tekislikdagi $M_1$ dan $M_0$ ga vektorning $\mathbf n$ dagi proyeksiyasi moduli:
$$d = \frac{|\mathbf n\cdot\overrightarrow{M_1M_0}|}{|\mathbf n|}.$$

---

## Masofa: misollar

- $M(1;\ 2;\ 3)$ dan $2x - y + 2z + 5 = 0$ gacha: $d = \frac{|2 - 2 + 6 + 5|}{3} = \frac{11}{3}$.
- $(1;\ 1;\ 1)$ dan $2x + 2y + z - 2 = 0$ gacha: $d = \frac{|2 + 2 + 1 - 2|}{3} = 1$.

Mashqlar bo'limidagi «Nuqtadan tekislikkacha masofa» masalalari shunday.

---

## Parallel tekisliklar orasidagi masofa

$Ax + By + Cz + D_1 = 0$ va $Ax + By + Cz + D_2 = 0$:
$$d = \frac{|D_1 - D_2|}{\sqrt{A^2 + B^2 + C^2}}.$$

:::misol
$x - 2y + 2z - 1 = 0$ va $x - 2y + 2z + 5 = 0$: $d = \frac{6}{3} = 2$.
:::

---

## Ishora ma'nosi

$\delta = Ax_0 + By_0 + Cz_0 + D$:
- $\delta > 0$ — nuqta normal yo'nalgan tomonda;
- $\delta < 0$ — qarama-qarshi tomonda;
- $\delta = 0$ — tekislikda.

Ikki nuqta tekislikning bir tomonidami? — $\delta_1\delta_2 > 0$.

---

## Tekisliklar dastasi

Kesishuvchi ikki tekislik orqali o'tuvchi barcha tekisliklar:
$$\alpha(A_1x + B_1y + C_1z + D_1) + \beta(A_2x + B_2y + C_2z + D_2) = 0.$$

:::misol
$x + y - z - 1 = 0$ va $2x - y + z - 2 = 0$ kesishmasidan va $O$ dan o'tuvchi tekislik: $\alpha(-1) + \beta(-2) = 0 \Rightarrow \alpha = 2$, $\beta = -1$:
$$2(x + y - z - 1) - (2x - y + z - 2) = 3y - 3z = 0 \Rightarrow y = z.$$
:::

---

## Tatbiq: kompyuter grafikasi

Lambert qonuni: sirt yoritilganligi
$$I = I_0\cos\theta = I_0\,\mathbf n^0\cdot\mathbf l^0,$$
$\mathbf n^0$ — birlik normal, $\mathbf l^0$ — yorug'lik yo'nalishi. Har bir piksel uchun skalyar ko'paytma!

---

## Tatbiq: mashinali o'rganish

Ikki sinfni (masalan, «spam» va «spam emas») ajratuvchi **giper-tekislik**:
$$\mathbf w\cdot\mathbf x + b = 0.$$

Yangi obyekt sinfi — $\mathbf w\cdot\mathbf x + b$ ishorasi; ishonchlilik — tekislikkacha masofa. Bu SVM usulining g'oyasi.

---

## Mashq: masofa

:::savol
$O(0;\ 0;\ 0)$ dan $2x + y - 2z - 9 = 0$ gacha masofa?
- 9
+ 3
- $\frac95$
- 1
> $\frac{9}{\sqrt{4 + 1 + 4}} = 3$.
:::

## Tipik xatolar

- Burchak formulasida modulni unutish (o'tmas burchak chiqadi).
- Masofa formulasida ildizni unutish.
- Normal tenglamaga keltirishda $\mu$ ishorasini noto'g'ri tanlash.
- Uch nuqtali determinantni noto'g'ri yoyish.

---

## Xulosa

- $Ax + By + Cz + D = 0$, $\mathbf n = (A;\ B;\ C)$.
- Nuqta+normal, uch nuqta, kesmalar, normal tenglama.
- $\cos\varphi = \frac{|\mathbf n_1\cdot\mathbf n_2|}{|\mathbf n_1||\mathbf n_2|}$; parallel — normallar proporsional.
- $d = \frac{|Ax_0 + By_0 + Cz_0 + D|}{|\mathbf n|}$.
