## Kanonik tenglama

$M_0(x_0;\ y_0;\ z_0)$ va yo'naltiruvchi $\mathbf s = (l;\ m;\ n)$:
$$\frac{x - x_0}{l} = \frac{y - y_0}{m} = \frac{z - z_0}{n}.$$

Ma'nosi: $\overrightarrow{M_0M} \parallel \mathbf s$. Maxraj 0 bo'lsa, surat ham 0 (masalan, $l = 0 \Rightarrow x = x_0$).

---

## Parametrik tenglama

$$x = x_0 + lt,\quad y = y_0 + mt,\quad z = z_0 + nt,\qquad t \in \mathbb R.$$

Fizikada: $t$ — vaqt, $\mathbf s$ — tezlik; nuqta to'g'ri chiziq bo'ylab tekis harakatlanadi.

---

## Ikki nuqta orqali

$$\frac{x - x_1}{x_2 - x_1} = \frac{y - y_1}{y_2 - y_1} = \frac{z - z_1}{z_2 - z_1}.$$

:::misol
$A(1;\ 0;\ 2)$, $B(3;\ 1;\ 5)$: $\mathbf s = (2;\ 1;\ 3)$,
$$\frac{x - 1}{2} = \frac y1 = \frac{z - 2}{3}.$$
:::

---

## Mashq: yo'naltiruvchi vektor

:::savol
$\frac{x - 1}{2} = \frac{y + 1}{-1} = \frac z3$ ning yo'naltiruvchi vektori?
- $(1;\ -1;\ 0)$
+ $(2;\ -1;\ 3)$
- $(-1;\ 1;\ 0)$
- $(2;\ 1;\ 3)$
:::

---

## Umumiy tenglama

Ikki tekislik kesishmasi:
$$\begin{cases} A_1x + B_1y + C_1z + D_1 = 0\\ A_2x + B_2y + C_2z + D_2 = 0\end{cases}$$

Kanonikka o'tish:
1. $\mathbf s = \mathbf n_1 \times \mathbf n_2$ (ikkala normalga perpendikulyar);
2. $M_0$ — biror yechim (masalan $z = 0$ deb).

---

## Umumiydan kanonikka: misol

$\begin{cases} x + 2y - z = 3\\ 2x - y + z = 1\end{cases}$

$$\mathbf s = \begin{vmatrix} \mathbf i & \mathbf j & \mathbf k\\ 1 & 2 & -1\\ 2 & -1 & 1\end{vmatrix} = (1;\ -3;\ -5).$$

$z = 0$: $x + 2y = 3$, $2x - y = 1$ $\Rightarrow$ $x = y = 1$.
$$\frac{x - 1}{1} = \frac{y - 1}{-3} = \frac z{-5}.$$

---

## Ikki to'g'ri chiziq

$L_1$: $M_1$, $\mathbf s_1$; $L_2$: $M_2$, $\mathbf s_2$.

| Shart | Vaziyat |
|---|---|
| $\mathbf s_1 \parallel \mathbf s_2 \parallel \overrightarrow{M_1M_2}$ | ustma-ust |
| $\mathbf s_1 \parallel \mathbf s_2$, aks holda | parallel |
| $\overrightarrow{M_1M_2}\mathbf s_1\mathbf s_2 = 0$, $\mathbf s_1 \nparallel \mathbf s_2$ | kesishadi |
| $\overrightarrow{M_1M_2}\mathbf s_1\mathbf s_2 \ne 0$ | **ayqash** |

---

## Ayqash chiziqlar

Fazoda ikki to'g'ri chiziq **na kesishadi, na parallel** bo'lishi mumkin — **ayqash**.

Masalan: ko'chadagi ko'prik va uning tagidan o'tuvchi yo'l. Tekislikda bunday holat yo'q!

---

## Burchak

$$\cos\varphi = \frac{|\mathbf s_1\cdot\mathbf s_2|}{|\mathbf s_1||\mathbf s_2|}.$$

- Parallel: $\mathbf s_1 \parallel \mathbf s_2$;
- perpendikulyar: $\mathbf s_1\cdot\mathbf s_2 = 0$ (ayqash bo'lsa ham!).

---

## Ayqash chiziqlar masofasi

$$d = \frac{|\overrightarrow{M_1M_2}\,\mathbf s_1\,\mathbf s_2|}{|\mathbf s_1 \times \mathbf s_2|}.$$

Ma'nosi: parallelepiped hajmi ÷ asos yuzi = balandlik = umumiy perpendikulyar uzunligi.

---

## Ayqash: misol

$L_1$ — $Ox$ o'qi: $O(0;0;0)$, $\mathbf s_1 = (1;\ 0;\ 0)$.
$L_2$: $M_2(0;\ 1;\ 2)$, $\mathbf s_2 = (0;\ 1;\ 0)$.

$$\begin{vmatrix} 0 & 1 & 2\\ 1 & 0 & 0\\ 0 & 1 & 0\end{vmatrix} = 2 \ne 0\ \text{— ayqash}.$$
$|\mathbf s_1 \times \mathbf s_2| = |\mathbf k| = 1$, $d = 2$.

---

## Mashq: vaziyat

:::savol
$\frac{x}{1} = \frac{y}{2} = \frac{z}{3}$ va $\frac{x - 1}{2} = \frac{y - 2}{4} = \frac{z - 3}{6}$?
- Kesishadi
- Parallel
+ Ustma-ust
- Ayqash
> $\mathbf s_2 = 2\mathbf s_1$, $(1;2;3)$ birinchi chiziqda yotadi.
:::

---

## Chiziq va tekislik

Tekislik $\mathbf n = (A;\ B;\ C)$, chiziq $\mathbf s = (l;\ m;\ n)$:

:::xossa
- **Parallel** (yoki tekislikda): $Al + Bm + Cn = 0$;
- **perpendikulyar:** $\frac Al = \frac Bm = \frac Cn$;
- burchak: $\sin\varphi = \dfrac{|Al + Bm + Cn|}{|\mathbf n||\mathbf s|}$.
:::

---

## Nega sinus?

$\mathbf n$ tekislikka perpendikulyar. Chiziq va $\mathbf n$ orasidagi burchak $\psi$ bo'lsa, chiziq va tekislik orasidagi burchak $\varphi = 90^\circ - \psi$:
$$\sin\varphi = \cos\psi = \frac{|\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf s|}{|\mathbf n||\mathbf s|}.$$

---

## Mashq: chiziq va tekislik

:::savol
$\frac x1 = \frac y2 = \frac z{-1}$ va $x + y + 3z - 5 = 0$?
+ Parallel
- Perpendikulyar
- Chiziq tekislikda yotadi
- $45^\circ$ ostida kesishadi
> $1 + 2 - 3 = 0$; $O$ tekislikda emas.
:::

---

## Kesishish nuqtasi

Parametrik tenglamani tekislikka qo'yamiz → $t$ → nuqta.

:::misol
$x = 1 + t$, $y = 2t$, $z = -1 + t$ va $x + y + z - 6 = 0$:
$$(1 + t) + 2t + (-1 + t) - 6 = 4t - 6 = 0,\ t = \tfrac32.$$
Nuqta $\left(\frac52;\ 3;\ \frac12\right)$.
:::

---

## Kesishish: generator misoli

$\frac{x + 2}{-1} = \frac{y - 3}{-3} = \frac z2$ va $-3x - y + 3z - 15 = 0$:

$x = -2 - t$, $y = 3 - 3t$, $z = 2t$: $6 + 3t - 3 + 3t + 6t - 15 = 12t - 12 = 0$, $t = 1$.

Nuqta $(-3;\ 0;\ 2)$. Mashqlar bo'limidagi masalalar shunday.

---

## Nuqtaning tekislikdagi proyeksiyasi

$M(4;\ 3;\ 2)$, tekislik $x + y + z - 3 = 0$:
1. Perpendikulyar: $\mathbf s = \mathbf n$: $x = 4 + t$, $y = 3 + t$, $z = 2 + t$;
2. $9 + 3t - 3 = 0 \Rightarrow t = -2$;
3. Proyeksiya $K(2;\ 1;\ 0)$.

$|MK| = 2\sqrt3$ — masofa formulasi bilan mos ✓

---

## Simmetrik nuqta

$K$ — $MM'$ ning o'rtasi:
$$M' = 2K - M = (0;\ -1;\ -2).$$

Ko'zgudagi aks — aynan shunday hisoblanadi.

---

## Nuqtaning chiziqdagi proyeksiyasi

$M$ dan $L$ ga perpendikulyar: $M$ dan o'tuvchi, $\mathbf s$ ga perpendikulyar tekislik bilan $L$ ning kesishishi.

Masofa: $d = \dfrac{|\overrightarrow{M_0M} \times \mathbf s|}{|\mathbf s|}$ (3-mavzu).

---

## Tatbiq: ray tracing

Kamera $C$ dan piksel yo'nalishida nur: $\mathbf r(t) = C + t\mathbf d$.

Sahna obyektlari bilan kesishish — $t$ ni topish; eng kichik musbat $t$ — ko'rinadigan obyekt. Har kadrda millionlab nur!

---

## Tatbiq: aviatsiya xavfsizligi

Ikki samolyot to'g'ri chiziqlar bo'ylab uchmoqda:
$$\mathbf r_1(t) = A + t\mathbf v_1,\qquad \mathbf r_2(t) = B + t\mathbf v_2.$$

Ayqash chiziqlar masofasi — trayektoriyalar orasidagi minimal masofa. Dispetcherlar tizimi uni doimiy kuzatadi.

## Tipik xatolar

- Kanonik tenglamada $\frac{y + 1}{-1}$ dan $y_0 = 1$ deb olish (to'g'risi $-1$).
- Kesishmada $\mathbf s = \mathbf n_1 + \mathbf n_2$ deb olish ($\times$ kerak).
- Chiziq-tekislik burchagida $\cos$ ishlatish.
- Ayqash va parallel chiziqlarni farqlamaslik.

---

## Xulosa

- Kanonik, parametrik, ikki nuqta, umumiy tenglamalar; $\mathbf s = \mathbf n_1 \times \mathbf n_2$.
- Ikki chiziq: ustma-ust, parallel, kesishadi, ayqash (aralash ko'paytma).
- $d = \frac{|\overrightarrow{M_1M_2}\mathbf s_1\mathbf s_2|}{|\mathbf s_1\times\mathbf s_2|}$.
- Chiziq va tekislik: $\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf s = 0$ — parallel; $\sin\varphi$ formulasi.
