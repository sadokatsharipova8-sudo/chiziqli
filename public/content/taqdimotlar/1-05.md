## Sonlardagi o'xshashlik

Sonlarda: $ax = b$, $a \ne 0 \Rightarrow x = a^{-1}b$, bunda $a\cdot a^{-1} = 1$.

Matritsalarda: $AX = B$. Agar $A^{-1}$ — «teskari» matritsa topilsa,
$$X = A^{-1}B.$$

Savol: qachon $A^{-1}$ mavjud va uni qanday topish mumkin?

---

## Ta'rif

:::tarif
$A$ kvadrat matritsa uchun
$$AA^{-1} = A^{-1}A = E$$
shartni qanoatlantiruvchi $A^{-1}$ — **teskari matritsa**.
:::

- Teskari matritsa faqat **kvadrat** matritsa uchun aniqlanadi.
- Agar mavjud bo'lsa — **yagona**.

---

## Xos va xosmas matritsalar

:::tarif
- $\det A \ne 0$ — **xosmas** (aynimagan) matritsa
- $\det A = 0$ — **xos** (aynigan) matritsa
:::

:::teorema
$A^{-1}$ mavjud $\iff$ $\det A \ne 0$.
:::

Zaruriylik: $\det A\cdot\det A^{-1} = \det E = 1$, demak $\det A \ne 0$.

---

## Teskari matritsa formulasi

:::teorema
$$A^{-1} = \frac{1}{\det A}\begin{pmatrix} A_{11} & A_{21} & \cdots & A_{n1}\\ A_{12} & A_{22} & \cdots & A_{n2}\\ \vdots & & & \vdots\\ A_{1n} & A_{2n} & \cdots & A_{nn}\end{pmatrix}.$$
:::

$A_{ij}$ — algebraik to'ldiruvchilar, **transponirlangan** holda joylashadi (biriktirilgan matritsa).

---

## Formula isboti

$A\cdot\widetilde A^{\,T}$ ko'paytmaning $(i, k)$ elementi:
$$\sum_j a_{ij}A_{kj} = \begin{cases} \det A, & i = k\\ 0, & i \ne k\end{cases}$$
(4-mavzu: begona to'ldiruvchilar teoremasi).

Demak $A\widetilde A^{\,T} = \det A\cdot E$, ya'ni $A\cdot\dfrac{\widetilde A^{\,T}}{\det A} = E$. ∎

---

## 2×2 uchun qisqa qoida

$$\begin{pmatrix} a & b\\ c & d\end{pmatrix}^{-1} = \frac{1}{ad - bc}\begin{pmatrix} d & -b\\ -c & a\end{pmatrix}.$$

**Diagonal elementlar o'rin almashadi, qolganlari ishorasini o'zgartiradi.**

:::misol
$\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1\\ 5 & 3\end{pmatrix}^{-1} = \dfrac{1}{1}\begin{pmatrix} 3 & -1\\ -5 & 2\end{pmatrix}$. Tekshirish: $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1\\ 5 & 3\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} 3 & -1\\ -5 & 2\end{pmatrix} = E$ ✓
:::

---

## Mashq: 2×2

:::savol
$\begin{pmatrix} 4 & 2\\ 1 & 1\end{pmatrix}^{-1}$ = ?
+ $\dfrac12\begin{pmatrix} 1 & -2\\ -1 & 4\end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & -2\\ -1 & 4\end{pmatrix}$
- $\dfrac12\begin{pmatrix} 4 & -2\\ -1 & 1\end{pmatrix}$
- mavjud emas
> $\det = 4 - 2 = 2$.
:::

---

## 3×3 misol: determinant

$$A = \begin{pmatrix} -1 & 2 & -3\\ 0 & 1 & 2\\ 2 & 4 & 5\end{pmatrix}$$

1-satr bo'yicha:
$$\det A = -1(5 - 8) - 2(0 - 4) - 3(0 - 2) = 3 + 8 + 6 = 17 \ne 0.$$

Teskari matritsa mavjud.

---

## 3×3 misol: algebraik to'ldiruvchilar

| | $j = 1$ | $j = 2$ | $j = 3$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| $A_{1j}$ | $-3$ | $4$ | $-2$ |
| $A_{2j}$ | $-22$ | $1$ | $8$ |
| $A_{3j}$ | $7$ | $2$ | $-1$ |

Masalan, $A_{21} = -\begin{vmatrix} 2 & -3\\ 4 & 5\end{vmatrix} = -(10 + 12) = -22$.

---

## 3×3 misol: natija

To'ldiruvchilarni **ustun qilib** yozamiz:
$$A^{-1} = \frac{1}{17}\begin{pmatrix} -3 & -22 & 7\\ 4 & 1 & 2\\ -2 & 8 & -1\end{pmatrix}.$$

Tekshirish ($AA^{-1}$ ning $(1,1)$ elementi):
$$\tfrac{1}{17}\big((-1)(-3) + 2\cdot 4 + (-3)(-2)\big) = \tfrac{17}{17} = 1\ ✓$$

---

## Diqqat: eng ko'p xato

:::diqqat
$A_{ij}$ ni $i$-satrga emas, **$j$-satr, $i$-ustunga** yozing (transponirlash!).

Har doim hech bo'lmaganda bitta elementni $AA^{-1} = E$ orqali tekshiring.
:::

---

## Teskari matritsa xossalari

:::xossa
- $(A^{-1})^{-1} = A$
- $(AB)^{-1} = B^{-1}A^{-1}$ — tartib teskari!
- $(A^T)^{-1} = (A^{-1})^T$
- $(\lambda A)^{-1} = \lambda^{-1}A^{-1}$
- $\det A^{-1} = \dfrac{1}{\det A}$
:::

---

## Gauss–Jordan usuli

$n$ katta bo'lsa, $n^2$ ta to'ldiruvchi hisoblash og'ir. Yaxshiroq yo'l:

$$(A\,|\,E) \xrightarrow{\text{faqat satrlar ustida elementar almashtirishlar}} (E\,|\,A^{-1}).$$

Chap qism birlik matritsaga aylanganda, o'ng qismda $A^{-1}$ hosil bo'ladi.

---

## Gauss–Jordan: misol

$$\left(\begin{array}{cc|cc} 1 & 2 & 1 & 0\\ 3 & 7 & 0 & 1\end{array}\right) \xrightarrow{S_2 - 3S_1} \left(\begin{array}{cc|cc} 1 & 2 & 1 & 0\\ 0 & 1 & -3 & 1\end{array}\right)$$
$$\xrightarrow{S_1 - 2S_2} \left(\begin{array}{cc|cc} 1 & 0 & 7 & -2\\ 0 & 1 & -3 & 1\end{array}\right).$$

$A^{-1} = \begin{pmatrix} 7 & -2\\ -3 & 1\end{pmatrix}$.

---

## Matritsaviy usul

$n$ noma'lumli $n$ ta tenglama: $AX = B$.

:::teorema
$\det A \ne 0$ bo'lsa, sistema **yagona** yechimga ega:
$$X = A^{-1}B.$$
:::

Isbot: chapdan $A^{-1}$ ga ko'paytiramiz: $A^{-1}AX = A^{-1}B \Rightarrow X = A^{-1}B$.

---

## Matritsaviy usul: misol

$$\begin{cases} -x_1 + 2x_2 - 3x_3 = -2\\ x_2 + 2x_3 = 3\\ 2x_1 + 4x_2 + 5x_3 = 11\end{cases}$$

$A$ — yuqoridagi matritsa, $A^{-1}$ ni bilamiz:
$$X = \frac{1}{17}\begin{pmatrix} -3 & -22 & 7\\ 4 & 1 & 2\\ -2 & 8 & -1\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} -2\\ 3\\ 11\end{pmatrix} = \frac{1}{17}\begin{pmatrix} 17\\ 17\\ 17\end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 1\\ 1\\ 1\end{pmatrix}.$$

---

## Kramer qoidasi

:::teorema Kramer formulalari
$\Delta = \det A \ne 0$ bo'lsa:
$$x_j = \frac{\Delta_j}{\Delta},\qquad j = 1, \dots, n,$$
$\Delta_j$ — $A$ ning $j$-ustunini ozod hadlar ustuni bilan almashtirib hosil qilingan determinant.
:::

Isbot g'oyasi: $X = A^{-1}B$ ning $j$-komponentasini yozib, uni $j$-ustun bo'yicha yoyilma deb tanish.

---

## Kramer: 2×2 misol

$$\begin{cases} x + 2y = 5\\ 3x + 4y = 11\end{cases}$$

$$\Delta = \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 2\\ 3 & 4\end{vmatrix} = -2,\quad \Delta_x = \begin{vmatrix} 5 & 2\\ 11 & 4\end{vmatrix} = -2,\quad \Delta_y = \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 5\\ 3 & 11\end{vmatrix} = -4.$$

$x = 1$, $y = 2$.

---

## Kramer: 3×3 misol (1)

$$\begin{cases} 3x_1 - x_2 + 3x_3 = -1\\ 2x_1 + x_2 + x_3 = 2\\ x_1 + x_2 + 2x_3 = 0\end{cases}$$

$$\Delta = \begin{vmatrix} 3 & -1 & 3\\ 2 & 1 & 1\\ 1 & 1 & 2\end{vmatrix} = 6 - 1 + 6 - 3 + 4 - 3 = 9.$$

---

## Kramer: 3×3 misol (2)

$$\Delta_1 = \begin{vmatrix} -1 & -1 & 3\\ 2 & 1 & 1\\ 0 & 1 & 2\end{vmatrix} = 9,\quad \Delta_2 = \begin{vmatrix} 3 & -1 & 3\\ 2 & 2 & 1\\ 1 & 0 & 2\end{vmatrix} = 9,\quad \Delta_3 = \begin{vmatrix} 3 & -1 & -1\\ 2 & 1 & 2\\ 1 & 1 & 0\end{vmatrix} = -9.$$

$$x_1 = 1,\quad x_2 = 1,\quad x_3 = -1.$$

Tekshirish: $3 - 1 - 3 = -1$ ✓, $2 + 1 - 1 = 2$ ✓, $1 + 1 - 2 = 0$ ✓

---

## $\Delta = 0$ bo'lsa-chi?

:::diqqat
$\Delta = 0$ bo'lsa Kramer qoidasini qo'llab bo'lmaydi.
- Sistema yechimga ega emas, yoki
- cheksiz ko'p yechimga ega.

Bu holatlar 7–8-mavzularda (rang, Gauss usuli) o'rganiladi.
:::

## Mashq: Kramer

:::savol
$\begin{cases} 2x - y = 1\\ x + y = 5\end{cases}$ uchun $\Delta_x$ nimaga teng?
- 3
+ 6
- −6
- 9
> $\Delta_x = \begin{vmatrix} 1 & -1\\ 5 & 1\end{vmatrix} = 1 + 5 = 6$, $\Delta = 3$, $x = 2$.
:::

| Matritsaviy | bir $A$ uchun ko'p $B$ | $A^{-1}$ ni topish kerak |
| Gauss | eng tez, har doim ishlaydi | — |

## Matritsaviy tenglamalar

:::xossa
- $AX = B \Rightarrow X = A^{-1}B$
- $XA = B \Rightarrow X = BA^{-1}$
- $AXB = C \Rightarrow X = A^{-1}CB^{-1}$
:::

:::diqqat
Ko'paytma kommutativ emas: teskari matritsaga **qaysi tomondan** ko'paytirishni aniq kuzating.
:::

---

## Matritsaviy tenglama: misol

$X\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1\\ 5 & 3\end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0\\ 2 & 1\end{pmatrix}$.

$$X = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0\\ 2 & 1\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} 3 & -1\\ -5 & 2\end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 3 & -1\\ 1 & 0\end{pmatrix}.$$

Tekshirish: $\begin{pmatrix} 3 & -1\\ 1 & 0\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1\\ 5 & 3\end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0\\ 2 & 1\end{pmatrix}$ ✓

---

## Tipik xatolar

- Algebraik to'ldiruvchilarni transponirlamaslik.
- $\det A$ ga bo'lishni unutish.
- $XA = B$ da $X = A^{-1}B$ deb yozish.
- $\Delta = 0$ da Kramer qoidasini qo'llashga urinish.

---

## Xulosa

- $A^{-1}$ mavjud $\iff \det A \ne 0$.
- $A^{-1} = \dfrac{1}{\det A}\widetilde A^{\,T}$ yoki Gauss–Jordan usuli.
- $AX = B$, $\det A \ne 0 \Rightarrow X = A^{-1}B$ — yagona yechim.
- Kramer: $x_j = \Delta_j/\Delta$.
