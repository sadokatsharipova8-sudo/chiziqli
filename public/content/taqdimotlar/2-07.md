## Asosiy teorema

:::teorema
Dekart koordinatalarida har qanday to'g'ri chiziq birinchi darajali tenglama bilan beriladi:
$$Ax + By + C = 0,\qquad A^2 + B^2 \ne 0,$$
va aksincha, har qanday bunday tenglama to'g'ri chiziqni ifodalaydi.
:::

Bu — **umumiy tenglama**.

---

## Normal vektor

$\mathbf n = (A;\ B)$ — to'g'ri chiziqqa **perpendikulyar**.

Sababi: $M_1, M_2$ chiziqda bo'lsa, $A(x_2 - x_1) + B(y_2 - y_1) = 0$, ya'ni $\mathbf n \perp \overrightarrow{M_1M_2}$.

Yo'naltiruvchi vektor: $\mathbf s = (-B;\ A)$ yoki $(B;\ -A)$.

---

## Mashq: normal vektor

:::savol
$Ax + By + C = 0$ ning normal vektori?
+ $(A;\ B)$
- $(B;\ -A)$
- $(A;\ C)$
- $(-B;\ A)$
:::

---

## Umumiy tenglamaning xususiy hollari

| Shart | Tenglama | To'g'ri chiziq |
|---|---|---|
| $C = 0$ | $Ax + By = 0$ | koordinata boshidan o'tadi |
| $A = 0$ | $y = -\frac{C}{B}$ | $Ox$ ga parallel |
| $B = 0$ | $x = -\frac{C}{A}$ | $Oy$ ga parallel |
| $A = C = 0$ | $y = 0$ | $Ox$ o'qi |

---

## Burchak koeffitsiyentli tenglama

$$y = kx + b.$$

- $k = \operatorname{tg}\alpha$ — **burchak koeffitsiyenti** ($\alpha$ — $Ox$ bilan burchak)
- $b$ — $Oy$ o'qidan ajratilgan kesma

Umumiy tenglamadan ($B \ne 0$): $k = -\dfrac{A}{B}$, $b = -\dfrac{C}{B}$.

$Oy$ ga parallel to'g'ri chiziqlar bu ko'rinishda yozilmaydi!

---

## Nuqta va burchak koeffitsiyent

$M_0(x_0;\ y_0)$ dan $k$ burchak koeffitsiyent bilan o'tuvchi:
$$y - y_0 = k(x - x_0).$$

$k$ ni o'zgartirsak — $M_0$ dan o'tuvchi **to'g'ri chiziqlar dastasi**.

:::misol
$M_0(2;\ -1)$, $k = 3$: $y + 1 = 3(x - 2)$, ya'ni $y = 3x - 7$.
:::

---

## Nuqta va normal vektor

$M_0(x_0;\ y_0)$ dan o'tuvchi, $\mathbf n = (A;\ B)$ ga perpendikulyar:
$$A(x - x_0) + B(y - y_0) = 0.$$

Ma'nosi: $\overrightarrow{M_0M} \perp \mathbf n$, ya'ni $\overrightarrow{M_0M}\cdot\mathbf n = 0$.

---

## Kanonik va parametrik tenglama

$M_0(x_0;\ y_0)$ va yo'naltiruvchi vektor $\mathbf s = (l;\ m)$:

**Kanonik:**
$$\frac{x - x_0}{l} = \frac{y - y_0}{m}.$$

**Parametrik:**
$$x = x_0 + lt,\qquad y = y_0 + mt,\qquad t \in \mathbb R.$$

Fizikada: $t$ — vaqt, $\mathbf s$ — tezlik vektori.

---

## Ikki nuqta orqali

$$\frac{x - x_1}{x_2 - x_1} = \frac{y - y_1}{y_2 - y_1}.$$

:::misol
$A(1;\ 2)$, $B(3;\ 6)$: $\dfrac{x - 1}{2} = \dfrac{y - 2}{4}$ $\Rightarrow$ $4(x - 1) = 2(y - 2)$ $\Rightarrow$ $2x - y = 0$.
:::

Maxraj nol bo'lsa, surat ham nol: masalan $x_1 = x_2$ bo'lsa, $x = x_1$.

---

## Kesmalarda tenglama

$$\frac{x}{a} + \frac{y}{b} = 1,$$
$a$, $b$ — o'qlardan ajratilgan kesmalar: chiziq $(a;\ 0)$ va $(0;\ b)$ dan o'tadi.

:::misol
$3x - 4y + 12 = 0 \Rightarrow 3x - 4y = -12 \Rightarrow \dfrac{x}{-4} + \dfrac{y}{3} = 1$.
:::

Grafikni chizish uchun juda qulay.

---

## Mashq: kesmalar

:::savol
$\frac{x}{3} + \frac{y}{4} = 1$ to'g'ri chiziq $Oy$ ni qaysi nuqtada kesadi?
- $(0;\ 3)$
+ $(0;\ 4)$
- $(4;\ 0)$
- $(0;\ 1)$
:::

## Normal tenglama

$$x\cos\alpha + y\sin\alpha - p = 0,$$
- $p \ge 0$ — koordinata boshidan masofa
- $\alpha$ — boshdan chiziqqa tushirilgan perpendikulyarning $Ox$ bilan burchagi

Umumiy tenglamadan: $\mu = \pm\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{A^2 + B^2}}$ ga ko'paytiramiz, $\mu$ ishorasi $C$ ga teskari.

---

## Normal tenglama: misol

$3x - 4y + 10 = 0$. $\sqrt{9 + 16} = 5$, $C > 0$ $\Rightarrow$ $\mu = -\frac15$:
$$-\frac35x + \frac45y - 2 = 0.$$

$p = 2$ — boshdan chiziqqacha masofa; $\cos\alpha = -\frac35$, $\sin\alpha = \frac45$.

---

## Ikki to'g'ri chiziq orasidagi burchak

Burchak koeffitsiyentlar orqali:
$$\operatorname{tg}\varphi = \left|\frac{k_2 - k_1}{1 + k_1k_2}\right|.$$

Normal vektorlar orqali:
$$\cos\varphi = \frac{|A_1A_2 + B_1B_2|}{\sqrt{A_1^2 + B_1^2}\sqrt{A_2^2 + B_2^2}}.$$

---

## Burchak: misol

$y = 2x + 1$ va $y = -3x + 4$:
$$\operatorname{tg}\varphi = \left|\frac{-3 - 2}{1 + 2(-3)}\right| = \left|\frac{-5}{-5}\right| = 1 \Rightarrow \varphi = 45^\circ.$$

---

## Parallellik va perpendikulyarlik

:::xossa
| | Burchak koeffitsiyent | Umumiy tenglama |
|---|---|---|
| Parallel | $k_1 = k_2$ | $\frac{A_1}{A_2} = \frac{B_1}{B_2} \ne \frac{C_1}{C_2}$ |
| Ustma-ust | $k_1 = k_2$, $b_1 = b_2$ | $\frac{A_1}{A_2} = \frac{B_1}{B_2} = \frac{C_1}{C_2}$ |
| Perpendikulyar | $k_1k_2 = -1$ | $A_1A_2 + B_1B_2 = 0$ |
:::

---

- $3$
- $-3$
+ $-\frac13$
- $\frac13$
:::

## Kesishish nuqtasi

Ikki tenglamadan iborat sistemani yechamiz (Kramer!):
$$\begin{cases} x + y = 2\\ 2x - y = 7\end{cases} \Rightarrow \Delta = -3,\ \Delta_x = -9,\ \Delta_y = 3 \Rightarrow (3;\ -1).$$

$\Delta = 0$ — parallel yoki ustma-ust (1-semestr, 7-mavzu: rang).

---

## Nuqtadan to'g'ri chiziqqacha masofa

:::teorema
$$d = \frac{|Ax_0 + By_0 + C|}{\sqrt{A^2 + B^2}}.$$
:::

Isbot g'oyasi: $M_0$ dan chiziqdagi istalgan $M_1$ ga vektorning $\mathbf n$ ga proyeksiyasi:
$$d = \left|\text{pr}_{\mathbf n}\overrightarrow{M_1M_0}\right| = \frac{|\mathbf n\cdot\overrightarrow{M_1M_0}|}{|\mathbf n|}.$$

---

## Masofa: misollar

:::misol
- $M(1;\ 2)$ dan $3x + 4y + 4 = 0$ gacha: $d = \dfrac{|3 + 8 + 4|}{5} = 3$.
- $O(0;\ 0)$ dan $6x + 8y - 20 = 0$ gacha: $d = \dfrac{20}{10} = 2$.
:::

Parallel chiziqlar $Ax + By + C_1 = 0$, $Ax + By + C_2 = 0$ orasida:
$$d = \frac{|C_1 - C_2|}{\sqrt{A^2 + B^2}}.$$

---

## Mashq: masofa

:::savol
$O(0;\ 0)$ dan $5x - 12y + 26 = 0$ gacha masofa?
- 26
+ 2
- $\frac{26}{17}$
- 13
> $\frac{26}{13} = 2$.
:::

## Uchburchak: balandlik

$A(0;\ 0)$, $B(4;\ 2)$, $C(1;\ 3)$. $C$ dan tushirilgan balandlik asosi.

1. $AB$: yo'naltiruvchi $(4;\ 2) \parallel (2;\ 1)$, tenglama $x - 2y = 0$.
2. $CD \perp AB$, normali $(2;\ 1)$: $2(x - 1) + (y - 3) = 0$, ya'ni $2x + y - 5 = 0$.
3. Kesishish: $x = 2y$, $5y = 5$ $\Rightarrow$ $D(2;\ 1)$.

---

## Uchburchak: balandlik uzunligi va yuza

$|CD| = \sqrt{1 + 4} = \sqrt5$.

Tekshirish masofa formulasi bilan: $d = \dfrac{|1 - 2\cdot3|}{\sqrt5} = \sqrt5$ ✓

Yuza: $S = \frac12|AB|\cdot|CD| = \frac12\cdot 2\sqrt5\cdot\sqrt5 = 5$.

---

## Uchburchak: mediana va bissektrisa

- **Mediana:** uchdan qarama-qarshi tomon o'rtasiga — ikki nuqta orqali tenglama.
- **Bissektrisa:** ikki to'g'ri chiziqqa teng uzoqlikdagi nuqtalar:
$$\frac{A_1x + B_1y + C_1}{\sqrt{A_1^2 + B_1^2}} = \pm\frac{A_2x + B_2y + C_2}{\sqrt{A_2^2 + B_2^2}}.$$

---

## Tipik xatolar

- Normal va yo'naltiruvchi vektorlarni chalkashtirish.
- Masofa formulasida modulni unutish.
- $Oy$ ga parallel chiziqni $y = kx + b$ ko'rinishda yozishga urinish.
- Perpendikulyarlikni $k_1k_2 = 1$ deb yozish.

---

## Xulosa

- $Ax + By + C = 0$, $\mathbf n = (A;\ B)$.
- 8 ta tenglama turi — berilganlarga qarab tanlanadi.
- Parallel: $k_1 = k_2$; perpendikulyar: $k_1k_2 = -1$.
- $d = \dfrac{|Ax_0 + By_0 + C|}{\sqrt{A^2 + B^2}}$.
