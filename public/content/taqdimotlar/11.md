## Chiziqli operator

:::tarif
$\mathcal A: V \to V$ akslantirish
$$\mathcal A(\alpha\mathbf x + \beta\mathbf y) = \alpha\mathcal A\mathbf x + \beta\mathcal A\mathbf y$$
shartni qanoatlantirsa — **chiziqli operator** (chiziqli almashtirish).
:::

Ma'nosi: chiziqli kombinatsiyani saqlaydi. Geometrik: to'g'ri chiziqlarni to'g'ri chiziqqa, koordinata boshini o'ziga o'tkazadi.

---

## Misollar

- Tekislikni $\varphi$ burchakka **burish**
- O'qqa **proyeksiyalash**
- O'qqa nisbatan **simmetriya**
- $k$ marta **cho'zish**: $\mathcal A\mathbf x = k\mathbf x$
- **Differensiallash** $D: P_n \to P_n$, $Dp = p'$
- Nol operator $\mathcal O$ va ayniy operator $\mathcal E$

Chiziqli emas: siljitish $\mathbf x \mapsto \mathbf x + \mathbf a$ ($\mathbf a \ne \mathbf 0$).

---

## Operator matritsasi

:::tarif
$\mathcal A\mathbf e_j = \sum_i a_{ij}\mathbf e_i$. $j$-ustuni $\mathcal A\mathbf e_j$ ning koordinatalaridan iborat $A = (a_{ij})$ — operator **matritsasi**.
:::

:::teorema
$\mathbf y = \mathcal A\mathbf x$ bo'lsa, koordinatalarda: $Y = AX$.
:::

Qoidani eslab qoling: **bazis vektorlari obrazlari — ustunlarga**.

---

## Matritsa: burish

$\mathcal A\mathbf i = (\cos\varphi, \sin\varphi)$, $\ \mathcal A\mathbf j = (-\sin\varphi, \cos\varphi)$:
$$A = \begin{pmatrix} \cos\varphi & -\sin\varphi\\ \sin\varphi & \cos\varphi\end{pmatrix}.$$

$90^\circ$ ga burish: $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & -1\\ 1 & 0\end{pmatrix}$; $(1, 0) \mapsto (0, 1)$.

---

## Matritsa: differensiallash

$P_2$, bazis $1, t, t^2$:
$$D1 = 0,\quad Dt = 1,\quad Dt^2 = 2t.$$
$$D = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 & 0\\ 0 & 0 & 2\\ 0 & 0 & 0\end{pmatrix}.$$

Tekshirish: $p = 3 - 2t + 5t^2 \to (3, -2, 5)^T$; $DX = (-2, 10, 0)^T \to p' = -2 + 10t$ ✓

---

## Mashq: matritsa

:::savol
$\begin{pmatrix} 0 & -1\\ 1 & 0\end{pmatrix}$ qanday almashtirish?
- O'qqa nisbatan simmetriya
+ $90^\circ$ ga burish
- Siljitish
- Proyeksiya
:::

## Operatorlar ustida amallar

- Yig'indi: $(\mathcal A + \mathcal B)\mathbf x = \mathcal A\mathbf x + \mathcal B\mathbf x$ $\leftrightarrow$ $A + B$
- Songa ko'paytirish $\leftrightarrow$ $\lambda A$
- Ko'paytma (ketma-ket bajarish): $(\mathcal A\mathcal B)\mathbf x = \mathcal A(\mathcal B\mathbf x)$ $\leftrightarrow$ $AB$
- Teskari operator $\leftrightarrow$ $A^{-1}$ (mavjud $\iff \det A \ne 0$)

Matritsalar ko'paytmasining ma'nosi — **almashtirishlarni ketma-ket bajarish**!

---

## Bazis almashganda

:::teorema
$T$ — o'tish matritsasi bo'lsa, yangi bazisda:
$$A' = T^{-1}AT.$$
:::

$A$ va $T^{-1}AT$ — **o'xshash** matritsalar: bitta operator, turli bazislar.

O'xshash matritsalarda bir xil: $\det$, $\operatorname{tr}$, rang, xarakteristik ko'phad — operatorning **invariantlari**.

---

## Yadro va obraz

:::tarif
- $\ker\mathcal A = \{\mathbf x : \mathcal A\mathbf x = \mathbf 0\}$ — **yadro**
- $\operatorname{Im}\mathcal A = \{\mathcal A\mathbf x : \mathbf x \in V\}$ — **obraz**
:::

Ikkalasi ham qism fazo. $\dim\operatorname{Im}$ — **rang**, $\dim\ker$ — **defekt**.

:::teorema
$$\operatorname{rang}\mathcal A + \operatorname{def}\mathcal A = \dim V,\qquad \operatorname{rang}\mathcal A = r(A).$$
:::

---

## Yadro va obraz: misol

$\mathcal A\mathbf x = (x_1 + x_2,\ x_2 + x_3,\ x_1 - x_3)$:
$$A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & 0\\ 0 & 1 & 1\\ 1 & 0 & -1\end{pmatrix},\quad \det A = 0,\quad r(A) = 2.$$

Yadro: $x_1 = -x_2$, $x_3 = -x_2$ $\Rightarrow$ $\ker\mathcal A = L\big((1, -1, 1)\big)$.

Rang 2 + defekt 1 = 3 ✓

---

## Xos son va xos vektor

:::tarif
$$\mathcal A\mathbf x = \lambda\mathbf x,\qquad \mathbf x \ne \mathbf 0.$$
$\lambda$ — **xos son**, $\mathbf x$ — unga mos **xos vektor**.
:::

Geometrik ma'no: operator xos vektorni **burmaydi**, faqat $\lambda$ marta cho'zadi (yoki $\lambda < 0$ da teskari tomonga o'giradi).

---

## Geometrik misollar

- $Ox$ ga proyeksiya: $\mathbf i$ — xos ($\lambda = 1$), $\mathbf j$ — xos ($\lambda = 0$).
- $Ox$ ga nisbatan simmetriya: $\mathbf i$ ($\lambda = 1$), $\mathbf j$ ($\lambda = -1$).
- $k$ marta cho'zish: har bir vektor xos, $\lambda = k$.
- $90^\circ$ ga burish: **haqiqiy** xos vektor yo'q! ($\lambda = \pm i$)

---

## Xarakteristik tenglama

$\mathcal A\mathbf x = \lambda\mathbf x \iff (A - \lambda E)X = O$ — bir jinsli sistema.

Notrivial yechim mavjud $\iff$
$$\det(A - \lambda E) = 0.$$

:::tarif
$\det(A - \lambda E)$ — **xarakteristik ko'phad** ($n$-darajali), tenglama — **xarakteristik tenglama**.
:::

---

## Vieta munosabatlari

:::xossa
$$\lambda_1 + \lambda_2 + \dots + \lambda_n = \operatorname{tr}A,\qquad \lambda_1\lambda_2\cdots\lambda_n = \det A.$$
:::

$2\times 2$ uchun: $\lambda^2 - (\operatorname{tr}A)\lambda + \det A = 0$.

Natija: $\det A = 0 \iff \lambda = 0$ xos son.

---

## Algoritm

1. $\det(A - \lambda E) = 0$ ni yechib, $\lambda_1, \lambda_2, \dots$ ni topish.
2. Har bir $\lambda_i$ uchun $(A - \lambda_iE)X = O$ sistemaning **FYS**ini topish.
3. FYS vektorlari va ularning chiziqli kombinatsiyalari (noldan tashqari) — xos vektorlar.

---

## Misol: xos sonlar

$$A = \begin{pmatrix} 4 & 1\\ 2 & 3\end{pmatrix}$$

$$\begin{vmatrix} 4 - \lambda & 1\\ 2 & 3 - \lambda\end{vmatrix} = \lambda^2 - 7\lambda + 10 = 0.$$

$\lambda_1 = 2$, $\lambda_2 = 5$. Tekshirish: $2 + 5 = 7 = \operatorname{tr}A$ ✓, $2\cdot 5 = 10 = \det A$ ✓

---

## Misol: xos vektorlar

$\lambda_1 = 2$: $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1\\ 2 & 1\end{pmatrix}X = O \Rightarrow 2x_1 + x_2 = 0 \Rightarrow X_1 = c(1;\ -2)$.

$\lambda_2 = 5$: $\begin{pmatrix} -1 & 1\\ 2 & -2\end{pmatrix}X = O \Rightarrow x_1 = x_2 \Rightarrow X_2 = c(1;\ 1)$.

Tekshirish: $A(1, 1)^T = (5, 5)^T = 5(1, 1)^T$ ✓

---

## Kompleks xos sonlar

Xarakteristik ko'phad $\lambda^3 - 5\lambda^2 + 17\lambda - 13$:
- $\lambda = 1$ ildiz (koeffitsiyentlar yig'indisi $1 - 5 + 17 - 13 = 0$).
- $\lambda - 1$ ga bo'lamiz: $\lambda^2 - 4\lambda + 13 = 0$.
- $\lambda_{2,3} = 2 \pm 3i$.

Haqiqiy matritsaning kompleks xos sonlari qo'shma juft bo'lib keladi (1-mavzu).

## Diagonallashtirish

:::teorema
Har xil xos sonlarga mos xos vektorlar chiziqli erkli.
:::

Agar xos vektorlardan bazis tuzish mumkin bo'lsa, shu bazisda:
$$T^{-1}AT = D = \operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1, \dots, \lambda_n),$$
$T$ ning ustunlari — xos vektorlar.

$n$ ta **har xil** xos son — yetarli shart.

---

## Diagonallashtirish: misol

$A = \begin{pmatrix} 4 & 1\\ 2 & 3\end{pmatrix}$, $\ T = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1\\ -2 & 1\end{pmatrix}$:
$$T^{-1}AT = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 0\\ 0 & 5\end{pmatrix}.$$

Foydasi: $A^k = TD^kT^{-1}$, $D^k = \operatorname{diag}(2^k, 5^k)$ — yuqori darajalarni hisoblash oson.

---

## Diagonallashmaydigan matritsa

$$A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1\\ 0 & 1\end{pmatrix}$$

$\lambda = 1$ (ikki karrali). $(A - E)X = O \Rightarrow x_2 = 0$ — faqat bitta xos yo'nalish $(1, 0)$.

Xos vektorlardan bazis tuzib bo'lmaydi — matritsa **diagonallashmaydi** (siljish almashtirishi).

---

## Invariant qism fazolar

:::tarif
$\mathcal A L \subset L$ bo'lsa, $L$ — **invariant qism fazo**.
:::

- Har bir xos vektor 1-o'lchovli invariant qism fazo hosil qiladi.
- $\ker\mathcal A$ va $\operatorname{Im}\mathcal A$ — invariant.
- Fazoni invariant qism fazolarga ajratish — operatorni «sodda bo'laklarga» bo'lish.

---

## Qo'shma va simmetrik operatorlar

:::tarif
$(\mathcal A\mathbf x, \mathbf y) = (\mathbf x, \mathcal A^*\mathbf y)$ — **qo'shma** operator; ortonormal bazisda $A^* = A^T$.
- $\mathcal A^* = \mathcal A$ — **o'z-o'ziga qo'shma** (simmetrik)
- $\mathcal A^*\mathcal A = \mathcal E$ — **ortogonal** (kompleksda **unitar**)
- $\mathcal A^*\mathcal A = \mathcal A\mathcal A^*$ — **normal**
:::

---

## Simmetrik operator haqida teorema

:::teorema
O'z-o'ziga qo'shma operator uchun:
1. barcha xos sonlar **haqiqiy**;
2. har xil xos sonlarga mos xos vektorlar **ortogonal**;
3. xos vektorlardan **ortonormal bazis** tuzish mumkin.
:::

Ya'ni har qanday simmetrik matritsa **ortogonal** almashtirish bilan diagonallashadi: $Q^TAQ = D$. Bu 10-mavzudagi ortogonal usulning asosi.

---

## Ortogonal operatorlar

- Uzunlik va burchaklarni saqlaydi: $|\mathcal A\mathbf x| = |\mathbf x|$.
- Matritsasi: $Q^TQ = E$, ya'ni $Q^{-1} = Q^T$.
- $\det Q = \pm 1$: $+1$ — burish, $-1$ — burish + simmetriya.

:::savol
Ortogonal matritsa determinanti qanday qiymatlar qabul qiladi?
- Faqat 1
+ $1$ yoki $-1$
- Istalgan musbat son
- 0
:::

## Tipik xatolar

- Xos vektorni nol vektor deb olish (ta'rif bo'yicha $\mathbf x \ne \mathbf 0$).
- $\det(A - \lambda E)$ da diagonaldan tashqari elementlardan $\lambda$ ayirish.
- Operator matritsasida obrazlarni satr qilib yozish.
- Har qanday matritsa diagonallashadi deb o'ylash.

---

## Xulosa

- Operator matritsasi: ustunlar — bazis vektorlari obrazlari.
- $A' = T^{-1}AT$; invariantlar: $\det$, $\operatorname{tr}$, xarakteristik ko'phad.
- Rang + defekt = $\dim V$.
- $\det(A - \lambda E) = 0$ — xos sonlar; FYS — xos vektorlar.
- Simmetrik matritsa ortogonal diagonallashadi, xos sonlari haqiqiy.
