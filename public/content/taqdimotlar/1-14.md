## Maqsad

$f = 5x^2 + 4xy + 8y^2$ — qanday shakl? $xy$ had tufayli tushunish qiyin.

Agar o'zgaruvchilarni almashtirib $f = 4x'^2 + 9y'^2$ ko'rinishga keltirsak — hammasi aniq: ikkala yo'nalishda ham musbat, «kosa» shakli.

Bu — **kanonik ko'rinish**.

---

## Kanonik ko'rinish

:::tarif
$$f = \lambda_1y_1^2 + \lambda_2y_2^2 + \dots + \lambda_ny_n^2$$
— **kanonik ko'rinish**. Koeffitsiyentlar $+1, -1, 0$ — **normal ko'rinish**.
:::

Matritsa tilida: shunday xosmas $T$ topish kerakki, $T^TAT$ diagonal bo'lsin.

---

## Lagranj teoremasi

:::teorema
Har qanday kvadratik formani **xosmas** chiziqli almashtirish bilan kanonik ko'rinishga keltirish mumkin.
:::

**Lagranj usuli** — to'liq kvadratlarni ketma-ket ajratish:
1. $x_1^2$ qatnashgan barcha hadlarni to'liq kvadratga yig'amiz.
2. Qolgan forma $x_1$ siz — xuddi shunday davom etamiz.

---

## Lagranj: oddiy misol

$f = x^2 + 4xy + 3y^2$:
$$x^2 + 4xy = (x + 2y)^2 - 4y^2 \Rightarrow f = (x + 2y)^2 - y^2.$$

$y_1 = x + 2y$, $y_2 = y$: $\ f = y_1^2 - y_2^2$.

Almashtirish xosmas: $\begin{vmatrix} 1 & 2\\ 0 & 1\end{vmatrix} = 1 \ne 0$ ✓

---

## Lagranj: uch o'zgaruvchi

$f = x_1^2 + 2x_1x_2 + 2x_2^2 + 4x_2x_3 + 5x_3^2$:
$$f = (x_1 + x_2)^2 + x_2^2 + 4x_2x_3 + 5x_3^2 = (x_1 + x_2)^2 + (x_2 + 2x_3)^2 + x_3^2.$$

$f = y_1^2 + y_2^2 + y_3^2$ — musbat aniqlangan.

---

## Kvadratlar yo'q bo'lsa

Formada birorta kvadrat yo'q (masalan $f = 2x_1x_2 + \dots$):
$$x_1 = t_1 + t_2,\qquad x_2 = t_1 - t_2\ \Rightarrow\ x_1x_2 = t_1^2 - t_2^2.$$

Kvadratlar paydo bo'ladi, keyin odatdagi Lagranj usuli.

---

## Murakkab misol (1)

$f = 2x_1x_2 - 6x_2x_3 + 2x_3x_1$. $x_1 = t_1 + t_2$, $x_2 = t_1 - t_2$, $x_3 = t_3$:
$$f = 2t_1^2 - 2t_2^2 - 4t_1t_3 + 8t_2t_3.$$

---

## Murakkab misol (2)

$$2t_1^2 - 4t_1t_3 = 2(t_1 - t_3)^2 - 2t_3^2,$$
$$-2t_2^2 + 8t_2t_3 = -2(t_2 - 2t_3)^2 + 8t_3^2.$$

$$f = 2(t_1 - t_3)^2 - 2(t_2 - 2t_3)^2 + 6t_3^2 = 2y_1^2 - 2y_2^2 + 6y_3^2.$$

---

## Ortogonal almashtirish usuli

:::teorema
Har qanday haqiqiy kvadratik formani **ortogonal** almashtirish bilan
$$f = \lambda_1y_1^2 + \dots + \lambda_ny_n^2$$
ko'rinishga keltirish mumkin; $\lambda_i$ — $A$ ning xos sonlari.
:::

Yangi o'qlar — ortonormal xos vektorlar (18-mavzuda isbot). Afzalligi: uzunlik va burchaklar saqlanadi.

---

## Ortogonal usul: misol

$f = 5x^2 + 4xy + 8y^2$, $A = \begin{pmatrix} 5 & 2\\ 2 & 8\end{pmatrix}$:
$$\lambda^2 - 13\lambda + 36 = 0 \Rightarrow \lambda_1 = 4,\ \lambda_2 = 9.$$

$f = 4x'^2 + 9y'^2$. O'qlar: $\frac{1}{\sqrt5}(2, -1)$ va $\frac{1}{\sqrt5}(1, 2)$.

Tekshirish: $4 + 9 = 13 = \operatorname{tr}A$, $4\cdot 9 = 36 = \det A$ ✓

---

## Mashq: ortogonal usul

:::savol
$f = 3x^2 + 3y^2 + 2xy$ ning ortogonal kanonik ko'rinishidagi koeffitsiyentlar?
+ $2$ va $4$
- $3$ va $3$
- $1$ va $5$
- $3$ va $1$
> $\lambda^2 - 6\lambda + 8 = 0$.
:::

---

## Kanonik ko'rinish yagonami?

Yo'q! Turli almashtirishlar turli koeffitsiyentlar beradi:
- Lagranj: $2y_1^2 - 2y_2^2 + 6y_3^2$;
- boshqa almashtirish: $y_1^2 - 5y_2^2 + 3y_3^2$ va h.k.

Lekin **ishoralar soni** o'zgarmaydi…

---

## Inersiya qonuni

:::teorema Silvestr inersiya qonuni
Kanonik ko'rinishda:
- musbat koeffitsiyentlar soni $p$ (**musbat inersiya indeksi**),
- manfiylar soni $q$ (**manfiy inersiya indeksi**)

almashtirish usulidan qat'i nazar bir xil. $p + q = r$ — forma rangi.
:::

Yuqoridagi misolda $p = 2$, $q = 1$, $r = 3$.

---

## Mashq: rang

:::savol
Kanonik ko'rinishi $3y_1^2 - y_2^2 + 0\cdot y_3^2$ bo'lgan forma rangi?
- 3
+ 2
- 1
- 0
:::

---

## Aniqlanganlik

:::tarif
- $\mathbf x \ne \mathbf 0$ da $f(\mathbf x) > 0$ — **musbat aniqlangan**
- $f(\mathbf x) < 0$ — **manfiy aniqlangan**
- har ikki ishorali — **aniqlanmagan**
:::

Kanonik ko'rinishda: musbat aniqlangan $\iff$ barcha $\lambda_i > 0$ ($p = n$).

---

## Burchak minorlari

$$\Delta_1 = a_{11},\quad \Delta_2 = \begin{vmatrix} a_{11} & a_{12}\\ a_{21} & a_{22}\end{vmatrix},\quad \dots,\quad \Delta_n = \det A.$$

Chap yuqori burchakdan boshlanuvchi ketma-ket minorlar.

---

## Silvestr mezoni

:::teorema
1. Musbat aniqlangan $\iff \Delta_1 > 0, \Delta_2 > 0, \dots, \Delta_n > 0$.
2. Manfiy aniqlangan $\iff \Delta_1 < 0, \Delta_2 > 0, \Delta_3 < 0, \dots$ (navbatlashadi).
:::

Kanonik ko'rinishga keltirmasdan — faqat determinantlar bilan!

---

## Silvestr: misol

$f = 2x_1^2 + 2x_2^2 + 2x_3^2 + 2x_1x_2 + 2x_2x_3$:
$$A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 & 0\\ 1 & 2 & 1\\ 0 & 1 & 2\end{pmatrix}.$$

$\Delta_1 = 2$, $\Delta_2 = 3$, $\Delta_3 = 4$ — hammasi musbat — **musbat aniqlangan**.

---

## Mashq: Silvestr

:::savol
$f = -x^2 + 2xy - 3y^2$?
- Musbat aniqlangan
+ Manfiy aniqlangan
- Aniqlanmagan
- Aynigan
> $\Delta_1 = -1$, $\Delta_2 = 2$.
:::

---

## Parametrli masala

$x^2 + 2\lambda xy + 4y^2$ qachon musbat aniqlangan?

$\Delta_1 = 1 > 0$, $\Delta_2 = 4 - \lambda^2 > 0 \iff |\lambda| < 2$.

Mashqlar bo'limidagi «Silvestr mezoni» masalalarida bosh minorlarni hisoblaysiz.

---

## Yakobi usuli

Barcha $\Delta_k \ne 0$ bo'lsa ($\Delta_0 = 1$):
$$f = \frac{\Delta_1}{\Delta_0}y_1^2 + \frac{\Delta_2}{\Delta_1}y_2^2 + \dots + \frac{\Delta_n}{\Delta_{n-1}}y_n^2.$$

Yuqoridagi misolda: $f = 2y_1^2 + \frac32y_2^2 + \frac43y_3^2$ — hammasi musbat ✓

Silvestr mezoni shu formuladan kelib chiqadi.

---

## Ekstremumga tatbiq

$f(x, y)$ ning statsionar nuqtasida Gesse matritsasi $H = \begin{pmatrix} f_{xx} & f_{xy}\\ f_{xy} & f_{yy}\end{pmatrix}$:
- musbat aniqlangan — **minimum**;
- manfiy aniqlangan — **maksimum**;
- aniqlanmagan — **egar** (ekstremum yo'q).

:::misol
$f = x^2 + xy + y^2$: $H = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1\\ 1 & 2\end{pmatrix}$, $\Delta_1 = 2$, $\Delta_2 = 3$ — $(0, 0)$ minimum.
:::

---

## Tatbiq: mashinali o'rganish

Neyron tarmoqni o'qitish — xato funksiyasini minimallashtirish. Minimum yaqinida funksiya taxminan kvadratik forma.

- Musbat aniqlangan — barqaror minimum.
- Aniqlanmagan — «egar nuqta»: o'qitish shu yerda «qotib qolishi» mumkin.

---

## Normal ko'rinish

Kanonik ko'rinishdan $y_i \to y_i/\sqrt{|\lambda_i|}$ almashtirish bilan:
$$f = 2y_1^2 - 2y_2^2 + 6y_3^2 \ \to\ z_1^2 - z_2^2 + z_3^2.$$

Normal ko'rinish **yagona** (tartib aniqligida) — uni $p$ va $q$ to'liq aniqlaydi.

---

## Tipik xatolar

- Lagranj usulida almashtirishning xosmasligini tekshirmaslik.
- Manfiy aniqlanganlik uchun «barcha $\Delta_k < 0$» deb o'ylash.
- Kanonik koeffitsiyentlarni yagona deb hisoblash (yagona — $p$ va $q$).
- Ortogonal usulda xos vektorlarni normallamaslik.

---

## Xulosa

- Kanonik ko'rinish: faqat kvadratlar.
- Lagranj usuli — to'liq kvadratlar; ortogonal usul — xos sonlar.
- Inersiya qonuni: $p$, $q$ — invariant.
- Silvestr: musbat aniqlangan $\iff$ barcha $\Delta_k > 0$.
