## Nima uchun yangi sonlar kerak?

$x^2 + 1 = 0$ tenglama haqiqiy sonlarda yechimga ega emas: har qanday haqiqiy $x$ uchun $x^2 \ge 0$.

- XVI asrda Kardano kub tenglamalarni yechishda «manfiy sondan kvadrat ildiz» bilan ishlashga majbur bo'lgan.
- Bugun kompleks sonlar elektrotexnika (o'zgaruvchan tok), signallarni qayta ishlash, kvant mexanikasi va tebranishlar nazariyasining tili.

---

## Mavhum birlik

:::tarif
**Mavhum birlik** $i$ — kvadrati $-1$ ga teng son:
$$i^2 = -1.$$
:::

$i$ ning darajalari 4 qadam bilan takrorlanadi:
$$i^1 = i,\quad i^2 = -1,\quad i^3 = -i,\quad i^4 = 1.$$

---

## Kompleks son

:::tarif
$z = x + iy$ ($x, y \in \mathbb R$) — **kompleks son**.
- $x = \operatorname{Re} z$ — haqiqiy qism
- $y = \operatorname{Im} z$ — mavhum qism
:::

- $y = 0$: $z$ — haqiqiy son, demak $\mathbb R \subset \mathbb C$.
- $x = 0$: $z$ — sof mavhum son.
- Ikki kompleks son teng $\iff$ haqiqiy va mavhum qismlari mos ravishda teng.

---

## Kvadrat tenglama endi doim yechiladi

$x^2 - 2px + q = 0$, bunda $q - p^2 > 0$ (diskriminant manfiy):
$$x_{1,2} = p \pm i\sqrt{q - p^2}.$$

:::misol
$x^2 - 4x + 13 = 0$: $p = 2$, $q - p^2 = 9$, demak $x_{1,2} = 2 \pm 3i$.
:::

---

## Qo'shish va ayirish

$z_1 = x_1 + iy_1$, $z_2 = x_2 + iy_2$:
$$z_1 \pm z_2 = (x_1 \pm x_2) + i(y_1 \pm y_2).$$

Haqiqiy va mavhum qismlar alohida qo'shiladi — xuddi vektorlar kabi.

:::misol
$(3 - 2i) + (1 + 5i) = 4 + 3i$, $\quad (3 - 2i) - (1 + 5i) = 2 - 7i$.
:::

---

## Ko'paytirish

Qavslarni ochib, $i^2 = -1$ ni hisobga olamiz:
$$z_1z_2 = (x_1x_2 - y_1y_2) + i(x_1y_2 + x_2y_1).$$

:::misol
$(1 + 2i)(3 + 4i) = 3 + 4i + 6i + 8i^2 = 3 + 10i - 8 = -5 + 10i.$
:::

---

## Mashq: ko'paytma

:::savol
$(2 - i)(1 + 3i)$ nimaga teng?
- $2 - 3i$
+ $5 + 5i$
- $-1 + 5i$
- $5 - 5i$
> $2 + 6i - i - 3i^2 = 2 + 5i + 3 = 5 + 5i$.
:::

---

## $i$ ning darajalari

Daraja ko'rsatkichini 4 ga bo'lib, qoldiqqa qaraymiz:
$$i^n = i^{\,n \bmod 4}.$$

:::misol
- $i^{103}$: $103 = 4\cdot 25 + 3$, demak $i^{103} = i^3 = -i$.
- $i^{2026}$: $2026 = 4\cdot 506 + 2$, demak $i^{2026} = -1$.
:::

---

## Qo'shma son

:::tarif
$z = x + iy$ ga **qo'shma** son: $\bar z = x - iy$.
:::

:::xossa
- $\overline{z_1 + z_2} = \bar z_1 + \bar z_2$, $\quad\overline{z_1z_2} = \bar z_1\,\bar z_2$
- $z + \bar z = 2x$ — haqiqiy son
- $z\bar z = x^2 + y^2$ — nomanfiy haqiqiy son
:::

Geometrik ma'nosi: $Ox$ o'qqa nisbatan simmetrik nuqta.

---

## Modul

:::tarif
$$|z| = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2} = \sqrt{z\bar z}$$
— koordinata boshidan $z$ nuqtagacha masofa.
:::

:::xossa
$|z_1z_2| = |z_1|\,|z_2|$, $\qquad |z_1 + z_2| \le |z_1| + |z_2|$ (uchburchak tengsizligi).
:::

:::misol
$|3 - 4i| = \sqrt{9 + 16} = 5$.
:::

---

## Bo'lish

Surat va maxrajni **maxrajning qo'shmasiga** ko'paytiramiz — maxraj haqiqiy bo'ladi:
$$\frac{z_1}{z_2} = \frac{z_1\bar z_2}{z_2\bar z_2} = \frac{z_1\bar z_2}{|z_2|^2}.$$

---

## Bo'lish: misol

$$\frac{1 + 2i}{3 + 4i} = \frac{(1 + 2i)(3 - 4i)}{(3 + 4i)(3 - 4i)} = \frac{3 - 4i + 6i - 8i^2}{9 + 16} = \frac{11 + 2i}{25}.$$

Javob: $\dfrac{11}{25} + \dfrac{2}{25}i$.

Tekshirish: $(3 + 4i)\cdot\dfrac{11 + 2i}{25} = \dfrac{33 + 6i + 44i - 8}{25} = \dfrac{25 + 50i}{25} = 1 + 2i$ ✓

---

## Kompleks tekislik

$z = x + iy$ ga tekislikdagi $(x;\ y)$ nuqta mos keladi.

- Gorizontal o'q — **haqiqiy o'q**, vertikal o'q — **mavhum o'q**.
- Qo'shish — radius-vektorlarni parallelogramm qoidasi bilan qo'shish.
- $|z|$ — radius-vektor uzunligi.
- $|z_1 - z_2|$ — ikki nuqta orasidagi masofa.

---

## Argument

:::tarif
$\varphi = \arg z$ — $Ox$ musbat yo'nalishidan $z$ ning radius-vektorigacha bo'lgan burchak:
$$\cos\varphi = \frac{x}{|z|},\qquad \sin\varphi = \frac{y}{|z|}.$$
:::

Asosiy qiymat odatda $-\pi < \varphi \le \pi$ oralig'ida olinadi.

:::diqqat
$\operatorname{tg}\varphi = y/x$ dan burchakni topishda nuqta **qaysi chorakda** ekanini albatta hisobga oling.
:::

---

## Trigonometrik shakl

$$z = r(\cos\varphi + i\sin\varphi),\qquad r = |z|,\ \varphi = \arg z.$$

:::misol
$z = 1 - i$: $\ r = \sqrt2$, nuqta IV chorakda, $\varphi = -\dfrac{\pi}{4}$.
$$1 - i = \sqrt2\left(\cos\left(-\tfrac{\pi}{4}\right) + i\sin\left(-\tfrac{\pi}{4}\right)\right).$$
:::

---

## Mashq: argument

:::savol
$z = -2i$ sonining argumenti (asosiy qiymati) nimaga teng?
- $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
+ $-\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- $\pi$
- $0$
> Nuqta $(0;\ -2)$ mavhum o'qning manfiy qismida yotadi.
:::

---

## Trigonometrik shaklda ko'paytirish

:::teorema
$$z_1z_2 = r_1r_2\big(\cos(\varphi_1 + \varphi_2) + i\sin(\varphi_1 + \varphi_2)\big).$$
**Modullar ko'paytiriladi, argumentlar qo'shiladi.**
:::

Isbot: qavslarni ochib, $\cos(\alpha + \beta)$ va $\sin(\alpha + \beta)$ formulalarini qo'llaymiz.

Geometrik ma'no: $z$ ga $e^{i\varphi}$ ni ko'paytirish — tekislikni $\varphi$ burchakka **burish**.

---

## Trigonometrik shaklda bo'lish

$$\frac{z_1}{z_2} = \frac{r_1}{r_2}\big(\cos(\varphi_1 - \varphi_2) + i\sin(\varphi_1 - \varphi_2)\big).$$

**Modullar bo'linadi, argumentlar ayiriladi.**

:::misol
$z_1 = 6\left(\cos\frac{\pi}{2} + i\sin\frac{\pi}{2}\right)$, $z_2 = 2\left(\cos\frac{\pi}{6} + i\sin\frac{\pi}{6}\right)$:
$$\frac{z_1}{z_2} = 3\left(\cos\frac{\pi}{3} + i\sin\frac{\pi}{3}\right) = \frac32 + \frac{3\sqrt3}{2}i.$$
:::

---

## Ko'rsatkichli shakl

:::teorema Eyler formulasi
$$e^{i\varphi} = \cos\varphi + i\sin\varphi.$$
:::

Demak $z = re^{i\varphi}$ — **ko'rsatkichli shakl**. Amallar daraja xossalari kabi bajariladi:
$$r_1e^{i\varphi_1}\cdot r_2e^{i\varphi_2} = r_1r_2\,e^{i(\varphi_1 + \varphi_2)}.$$

Xususan: $e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0$ — matematikaning eng mashhur tengliklaridan biri.

---

## Muavr formulasi

:::teorema Muavr formulasi
Istalgan butun $n$ uchun
$$\big(r(\cos\varphi + i\sin\varphi)\big)^n = r^n(\cos n\varphi + i\sin n\varphi).$$
:::

Bu ko'paytirish qoidasini $n$ marta qo'llashdan kelib chiqadi.

---

## Muavr formulasi: misol 1

$(1 - i)^8 = ?$

1. $1 - i = \sqrt2\left(\cos\left(-\frac\pi4\right) + i\sin\left(-\frac\pi4\right)\right)$
2. $(\sqrt2)^8 = 16$, $\quad 8\cdot\left(-\frac\pi4\right) = -2\pi$
3. $(1 - i)^8 = 16\left(\cos(-2\pi) + i\sin(-2\pi)\right) = 16.$

---

## Muavr formulasi: misol 2

$(1 + i)^6 = ?$

1. $1 + i = \sqrt2\left(\cos\frac\pi4 + i\sin\frac\pi4\right)$
2. $(\sqrt2)^6 = 8$, $\quad 6\cdot\frac\pi4 = \frac{3\pi}{2}$
3. $(1 + i)^6 = 8\left(\cos\frac{3\pi}{2} + i\sin\frac{3\pi}{2}\right) = 8(0 - i) = -8i.$

Ochib chiqib tekshirish ancha uzoq — Muavr formulasining kuchi shunda.

---

## Trigonometriyaga tatbiq

$n = 2$ da Muavr formulasi:
$$(\cos\varphi + i\sin\varphi)^2 = \cos2\varphi + i\sin2\varphi.$$
Chap tomonni ochamiz: $\cos^2\varphi - \sin^2\varphi + 2i\sin\varphi\cos\varphi$.

Haqiqiy va mavhum qismlarni tenglaymiz:
$$\cos2\varphi = \cos^2\varphi - \sin^2\varphi,\qquad \sin2\varphi = 2\sin\varphi\cos\varphi.$$

---

## $n$-darajali ildiz

:::teorema
$z = r(\cos\varphi + i\sin\varphi) \ne 0$ son roppa-rosa $n$ ta har xil $n$-darajali ildizga ega:
$$z_k = \sqrt[n]{r}\left(\cos\frac{\varphi + 2\pi k}{n} + i\sin\frac{\varphi + 2\pi k}{n}\right),\quad k = 0, 1, \dots, n - 1.$$
:::

---

## Ildizlarning geometriyasi

- Barcha ildizlar radiusi $\sqrt[n]{r}$ bo'lgan aylanada yotadi.
- Qo'shni ildizlar orasidagi burchak $\dfrac{2\pi}{n}$.
- Ildizlar **muntazam $n$-burchak** uchlarini tashkil etadi.

$k = n$ deb olsak, yana $z_0$ hosil bo'ladi — shuning uchun ildizlar soni aynan $n$ ta.

---

## Misol: birning kub ildizlari

$\sqrt[3]{1}$: $r = 1$, $\varphi = 0$:
$$z_k = \cos\frac{2\pi k}{3} + i\sin\frac{2\pi k}{3},\quad k = 0, 1, 2.$$

$$z_0 = 1,\qquad z_1 = -\frac12 + \frac{\sqrt3}{2}i,\qquad z_2 = -\frac12 - \frac{\sqrt3}{2}i.$$

Ular birlik aylanaga ichki chizilgan muntazam uchburchak uchlari.

---

## Mashq: ildizlar soni

:::savol
$z^4 = 16$ tenglama nechta kompleks ildizga ega?
- 1
- 2
+ 4
- 16
> Ildizlar: $2,\ 2i,\ -2,\ -2i$.
:::

## Bezu teoremasi

:::teorema Bezu
$P(z)$ ko'phadni $z - \alpha$ ga bo'lishdagi qoldiq $P(\alpha)$ ga teng.
:::

Natija: $P(\alpha) = 0 \iff P(z)$ ko'phad $z - \alpha$ ga qoldiqsiz bo'linadi.

Bo'lishni **Gorner sxemasi** bilan bajarish qulay. Masalan, $2x^5 + 3x^4 - x^2 + x - 5$ ni $x - 2$ ga bo'lganda qoldiq $P(2) = 64 + 48 - 4 + 2 - 5 = 105$.

---

## Algebraning asosiy teoremasi

:::teorema
$n \ge 1$ darajali har qanday ko'phad kamida bitta kompleks ildizga ega.
:::

Natijalar:
- $n$-darajali ko'phad karraliklari bilan hisoblanganda roppa-rosa $n$ ta ildizga ega.
- Haqiqiy koeffitsiyentli ko'phadda: $\alpha$ ildiz bo'lsa, $\bar\alpha$ ham ildiz.
- Toq darajali haqiqiy ko'phad kamida bitta haqiqiy ildizga ega.
