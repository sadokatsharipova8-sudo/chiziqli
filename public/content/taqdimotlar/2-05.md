## Bir obyekt — turli koordinatalar

- $xy = 1$ va $\dfrac{x'^2}{2} - \dfrac{y'^2}{2} = 1$ — **bitta** giperbola!
- $x^2 + y^2 - 4x + 6y = 3$ va $x'^2 + y'^2 = 16$ — **bitta** aylana!

Koordinata sistemasini to'g'ri tanlash tenglamani keskin soddalashtiradi.

---

## Hayotda

- **GPS:** sun'iy yo'ldosh, Yer va xarita koordinatalari orasida doimiy almashtirish.
- **Robototexnika:** har bir bo'g'inning o'z koordinata sistemasi.
- **Kompyuter o'yinlari:** obyekt → dunyo → kamera → ekran.
- **Aviatsiya:** samolyot (yaw, pitch, roll) va Yer koordinatalari.

## Affin sistema

:::tarif
$O$ nuqta va $\mathbf e_1, \mathbf e_2, \mathbf e_3$ bazis — **affin koordinatalar sistemasi**:
$$\overrightarrow{OM} = x\mathbf e_1 + y\mathbf e_2 + z\mathbf e_3.$$
:::

Bazis ortonormal ($\mathbf i, \mathbf j, \mathbf k$) — **to'g'ri burchakli Dekart** sistemasi.

---

## Masofa va yuza

$$|AB| = \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2 + (z_2 - z_1)^2}.$$

Tekislikdagi uchburchak:
$$S = \frac12\left|\begin{vmatrix} x_1 & y_1 & 1\\ x_2 & y_2 & 1\\ x_3 & y_3 & 1\end{vmatrix}\right|.$$
Determinant nol — uch nuqta bitta to'g'ri chiziqda.

## Parallel ko'chirish

Yangi boshi $O'(a;\ b)$, o'qlar yo'nalishi saqlanadi:
$$x = x' + a,\quad y = y' + b;\qquad x' = x - a,\quad y' = y - b.$$

Fazoda: $z = z' + c$ qo'shiladi.

---

## Ko'chirish: misol 1

$O'(2;\ 3)$, $M(5;\ 1)$:
$$x' = 5 - 2 = 3,\quad y' = 1 - 3 = -2.$$

Yangi koordinatalar $M'(3;\ -2)$.

---

## Ko'chirish: misol 2

$$x^2 + y^2 - 4x + 6y = 3 \Rightarrow (x - 2)^2 + (y + 3)^2 = 16.$$

$O'(2;\ -3)$ ga ko'chiramiz: $x'^2 + y'^2 = 16$ — aylana, $R = 4$.

**Ko'chirish birinchi darajali hadlarni yo'qotadi** (to'liq kvadrat ajratish).

---

## O'qlarni burish

O'qlar $\alpha$ ga burilsa:
$$\begin{cases} x = x'\cos\alpha - y'\sin\alpha\\ y = x'\sin\alpha + y'\cos\alpha\end{cases}\qquad \begin{cases} x' = x\cos\alpha + y\sin\alpha\\ y' = -x\sin\alpha + y\cos\alpha\end{cases}$$

---

## Formulaning kelib chiqishi

Yangi birlik vektorlar: $\mathbf i' = (\cos\alpha;\ \sin\alpha)$, $\mathbf j' = (-\sin\alpha;\ \cos\alpha)$.

$\overrightarrow{OM} = x'\mathbf i' + y'\mathbf j'$ ni koordinatalarda yozamiz — burish formulalari.

Matritsa shaklida (1-semestr 9-mavzu, $X = TX'$):
$$\begin{pmatrix} x\\ y\end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} \cos\alpha & -\sin\alpha\\ \sin\alpha & \cos\alpha\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} x'\\ y'\end{pmatrix}.$$

---

## Burish matritsasi

$$T = \begin{pmatrix} \cos\alpha & -\sin\alpha\\ \sin\alpha & \cos\alpha\end{pmatrix}$$

- **Ortogonal:** $T^TT = E$, $T^{-1} = T^T$;
- $\det T = 1$;
- masofa va burchaklarni saqlaydi.

Shuning uchun teskari formulalar shunchaki $T^T$.

---

## Burish: misol 1

$90^\circ$, $M(3;\ 1)$:
$$x' = 3\cdot0 + 1\cdot1 = 1,\quad y' = -3\cdot1 + 1\cdot0 = -3.$$

$M'(1;\ -3)$. Masofa saqlangan: $\sqrt{10} = \sqrt{10}$ ✓

---

## Burish: misol 2 (giperbola)

$xy = 1$, $\alpha = 45^\circ$:
$$x = \frac{x' - y'}{\sqrt2},\quad y = \frac{x' + y'}{\sqrt2}\ \Rightarrow\ xy = \frac{x'^2 - y'^2}{2} = 1.$$

$\dfrac{x'^2}{2} - \dfrac{y'^2}{2} = 1$ — teng tomonli giperbola. **Burish $xy$ hadni yo'qotadi.**

---

## Burish burchagini tanlash

$Ax^2 + 2Bxy + Cy^2$ da $xy$ hadni yo'qotish uchun:
$$\operatorname{ctg}2\alpha = \frac{A - C}{2B}.$$

$A = C$ bo'lsa — $\alpha = 45^\circ$. Batafsil — 14-mavzu (xos vektorlar orqali).

---

## Umumiy almashtirish

Ko'chirish + burish:
$$x = x'\cos\alpha - y'\sin\alpha + a,\qquad y = x'\sin\alpha + y'\cos\alpha + b.$$

Teskarisi:
$$x' = (x - a)\cos\alpha + (y - b)\sin\alpha,\quad y' = -(x - a)\sin\alpha + (y - b)\cos\alpha.$$

---

## Umumiy: misol

Boshi $O'(-2;\ 2)$, burish $180^\circ$, $M(3;\ 4)$:
1. $x - a = 5$, $y - b = 2$;
2. $x' = 5\cdot(-1) + 2\cdot0 = -5$, $y' = -5\cdot0 + 2\cdot(-1) = -2$.

$M'(-5;\ -2)$. Mashqlar bo'limidagi «Koordinatalarni almashtirish» masalalari shunday.

---

## Harakatlar

Ko'chirish va burishlar — **harakatlar**: masofa va burchaklarni saqlaydi.

- Figuraning shakli o'zgarmaydi, faqat «qaraydigan joyimiz» o'zgaradi.
- Shuning uchun kanonik ko'rinishga keltirishda chiziq turi va o'lchamlari saqlanadi (12–15-mavzular).

---

## Fazoda ko'chirish

$$x = x' + a,\quad y = y' + b,\quad z = z' + c.$$

:::misol
$x^2 + y^2 + z^2 - 2x + 4y - 6z = 2$: $O'(1;\ -2;\ 3)$ ga ko'chirsak $x'^2 + y'^2 + z'^2 = 16$ — sfera.
:::

---

## Fazoda burish

Yangi birlik vektorlar $\mathbf i', \mathbf j', \mathbf k'$ ning eski koordinatalari — yo'naltiruvchi kosinuslar:
$$\begin{pmatrix} x\\ y\\ z\end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} \cos\alpha_1 & \cos\alpha_2 & \cos\alpha_3\\ \cos\beta_1 & \cos\beta_2 & \cos\beta_3\\ \cos\gamma_1 & \cos\gamma_2 & \cos\gamma_3\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} x'\\ y'\\ z'\end{pmatrix}.$$

Matritsa ortogonal: ustunlari ortonormal, $\det T = \pm1$.

---

## $Oz$ atrofida burish

$$T = \begin{pmatrix} \cos\alpha & -\sin\alpha & 0\\ \sin\alpha & \cos\alpha & 0\\ 0 & 0 & 1\end{pmatrix}.$$

$z$ o'zgarmaydi; $(x, y)$ tekislikda burish.

---

## Eyler burchaklari

Fazodagi har qanday burish — uchta ketma-ket burish:
- **yaw** — vertikal o'q atrofida (burilish);
- **pitch** — ko'ndalang o'q atrofida (burun ko'tarilishi);
- **roll** — bo'ylama o'q atrofida (qanot og'ishi).

$T = R_z(\psi)R_y(\theta)R_x(\varphi)$. Samolyot, dron, smartfon giroskopi shunday ishlaydi.

## Tatbiq: GPS

- Sun'iy yo'ldosh Yer markazi bilan bog'liq (ECEF) koordinatalarda o'lchaydi.
- Xaritada — mahalliy «sharq–shimol–yuqori» (ENU) koordinatalari.
- O'tish: ko'chirish (kuzatuvchi nuqtasiga) + burish (kenglik va uzunlik burchaklariga).

---

## Tatbiq: robot qo'li

Har bir bo'g'in o'z koordinata sistemasiga ega. Qo'l uchining dunyo koordinatalari:
$$\mathbf p = T_1(T_2(T_3\mathbf p_3 + \mathbf d_3) + \mathbf d_2) + \mathbf d_1.$$

Burish matritsalari va ko'chirishlar ketma-ketligi — «to'g'ri kinematika».

---

## Tipik xatolar

- $\sin$ oldidagi ishorani chalkashtirish.
- To'g'ri va teskari formulalarni aralashtirish.
- Avval burib, keyin ko'chirish kerak bo'lgan joyda tartibni buzish.
- $\cos$, $\sin$ qiymatlarini burchakka mos tanlamaslik ($270^\circ$ da $\sin = -1$).

---

## Xulosa

- Ko'chirish: $x = x' + a$ — chiziqli hadlarni yo'qotadi.
- Burish: ortogonal $T$, $X = TX'$, $X' = T^TX$ — $xy$ hadni yo'qotadi.
- Harakatlar masofa va burchakni saqlaydi.
- Fazoda: yo'naltiruvchi kosinuslar matritsasi, Eyler burchaklari.
