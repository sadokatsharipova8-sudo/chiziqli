## Qayerda uchraydi?

- **Geometriya:** ikkinchi tartibli chiziq va sirtlarning bosh qismi $ax^2 + 2bxy + cy^2$.
- **Analiz:** ko'p o'zgaruvchili funksiya ekstremumi — ikkinchi differensial $d^2f$.
- **Mexanika:** kinetik va potensial energiya.
- **Statistika:** dispersiya, kovariatsiya.
- **Optimallashtirish:** funksiyaning qavariqligi.

## Chiziqli forma

:::tarif
$f: V \to \mathbb R$,
$$f(\alpha\mathbf x + \beta\mathbf y) = \alpha f(\mathbf x) + \beta f(\mathbf y)$$
— **chiziqli forma** (chiziqli funksional).
:::

Bazisda: $f(\mathbf x) = a_1x_1 + \dots + a_nx_n$, $a_i = f(\mathbf e_i)$.

---

## Chiziqli forma: misollar

- $f(x, y, z) = 2x - y + 5z$;
- Yevklid fazosida $f(\mathbf x) = (\mathbf a, \mathbf x)$;
- $C[a, b]$ da $f(g) = \displaystyle\int_a^b g(t)\,dt$;
- matritsa izi $f(A) = \operatorname{tr}A$;
- $P_n$ da $f(p) = p(2)$ (nuqtadagi qiymat).

Iqtisodda: narxlar vektori bilan xarid vektorining skalyar ko'paytmasi — umumiy narx.

---

## Bichiziqli forma

:::tarif
$B(\mathbf x, \mathbf y)$ har bir argumenti bo'yicha chiziqli bo'lsa — **bichiziqli forma**:
$$B(\alpha\mathbf x_1 + \beta\mathbf x_2, \mathbf y) = \alpha B(\mathbf x_1, \mathbf y) + \beta B(\mathbf x_2, \mathbf y),$$
va ikkinchi argument bo'yicha ham.
:::

---

## Bichiziqli forma matritsasi

$$B(\mathbf x, \mathbf y) = \sum_{i,j}b_{ij}x_iy_j = X^TBY,\qquad b_{ij} = B(\mathbf e_i, \mathbf e_j).$$

:::misol
$B(\mathbf x, \mathbf y) = 2x_1y_1 - x_1y_2 + 3x_2y_1 + 4x_2y_2$:
$$B = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & -1\\ 3 & 4\end{pmatrix}.$$
:::

---

## Hisoblash misoli

$B(\mathbf x, \mathbf y) = x_1y_1 - x_1y_2 - 3x_2y_1$, $\mathbf x = (-3, -1)$, $\mathbf y = (3, 2)$:
$$B = (-3)(3) - (-3)(2) - 3(-1)(3) = -9 + 6 + 9 = 6.$$

Matritsa orqali: $X^TBY = (-3, -1)\begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1\\ -3 & 0\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} 3\\ 2\end{pmatrix} = (-3, -1)\begin{pmatrix} 1\\ -9\end{pmatrix} = 6$ ✓

---

## Simmetrik formalar

:::tarif
$B(\mathbf x, \mathbf y) = B(\mathbf y, \mathbf x)$ — **simmetrik** $\iff B^T = B$.
:::

Misollar:
- skalyar ko'paytma ($B = E$);
- $B(\mathbf x, \mathbf y) = x_1y_1 + 2x_1y_2 + 2x_2y_1 - x_2y_2$ ($b_{12} = b_{21} = 2$).

---

## Mashq: simmetriklik

:::savol
Qaysi bichiziqli forma simmetrik?
- $x_1y_2 - x_2y_1$
+ $x_1y_1 + 3x_1y_2 + 3x_2y_1$
- $x_1y_2$
- $2x_1y_1 + x_2y_1$
:::

---

## Bazis almashganda

$X = TX'$, $Y = TY'$:
$$B(\mathbf x, \mathbf y) = (TX')^TB(TY') = X'^T(T^TBT)Y'.$$

:::xossa
$B' = T^TBT$; rang o'zgarmaydi — **forma rangi**.
:::

---

## Operator bilan farq

| Obyekt | Bazis almashganda |
|---|---|
| chiziqli operator | $A' = T^{-1}AT$ |
| bichiziqli forma | $B' = T^TBT$ |

Ortogonal $T$ da ($T^{-1} = T^T$) — bir xil. Shuning uchun Yevklid fazosida simmetrik operator va kvadratik forma «bitta obyekt» kabi ishlaydi.

---

## Kvadratik forma

:::tarif
Simmetrik bichiziqli formada $\mathbf y = \mathbf x$:
$$f(\mathbf x) = \sum_{i,j}a_{ij}x_ix_j = X^TAX,\qquad A^T = A.$$
:::

Ikki o'zgaruvchi: $f = a_{11}x^2 + 2a_{12}xy + a_{22}y^2$.

---

## Matritsani tuzish qoidasi

:::diqqat
- $x_i^2$ oldidagi koeffitsiyent → $a_{ii}$ (diagonal).
- $x_ix_j$ ($i \ne j$) oldidagi koeffitsiyent **ikkiga bo'linib** → $a_{ij} = a_{ji}$.
:::

$$f = 3x_1^2 - 2x_2^2 + x_3^2 + 4x_1x_2 - 6x_2x_3 \Rightarrow A = \begin{pmatrix} 3 & 2 & 0\\ 2 & -2 & -3\\ 0 & -3 & 1\end{pmatrix}.$$

---

## Mashq: matritsa elementi

:::savol
$f = x_1^2 + 6x_1x_2 - 5x_2^2$ matritsasida $a_{12}$?
- 6
+ 3
- −5
- 1
:::

---

## Teskari yo'nalish

Matritsadan formani tiklash:
$$A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -2\\ -2 & 4\end{pmatrix} \Rightarrow f = x^2 - 4xy + 4y^2 = (x - 2y)^2.$$

$\det A = 0$, $r = 1$ — forma bitta kvadratga aylanadi.

---

## Forma qiymati

$f = x^2 - 2xy$ ($A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1\\ -1 & 0\end{pmatrix}$), $\mathbf v = (-1, -1)$:
$$f(\mathbf v) = 1 - 2 = -1.$$

Matritsa orqali: $X^TAX = (-1, -1)\begin{pmatrix} 0\\ 1\end{pmatrix} = -1$ ✓

---

## Qutblashtirish

Kvadratik forma $f$ dan simmetrik bichiziqli formani tiklash:
$$B(\mathbf x, \mathbf y) = \frac12\big(f(\mathbf x + \mathbf y) - f(\mathbf x) - f(\mathbf y)\big).$$

Masalan, $f(\mathbf x) = |\mathbf x|^2$ dan: $(\mathbf x, \mathbf y) = \frac12\big(|\mathbf x + \mathbf y|^2 - |\mathbf x|^2 - |\mathbf y|^2\big)$.

Simmetrik bichiziqli formalar va kvadratik formalar — **bir-biriga mos**.

---

## Kvadratik forma rangi

$f$ rangi = $r(A)$. Bazis almashganda o'zgarmaydi.

:::misol
$f = (x + y)^2 + (y + z)^2$: $A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & 0\\ 1 & 2 & 1\\ 0 & 1 & 1\end{pmatrix}$, $\det A = 1(2 - 1) - 1(1 - 0) = 0$; rang 2.
:::

---

## Tatbiq: ekstremum

$f(x, y)$ ning statsionar nuqtasida Teylor formulasi:
$$f(x_0 + h, y_0 + k) - f(x_0, y_0) \approx \tfrac12\big(f_{xx}h^2 + 2f_{xy}hk + f_{yy}k^2\big).$$

O'ng tomon — **kvadratik forma**. Uning ishorasi — minimum, maksimum yoki egar (14-mavzu).

---

## Tatbiq: energiya

- Kinetik energiya: $T = \frac12\sum m_{ij}\dot q_i\dot q_j$ — tezliklarga nisbatan kvadratik forma.
- Prujinalar tizimi potensial energiyasi: $U = \frac12X^TKX$, $K$ — qattiqlik matritsasi.

Tebranishlar chastotalari — bu formalarni bir vaqtda «diagonallash» (18-mavzu).

---

## Tatbiq: statistika

$n$ ta aksiyaga $\mathbf w$ vaznlar bilan investitsiya. Portfel dispersiyasi (risk):
$$\sigma^2 = \mathbf w^T\Sigma\mathbf w,$$
$\Sigma$ — kovariatsiya matritsasi. Riskni minimallashtirish — kvadratik formani minimallashtirish (Markovits, Nobel mukofoti 1990).

---

## Bichiziqli forma: bazis almashinuvi misoli

$B(\mathbf x, \mathbf y) = x_1y_2 + x_2y_1$, $B = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1\\ 1 & 0\end{pmatrix}$. Yangi bazis $\mathbf e'_1 = (1, 1)$, $\mathbf e'_2 = (1, -1)$, $T = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1\\ 1 & -1\end{pmatrix}$:
$$B' = T^TBT = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 0\\ 0 & -2\end{pmatrix}.$$

Yangi bazisda forma diagonal: $B = 2x'_1y'_1 - 2x'_2y'_2$.

---

## Kvadratik forma: diagonal holat

$A$ diagonal bo'lsa, forma faqat kvadratlardan iborat:
$$A = \begin{pmatrix} 3 & 0\\ 0 & -1\end{pmatrix} \Rightarrow f = 3x^2 - y^2.$$

Bu eng qulay — **kanonik** ko'rinish. Uni qanday hosil qilish — keyingi mavzu.

---

## Mashq: forma qiymati

:::savol
$f = x^2 + 2xy - 3y^2$, $\mathbf v = (1, 1)$. $f(\mathbf v)$?
+ 0
- 1
- −1
- 4
> $1 + 2 - 3 = 0$.
:::

---

## Mashq: matritsadan forma

:::savol
$A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 3\\ 3 & -1\end{pmatrix}$ matritsali kvadratik forma?
- $2x^2 + 3xy - y^2$
+ $2x^2 + 6xy - y^2$
- $2x^2 + 9xy - y^2$
- $2x^2 - 6xy - y^2$
:::

## Tipik xatolar

- $x_ix_j$ koeffitsiyentini ikkiga bo'lmaslik.
- Nosimmetrik matritsa bilan kvadratik forma yozish (mumkin, lekin noqulay; doim simmetriklashtiring).
- $T^TBT$ o'rniga $T^{-1}BT$ yozish.
- Bichiziqli formada $X^TBY$ tartibini buzish.

---

## Xulosa

- Chiziqli forma: $f(\mathbf x) = \sum a_ix_i$.
- Bichiziqli forma: $X^TBY$; bazis almashganda $T^TBT$.
- Kvadratik forma: $X^TAX$, $A$ simmetrik; $x_ix_j$ koeffitsiyenti ikkiga bo'linadi.
- Simmetrik bichiziqli va kvadratik formalar — qutblashtirish orqali bog'liq.
