## O'ng va chap uchlik

Nokomplanar $\mathbf a, \mathbf b, \mathbf c$ (tartib muhim!):
- **o'ng uchlik** — $\mathbf c$ ning oxiridan qaraganda $\mathbf a$ dan $\mathbf b$ ga eng qisqa burilish **soat strelkasiga qarshi**;
- aks holda — **chap uchlik**.

**O'ng qo'l qoidasi:** bosh barmoq — $\mathbf a$, ko'rsatkich — $\mathbf b$, o'rta barmoq — $\mathbf c$.

$\mathbf i, \mathbf j, \mathbf k$ — o'ng uchlik.

---

## Vektor ko'paytma

:::tarif
$\mathbf c = \mathbf a \times \mathbf b$ — **vektor ko'paytma**:
1. $|\mathbf c| = |\mathbf a|\,|\mathbf b|\sin\varphi$
2. $\mathbf c \perp \mathbf a$, $\mathbf c \perp \mathbf b$
3. $\mathbf a, \mathbf b, \mathbf c$ — o'ng uchlik
:::

Natija — **vektor** (skalyar ko'paytmadan farqli). Belgilash: $\mathbf a \times \mathbf b$ yoki $[\mathbf a, \mathbf b]$.

---

## Geometrik ma'no

$$|\mathbf a \times \mathbf b| = |\mathbf a|\,|\mathbf b|\sin\varphi = S_{\text{parallelogramm}}.$$

- Vektor ko'paytma uzunligi — $\mathbf a$, $\mathbf b$ ga qurilgan **parallelogramm yuzi**.
- Yo'nalishi — shu parallelogramm tekisligiga **perpendikulyar**.
- Uchburchak yuzi: $S = \frac12|\mathbf a \times \mathbf b|$.

---

## Xossalar

:::xossa
1. $\mathbf a \times \mathbf b = -\mathbf b \times \mathbf a$ — **antikommutativ**
2. $(\lambda\mathbf a) \times \mathbf b = \lambda(\mathbf a \times \mathbf b)$
3. $\mathbf a \times (\mathbf b + \mathbf c) = \mathbf a \times \mathbf b + \mathbf a \times \mathbf c$
4. $\mathbf a \times \mathbf b = \mathbf 0 \iff \mathbf a \parallel \mathbf b$; xususan $\mathbf a \times \mathbf a = \mathbf 0$
:::

:::diqqat
Vektor ko'paytma **assotsiativ emas**: $(\mathbf a \times \mathbf b) \times \mathbf c \ne \mathbf a \times (\mathbf b \times \mathbf c)$.
:::

---

## Bazis vektorlari

$$\mathbf i \times \mathbf j = \mathbf k,\qquad \mathbf j \times \mathbf k = \mathbf i,\qquad \mathbf k \times \mathbf i = \mathbf j.$$
$$\mathbf j \times \mathbf i = -\mathbf k,\qquad \mathbf k \times \mathbf j = -\mathbf i,\qquad \mathbf i \times \mathbf k = -\mathbf j.$$

**Doiraviy qoida:** $\mathbf i \to \mathbf j \to \mathbf k \to \mathbf i$ yo'nalishida «+», teskarisiga «−».

---

## Mashq: bazis

:::savol
$\mathbf i \times \mathbf k$ = ?
- $\mathbf j$
+ $-\mathbf j$
- $\mathbf 0$
- $\mathbf i$
> $\mathbf k \times \mathbf i = \mathbf j$, demak $\mathbf i \times \mathbf k = -\mathbf j$.
:::

---

## Koordinatalardagi formula

:::teorema
$$\mathbf a \times \mathbf b = \begin{vmatrix} \mathbf i & \mathbf j & \mathbf k\\ a_x & a_y & a_z\\ b_x & b_y & b_z\end{vmatrix}.$$
:::

1-satr bo'yicha yoyib:
$$\mathbf a \times \mathbf b = \begin{vmatrix} a_y & a_z\\ b_y & b_z\end{vmatrix}\mathbf i - \begin{vmatrix} a_x & a_z\\ b_x & b_z\end{vmatrix}\mathbf j + \begin{vmatrix} a_x & a_y\\ b_x & b_y\end{vmatrix}\mathbf k.$$

---

## Misol: vektor ko'paytma

$\mathbf a = (1;\ 2;\ -1)$, $\mathbf b = (2;\ 0;\ 3)$:
$$\mathbf a \times \mathbf b = \begin{vmatrix} \mathbf i & \mathbf j & \mathbf k\\ 1 & 2 & -1\\ 2 & 0 & 3\end{vmatrix} = (6 - 0)\mathbf i - (3 + 2)\mathbf j + (0 - 4)\mathbf k = (6;\ -5;\ -4).$$

Tekshirish: $(6, -5, -4)\cdot(1, 2, -1) = 6 - 10 + 4 = 0$ ✓, $\ (6, -5, -4)\cdot(2, 0, 3) = 12 - 12 = 0$ ✓

---

## Parallelogramm yuzi

Yuqoridagi misolda:
$$S = |\mathbf a \times \mathbf b| = \sqrt{36 + 25 + 16} = \sqrt{77}.$$

Uchburchak yuzi: $\dfrac{\sqrt{77}}{2}$.

---

## Uchburchak yuzi: tekislikda

Uchlari $A(x_1, y_1)$, $B(x_2, y_2)$, $C(x_3, y_3)$:
$$S = \frac12\left|\begin{vmatrix} x_2 - x_1 & y_2 - y_1\\ x_3 - x_1 & y_3 - y_1\end{vmatrix}\right|.$$

:::misol
$A(0;\ -2)$, $B(-2;\ -1)$, $C(0;\ 3)$: $\overrightarrow{AB} = (-2;\ 1)$, $\overrightarrow{AC} = (0;\ 5)$,
$$S = \tfrac12|-10 - 0| = 5.$$
:::

---

## Yuza: vektorlarning kombinatsiyasi

$|\mathbf a| = 2$, $|\mathbf b| = 3$, $\varphi = \frac{\pi}{3}$. $\mathbf p = 2\mathbf a + \mathbf b$, $\mathbf q = \mathbf a - 2\mathbf b$ ga qurilgan parallelogramm:

$$\mathbf p \times \mathbf q = 2\,\mathbf a \times \mathbf a - 4\,\mathbf a \times \mathbf b + \mathbf b \times \mathbf a - 2\,\mathbf b \times \mathbf b = -5\,\mathbf a \times \mathbf b.$$

$|\mathbf a \times \mathbf b| = 2\cdot 3\cdot\frac{\sqrt3}{2} = 3\sqrt3$, demak $S = 15\sqrt3$.

---

## Tatbiq: tekislikka normal

Uch nuqta $A, B, C$ orqali o'tuvchi tekislikning normal vektori:
$$\mathbf n = \overrightarrow{AB} \times \overrightarrow{AC}.$$

:::misol
$A(1;\ 1;\ 1)$, $B(2;\ 3;\ 1)$, $C(1;\ 2;\ 3)$: $\overrightarrow{AB} = (1;\ 2;\ 0)$, $\overrightarrow{AC} = (0;\ 1;\ 2)$,
$$\mathbf n = (4 - 0;\ -(2 - 0);\ 1 - 0) = (4;\ -2;\ 1).$$
Tekislik: $4x - 2y + z - 3 = 0$ (16-mavzu).
:::

---

## Tatbiq: nuqtadan to'g'ri chiziqqacha masofa

$M$ nuqtadan $A$ orqali $\mathbf s$ yo'nalishda o'tuvchi to'g'ri chiziqqacha masofa — parallelogramm balandligi:
$$d = \frac{|\overrightarrow{AM} \times \mathbf s|}{|\mathbf s|}.$$

:::misol
$M(0;\ 1;\ 2)$, chiziq — $Ox$ o'qi ($A = O$, $\mathbf s = \mathbf i$): $\overrightarrow{AM} \times \mathbf i = (0;\ 2;\ -1)$, $\ d = \sqrt5$.
:::

Tekshirish: $Ox$ o'qigacha masofa $\sqrt{y^2 + z^2} = \sqrt{1 + 4} = \sqrt5$ ✓

---

## Mexanik ma'no: moment

$A$ nuqtaga qo'yilgan $\mathbf F$ kuchning $O$ nuqtaga nisbatan **momenti**:
$$\mathbf M = \overrightarrow{OA} \times \mathbf F.$$

- $|\mathbf M|$ — kuch × yelka
- yo'nalishi — aylanish o'qi

Shuningdek: Lorens kuchi $\mathbf F = q\,\mathbf v \times \mathbf B$, burchak tezlik $\mathbf v = \boldsymbol\omega \times \mathbf r$.

---

## Aralash ko'paytma

:::tarif
$$\mathbf a\mathbf b\mathbf c = (\mathbf a \times \mathbf b)\cdot\mathbf c$$
— **aralash ko'paytma** (natija — son).
:::

:::teorema Koordinatalarda
$$\mathbf a\mathbf b\mathbf c = \begin{vmatrix} a_x & a_y & a_z\\ b_x & b_y & b_z\\ c_x & c_y & c_z\end{vmatrix}.$$
:::

---

## Geometrik ma'no

$$|\mathbf a\mathbf b\mathbf c| = V_{\text{parallelepiped}}.$$

$|\mathbf a \times \mathbf b|$ — asos yuzi, $\text{pr}_{\mathbf a\times\mathbf b}\mathbf c$ — balandlik.

- $\mathbf a\mathbf b\mathbf c > 0$ — o'ng uchlik
- $\mathbf a\mathbf b\mathbf c < 0$ — chap uchlik
- Tetraedr hajmi: $V = \frac16|\mathbf a\mathbf b\mathbf c|$

---

## Aralash ko'paytma xossalari

:::xossa
1. Doiraviy almashtirishda o'zgarmaydi: $\mathbf a\mathbf b\mathbf c = \mathbf b\mathbf c\mathbf a = \mathbf c\mathbf a\mathbf b$
2. Ikki ko'paytuvchi o'rni almashsa — ishora o'zgaradi: $\mathbf b\mathbf a\mathbf c = -\mathbf a\mathbf b\mathbf c$
3. $(\mathbf a \times \mathbf b)\cdot\mathbf c = \mathbf a\cdot(\mathbf b \times \mathbf c)$
:::

Bular determinant xossalaridan (satrlar almashinuvi) kelib chiqadi.

## Komplanarlik sharti

:::teorema
$$\mathbf a, \mathbf b, \mathbf c \text{ komplanar} \iff \mathbf a\mathbf b\mathbf c = 0.$$
:::

Ma'nosi: parallelepiped «yassilanib» qolgan — hajm nol. Algebraik: uch vektor chiziqli bog'liq $\iff$ determinant nol (6-mavzu).

---

## Komplanarlik: misol

Nuqtalar $A(1;\ 2;\ -1)$, $B(0;\ 1;\ 5)$, $C(-1;\ 2;\ 1)$, $D(2;\ 1;\ 3)$ bitta tekislikdami?

$\overrightarrow{AB} = (-1;\ -1;\ 6)$, $\overrightarrow{AC} = (-2;\ 0;\ 2)$, $\overrightarrow{AD} = (1;\ -1;\ 4)$:
$$\begin{vmatrix} -1 & -1 & 6\\ -2 & 0 & 2\\ 1 & -1 & 4\end{vmatrix} = 0 - 2 + 12 - 0 - 8 - 2 = 0.$$

Ha, to'rttala nuqta bitta tekislikda.

---

## Mashq: komplanarlik

:::savol
$\mathbf a = (1;\ 0;\ 0)$, $\mathbf b = (0;\ 2;\ 0)$, $\mathbf c = (1;\ 1;\ k)$ qaysi $k$ da komplanar?
+ $k = 0$
- $k = 1$
- $k = 2$
- hech qanday $k$ da
> $\mathbf a\mathbf b\mathbf c = 2k = 0$.
:::

---

## Piramida hajmi: misol

$A(1;\ 1;\ 1)$, $B(3;\ 1;\ 1)$, $C(1;\ 4;\ 1)$, $D(1;\ 1;\ 5)$:
$$\overrightarrow{AB} = (2;\ 0;\ 0),\ \overrightarrow{AC} = (0;\ 3;\ 0),\ \overrightarrow{AD} = (0;\ 0;\ 4).$$
$$\overrightarrow{AB}\,\overrightarrow{AC}\,\overrightarrow{AD} = 24,\qquad V_{\text{piramida}} = \frac{24}{6} = 4.$$

---

## Piramida balandligi

Hajm va asos yuzini bilsak:
$$V = \frac13 S_{\text{asos}}\cdot h \Rightarrow h = \frac{3V}{S_{\text{asos}}}.$$

Yuqoridagi misolda: $S_{ABC} = \frac12|\overrightarrow{AB} \times \overrightarrow{AC}| = \frac12\cdot 6 = 3$,
$$h = \frac{3\cdot 4}{3} = 4\ \text{— } D \text{ dan } ABC \text{ tekisligigacha masofa.}$$

---

## Qo'sh vektor ko'paytma

:::teorema
$$\mathbf a \times (\mathbf b \times \mathbf c) = \mathbf b(\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf c) - \mathbf c(\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b).$$
:::

Eslab qolish: «**BAC − CAB**».

Natija: $\mathbf a \times (\mathbf b \times \mathbf c)$ — $\mathbf b$ va $\mathbf c$ tekisligida yotadi.

---

## Uchta ko'paytmani solishtirish

| | Natija | Formula | Geometrik ma'no |
|---|---|---|---|
| $\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b$ | son | $\sum a_ib_i$ | proyeksiya, burchak |
| $\mathbf a \times \mathbf b$ | vektor | determinant ($\mathbf i, \mathbf j, \mathbf k$ bilan) | yuza, normal |
| $\mathbf a\mathbf b\mathbf c$ | son | $3\times3$ determinant | hajm |

---

## Tipik xatolar

- Vektor ko'paytmada $\mathbf j$ oldidagi minusni unutish.
- $\mathbf a \times \mathbf b = \mathbf b \times \mathbf a$ deb yozish.
- Hajmni topishda modulni olmaslik.
- Tetraedr hajmida $\frac16$ ni unutish ($\frac13$ emas!).

---

## Xulosa

- $|\mathbf a \times \mathbf b|$ — parallelogramm yuzi; $\mathbf a \times \mathbf b \perp \mathbf a, \mathbf b$.
- $\mathbf a \times \mathbf b = -\mathbf b \times \mathbf a$; kollinear $\iff \mathbf a \times \mathbf b = \mathbf 0$.
- $\mathbf a\mathbf b\mathbf c$ — $3\times3$ determinant, $|\mathbf a\mathbf b\mathbf c|$ — parallelepiped hajmi.
- Komplanar $\iff \mathbf a\mathbf b\mathbf c = 0$; tetraedr: $V = \frac16|\mathbf a\mathbf b\mathbf c|$.
