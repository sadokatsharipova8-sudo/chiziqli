## Paradoks

Bir pallali giperboloid — egri sirt. Ammo uni **to'g'ri** tayoqlardan yasash mumkin!

Tajriba: ikki halqa orasiga ko'p tayoqlarni bir xil qiyalikda bog'lang — giperboloid hosil bo'ladi. Halqalarni bursangiz, sirt «bo'yni» toraydi.

## Nega ellipsoid chiziqli emas?

Ellipsoid **qavariq**: to'g'ri chiziq u bilan ko'pi bilan 2 nuqtada kesishadi (kvadrat tenglama!). Butun to'g'ri chiziq sirtda yotishi mumkin emas.

Elliptik paraboloid va ikki pallali giperboloid ham xuddi shunday.

---

## Silindr va konus

- **Silindr** $\frac{x^2}{a^2} + \frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1$: har bir nuqtadan $Oz$ ga parallel to'g'ri chiziq $\{(x_0, y_0, z)\}$.
- **Konus** $x^2 + y^2 = z^2$: har bir nuqta $M$ dan $OM$ to'g'ri chizig'i — $tM$ ham konusda: $t^2(x^2 + y^2) = t^2z^2$ ✓

---

## Bir pallali giperboloid: faktorlash

$$\frac{x^2}{a^2} + \frac{y^2}{b^2} - \frac{z^2}{c^2} = 1 \iff \frac{x^2}{a^2} - \frac{z^2}{c^2} = 1 - \frac{y^2}{b^2},$$
$$\left(\frac xa - \frac zc\right)\left(\frac xa + \frac zc\right) = \left(1 - \frac yb\right)\left(1 + \frac yb\right).$$

---

## 1-oila

Har bir $k$ uchun:
$$\frac xa - \frac zc = k\left(1 - \frac yb\right),\qquad k\left(\frac xa + \frac zc\right) = 1 + \frac yb.$$

Ikki tekislik kesishmasi — to'g'ri chiziq. Ikkala tenglamani ko'paytirsak — giperboloid tenglamasi. Demak chiziq sirtda yotadi.

---

## 2-oila

Har bir $m$ uchun:
$$\frac xa - \frac zc = m\left(1 + \frac yb\right),\qquad m\left(\frac xa + \frac zc\right) = 1 - \frac yb.$$

(Omillarni boshqacha juftlash.) Har bir nuqtadan har bir oiladan **bittadan** yasovchi o'tadi.

---

## Giperbolik paraboloid

$$\frac{x^2}{p} - \frac{y^2}{q} = 2z \iff \left(\frac{x}{\sqrt p} - \frac{y}{\sqrt q}\right)\left(\frac{x}{\sqrt p} + \frac{y}{\sqrt q}\right) = 2z.$$

1-oila: $\frac{x}{\sqrt p} - \frac{y}{\sqrt q} = 2k$, $\ k\left(\frac{x}{\sqrt p} + \frac{y}{\sqrt q}\right) = z$.

2-oila — omillar o'rnini almashtirib.

---

## Eng sodda misol: $z = xy$

$z = xy$ — giperbolik paraboloid ($45^\circ$ burilgan).

Har bir nuqta $(x_0;\ y_0;\ x_0y_0)$ dan:
- $x = x_0$, $z = x_0y$ — 1-oila (to'g'ri chiziq!);
- $y = y_0$, $z = xy_0$ — 2-oila.

Yasovchilar koordinata tekisliklariga parallel — «to'r».

## Yasovchini topish usuli

$M_0(x_0;\ y_0;\ z_0)$ sirtda. Yasovchi: $x = x_0 + lt$, $y = y_0 + mt$, $z = z_0 + nt$.

Sirt tenglamasiga qo'yamiz → $t$ ga nisbatan ko'phad. To'g'ri chiziq **butunlay** sirtda $\iff$ ko'phad **aynan nol** $\iff$ $t$ va $t^2$ oldidagi koeffitsiyentlar nol.

---

## Misol: tenglama

$$\frac{x^2}{9} + \frac{y^2}{1} - \frac{z^2}{25} = 1,\qquad M(3;\ 0;\ 0).$$

$x = 3 + lt$, $y = mt$, $z = nt$:
$$\frac{(3 + lt)^2}{9} + m^2t^2 - \frac{n^2t^2}{25} = 1.$$

---

## Misol: koeffitsiyentlar

Ochamiz: $1 + \frac{2l}{3}t + \left(\frac{l^2}{9} + m^2 - \frac{n^2}{25}\right)t^2 = 1$.

- $t$: $\frac{2l}{3} = 0 \Rightarrow l = 0$;
- $t^2$: $m^2 - \frac{n^2}{25} = 0 \Rightarrow n = \pm5m$.

---

## Misol: javob

$m = 1$:
$$\mathbf s_1 = (0;\ 1;\ 5),\qquad \mathbf s_2 = (0;\ 1;\ -5).$$

Ikki yasovchi:
$$\frac{x - 3}{0} = \frac y1 = \frac z5,\qquad \frac{x - 3}{0} = \frac y1 = \frac{z}{-5}.$$

Mashqlar bo'limidagi «Giperboloid yasovchisi» masalalari shunday.

---

## Tekshirish

$\mathbf s_1$: $x = 3$, $y = t$, $z = 5t$:
$$\frac{9}{9} + t^2 - \frac{25t^2}{25} = 1 + t^2 - t^2 = 1\ ✓$$

Har qanday $t$ da bajariladi — to'g'ri chiziq butunlay sirtda!

## Shuxov minoralari

V. G. Shuxov (1853–1939) — giperboloid konstruksiyalarni 1896-yilda patentlagan.

- To'g'ri po'lat balkalardan egri sirt — **arzon**, **yengil**, **mustahkam**.
- Shamol yuki kam (to'r konstruksiya).
- Shabolovka teleminorasi (Moskva, 1922) — bir necha giperboloid seksiyalar.

---

## Konus yasovchisi: misol

$x^2 + y^2 - z^2 = 0$, $M(3;\ 4;\ 5)$ (tekshirish: $9 + 16 - 25 = 0$ ✓).

Yasovchi — $OM$ to'g'ri chizig'i: $\frac x3 = \frac y4 = \frac z5$. Har bir nuqta $t(3, 4, 5)$: $9t^2 + 16t^2 - 25t^2 = 0$ ✓

## Tipik xatolar

- Ellipsoidda yasovchi izlash.
- Faqat $t$ ning birinchi darajasini nolga tenglab, $t^2$ ni unutish.
- Ikkala oiladan faqat bittasini topish.
- Yasovchining sirtda butunlay yotishini tekshirmaslik.

---

## Xulosa

- Chiziqli sirtlar: silindr, konus, bir pallali giperboloid, giperbolik paraboloid.
- Giperboloid va giperbolik paraboloid — **ikki oila** yasovchilar.
- Topish: parametrik chiziqni qo'yib, $t$, $t^2$ koeffitsiyentlarini nolga tenglash.
- Tatbiq: to'g'ri balkalardan egri konstruksiyalar.
