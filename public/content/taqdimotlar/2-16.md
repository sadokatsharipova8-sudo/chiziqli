## Kesimlar usuli

Sirt shaklini aniqlash uchun uni tekisliklar bilan kesamiz:
- $z = h$ (gorizontal kesimlar);
- $x = 0$, $y = 0$ (koordinata tekisliklari).

Har bir kesim — ikkinchi tartibli **chiziq** (10–15-mavzular). Kesimlardan fazoviy tasvirni «yig'amiz».

---

## Sfera

$$(x - a)^2 + (y - b)^2 + (z - c)^2 = R^2.$$

:::misol
$x^2 + y^2 + z^2 - 2x + 4y - 6z - 2 = 0$:
$$(x - 1)^2 + (y + 2)^2 + (z - 3)^2 = 2 + 1 + 4 + 9 = 16.$$
Markaz $(1;\ -2;\ 3)$, $R = 4$.
:::

---

## Mashq: sfera

:::savol
$x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 9$?
- Sfera, $R = 9$
+ Sfera, $R = 3$
- Ellipsoid
- Konus
:::

## Ellipsoid

$$\frac{x^2}{a^2} + \frac{y^2}{b^2} + \frac{z^2}{c^2} = 1.$$

$a, b, c$ — **yarim o'qlar**. $a = b$ — aylanma ellipsoid; $a = b = c$ — sfera.

---

## Ellipsoid kesimlari

$z = h$:
$$\frac{x^2}{a^2} + \frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1 - \frac{h^2}{c^2}.$$

- $|h| < c$ — ellips (h o'sgan sari kichrayadi);
- $|h| = c$ — nuqta;
- $|h| > c$ — bo'sh.

Ellipsoid — yopiq, chegaralangan sirt.

---

## Ellipsoid: misol

$32x^2 + 8y^2 + 128z^2 = 128$ ni 128 ga bo'lamiz:
$$\frac{x^2}{4} + \frac{y^2}{16} + \frac{z^2}{1} = 1.$$

$a = 2$, $b = 4$, $c = 1$. Mashqlar bo'limidagi «Ellipsoid yarim o'qlari» masalalari shunday.

---

## Bir pallali giperboloid

$$\frac{x^2}{a^2} + \frac{y^2}{b^2} - \frac{z^2}{c^2} = 1.$$

Kesimlar:
- $z = h$: ellipslar, **har qanday** $h$ da; eng kichigi — $z = 0$ da (bo'yin ellipsi);
- $x = 0$, $y = 0$: giperbolalar.

Shakli — «g'altak», sovitish minorasi.

---

## Ikki pallali giperboloid

$$\frac{x^2}{a^2} + \frac{y^2}{b^2} - \frac{z^2}{c^2} = -1.$$

- $|z| < c$: nuqtalar yo'q — ikki alohida **palla**;
- $|z| > c$: ellipslar;
- $x = 0$, $y = 0$: giperbolalar.

---

## Ishoralar qoidasi

Kanonik ko'rinishda o'ng tomon $= 1$:
- minus yo'q — ellipsoid;
- **bitta** minus — bir pallali giperboloid;
- **ikkita** minus — ikki pallali giperboloid;
- uchta minus — mavhum ellipsoid.

Minus ishorali o'zgaruvchi — giperboloid **o'qi**.

---

:::savol
$x^2 - y^2 - z^2 = 1$?
- Bir pallali giperboloid
+ Ikki pallali giperboloid
- Konus
- Giperbolik silindr
> Ikkita minus; o'qi $Ox$.
:::

:::savol

- Ikki pallali giperboloid

> $\frac{x^2}{25} + \frac{y^2}{36} - \frac{z^2}{1} = 1$.

## Ikkinchi tartibli konus

$$\frac{x^2}{a^2} + \frac{y^2}{b^2} - \frac{z^2}{c^2} = 0.$$

- Uchi — $O$;
- $z = h$ — ellipslar, $h$ ga proporsional;
- $x = 0$: $\frac{y^2}{b^2} = \frac{z^2}{c^2}$ — ikki kesishuvchi to'g'ri chiziq.

Ikkala giperboloidning **asimptotik konusi**.

---

## Konus va konus kesimlari

Konusni tekislik bilan kesish:
- o'qqa perpendikulyar — aylana/ellips;
- yasovchiga parallel — parabola;
- ikkala pallani — giperbola;
- uchdan o'tsa — aynigan holatlar.

Mana nega ellips, parabola, giperbola **konus kesimlari** deyiladi.

---

## Elliptik paraboloid

$$\frac{x^2}{p} + \frac{y^2}{q} = 2z\qquad(p, q > 0).$$

- $z = h > 0$: ellipslar; $z = 0$: nuqta; $z < 0$: bo'sh;
- $x = 0$, $y = 0$: yuqoriga ochilgan parabolalar.

«Kosa». Aylanma paraboloid ($p = q$) — antenna, aylanayotgan idishdagi suv sirti.

---

## Giperbolik paraboloid

$$\frac{x^2}{p} - \frac{y^2}{q} = 2z\qquad(p, q > 0).$$

- $z = h$: giperbolalar ($h = 0$ — kesishuvchi to'g'ri chiziqlar);
- $y = 0$: yuqoriga, $x = 0$: pastga ochilgan parabolalar.

«**Egar**». $O$ — egar nuqtasi.

---

## Egar: misol

$z = 2xy$. $Oz$ atrofida $45^\circ$ burish:
$$x = \frac{X - Y}{\sqrt2},\quad y = \frac{X + Y}{\sqrt2} \Rightarrow 2xy = X^2 - Y^2.$$

$z = X^2 - Y^2$ — giperbolik paraboloid.

---

## Mashq: paraboloid

:::savol
$z = x^2 - y^2$?
- Elliptik paraboloid
+ Giperbolik paraboloid
- Konus
- Giperboloid
:::

## Silindrlar

Tenglamada bitta o'zgaruvchi qatnashmasa — **silindr** (yasovchilari shu o'qqa parallel):

| Tenglama | Sirt |
|---|---|
| $\frac{x^2}{a^2} + \frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1$ | elliptik silindr |
| $\frac{x^2}{a^2} - \frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1$ | giperbolik silindr |
| $y^2 = 2px$ | parabolik silindr |

---

## Aylanma sirtlar

Tekislikdagi chiziqni o'q atrofida aylantirsak:
- aylana → sfera;
- ellips → aylanma ellipsoid;
- giperbola → giperboloid (qaysi o'q atrofida aylantirishga qarab — bir yoki ikki pallali);
- parabola → aylanma paraboloid.

Masalan, $x^2 - z^2 = 1$ ni $Oz$ atrofida: $x^2 + y^2 - z^2 = 1$.

---

## Tatbiq: Yer shakli

Yer — aylanma ellipsoid (WGS-84 standarti):
- ekvatorial yarim o'q $a \approx 6378{,}137$ km;
- qutb yarim o'qi $c \approx 6356{,}752$ km.

GPS koordinatalari shu ellipsoidga nisbatan hisoblanadi; farq 21 km — e'tiborsiz qoldirsak, xatolar yuzlab metr bo'ladi.

---

## Tipik xatolar

- Konusni giperboloid bilan chalkashtirish (o'ng tomon 0!).
- Fazodagi $x^2 + y^2 = 4$ ni aylana deb atash.
- Paraboloidda chiziqli $z$ ni kvadrat deb o'qish.
- Yarim o'qni maxrajning o'zi deb olish (ildiz kerak).
