# Ikkinchi tartibli chiziqlarning umumiy tenglamasini kanonik ko'rinishga keltirish (markaziy hol)

**2-semestr, 14-mavzu** · Chiziqli algebra va analitik geometriya

$\delta \ne 0$ · Markazga ko'chirish · Xos sonlar bilan burish · Kanonik ko'rinish · Invariantlar bilan tekshirish

---

1. Maqsad va g'oya
2. Algoritm (4 qadam)
3. 1-misol: ellips (ko'chirishsiz)
4. 2-misol: giperbola (to'liq)

## Maqsad

Umumiy tenglamani harakatlar (ko'chirish + burish) bilan kanonik ko'rinishga keltirish:
$$\lambda_1X^2 + \lambda_2Y^2 + c' = 0.$$

Bu mavzu — $\delta \ne 0$ (markaziy chiziqlar: ellips va giperbola) holi.

---

## G'oya

1. **Ko'chirish** chiziqli hadlarni yo'qotadi — buning uchun boshni **markazga** qo'yamiz.
2. **Burish** $xy$ hadni yo'qotadi — o'qlarni bosh qism matritsasining **xos vektorlari** bo'ylab yo'naltiramiz (1-semestr 18-mavzu).

---

## 1-qadam: markaz

$$\begin{cases} a_{11}x_0 + a_{12}y_0 + a_{13} = 0\\ a_{12}x_0 + a_{22}y_0 + a_{23} = 0\end{cases}$$

$\delta \ne 0$ — yagona yechim.

---

## 2-qadam: ko'chirish

$x = x' + x_0$, $y = y' + y_0$:
$$a_{11}x'^2 + 2a_{12}x'y' + a_{22}y'^2 + c' = 0,$$
$$c' = a_{13}x_0 + a_{23}y_0 + a_{33} = \frac{\Delta}{\delta}.$$

---

## 3-qadam: burish

$A = \begin{pmatrix} a_{11} & a_{12}\\ a_{12} & a_{22}\end{pmatrix}$ ning xos sonlari:
$$\lambda^2 - I_1\lambda + \delta = 0.$$

Ortonormal xos vektorlar $\mathbf e_1$, $\mathbf e_2$ — yangi o'qlar. Natija:
$$\lambda_1X^2 + \lambda_2Y^2 + c' = 0.$$

---

## 4-qadam: kanonik ko'rinish

$-c'$ ga bo'lib:
$$\frac{X^2}{-c'/\lambda_1} + \frac{Y^2}{-c'/\lambda_2} = 1.$$

Maxrajlar ishorasiga qarab: ellips, giperbola yoki mavhum ellips.

---

## 1-misol: tenglama

$$5x^2 + 4xy + 8y^2 - 36 = 0.$$

Markaz: $5x + 2y = 0$, $2x + 8y = 0$ $\Rightarrow$ $(0;\ 0)$ — chiziqli hadlar allaqachon yo'q.

---

## 1-misol: xos sonlar

$$\begin{vmatrix} 5 - \lambda & 2\\ 2 & 8 - \lambda\end{vmatrix} = \lambda^2 - 13\lambda + 36 = 0 \Rightarrow \lambda_1 = 4,\ \lambda_2 = 9.$$

Tekshirish: $4 + 9 = 13 = I_1$, $36 = \delta$ ✓

---

## 1-misol: xos vektorlar

- $\lambda_1 = 4$: $x + 2y = 0 \Rightarrow (2;\ -1)$, normallangan $\frac{1}{\sqrt5}(2;\ -1)$;
- $\lambda_2 = 9$: $-4x + 2y = 0 \Rightarrow (1;\ 2)$, normallangan $\frac{1}{\sqrt5}(1;\ 2)$.

Ortogonal: $2 - 2 = 0$ ✓

---

## 1-misol: natija

Burish: $x = \frac{2X + Y}{\sqrt5}$, $y = \frac{-X + 2Y}{\sqrt5}$:
$$4X^2 + 9Y^2 = 36 \Rightarrow \frac{X^2}{9} + \frac{Y^2}{4} = 1.$$

**Ellips**: $a = 3$ ($(2;\ -1)$ yo'nalishida), $b = 2$.

---

## 2-misol: tenglama va markaz

$$3x^2 + 10xy + 3y^2 - 2x - 14y - 13 = 0.$$

Markaz: $\begin{cases} 3x + 5y - 1 = 0\\ 5x + 3y - 7 = 0\end{cases} \Rightarrow (2;\ -1)$.

---

## 2-misol: ko'chirish

$$c' = a_{13}x_0 + a_{23}y_0 + a_{33} = (-1)(2) + (-7)(-1) - 13 = -8.$$

Invariant bilan: $\Delta = 128$, $\delta = -16$, $\frac\Delta\delta = -8$ ✓
$$3x'^2 + 10x'y' + 3y'^2 - 8 = 0.$$

---

## 2-misol: burish

$\lambda^2 - 6\lambda - 16 = 0$: $\lambda_1 = 8$, $\lambda_2 = -2$.

Xos vektorlar: $\lambda = 8$ — $(1;\ 1)$, $\lambda = -2$ — $(1;\ -1)$ — burish $45^\circ$.

---

## 2-misol: natija

$$8X^2 - 2Y^2 - 8 = 0 \Rightarrow X^2 - \frac{Y^2}{4} = 1.$$

**Giperbola**: markaz $(2;\ -1)$, $a = 1$, $b = 2$, haqiqiy o'q $(1;\ 1)$ yo'nalishida.

---

## Mashq: ozod had

:::savol

+ $-8$
- $8$

:::

## 3-misol: tenglama

$$8x^2 + 12xy - y^2 + 32x + 24y + 44 = 0.$$

Markaz: $\begin{cases} 8x + 6y + 16 = 0\\ 6x - y + 12 = 0\end{cases} \Rightarrow (-2;\ 0)$.

$c' = 16\cdot(-2) + 12\cdot 0 + 44 = 12$.

---

## 3-misol: natija

$\lambda^2 - 7\lambda - 44 = 0$: $\lambda_1 = -4$, $\lambda_2 = 11$.
$$-4X^2 + 11Y^2 + 12 = 0 \Rightarrow \frac{X^2}{3} - \frac{Y^2}{12/11} = 1.$$

**Giperbola.** Mashqlar bo'limidagi «Markaziy chiziqni kanonik ko'rinishga keltirish» masalalari shunday.

---

## Burish burchagi

Xos vektorlar o'rniga burchak formulasi:
$$\operatorname{ctg}2\alpha = \frac{a_{11} - a_{22}}{2a_{12}}.$$

- $a_{11} = a_{22}$ — $\alpha = 45^\circ$ (2-misol);
- 1-misol: $\operatorname{ctg}2\alpha = \frac{5 - 8}{4} = -\frac34$.

Xos vektorlar usuli odatda soddaroq va xatolar kamroq.

---

## Natijani tahlil qilish

$\lambda_1X^2 + \lambda_2Y^2 + c' = 0$:

| $\lambda_1$, $\lambda_2$ | $c'$ | Chiziq |
|---|---|---|
| bir ishorali | qarama-qarshi ishorali | haqiqiy ellips |
| bir ishorali | bir xil ishorali | mavhum ellips |
| bir ishorali | 0 | nuqta |
| turli ishorali | $\ne 0$ | giperbola |
| turli ishorali | 0 | kesishuvchi chiziqlar |

## Tekshirish usullari

:::xossa
- $\lambda_1 + \lambda_2 = I_1$, $\lambda_1\lambda_2 = \delta$;
- $c' = \frac\Delta\delta$;
- xos vektorlar ortogonal;
- topilgan markazni dastlabki tenglamaga qo'yib, $F(x_0, y_0) = c'$ ekanini tekshirish.
:::

---

## Diqqat: normallash

:::diqqat
Burishda xos vektorlarni **normallash** shart. Aks holda almashtirish ortogonal bo'lmaydi va yarim o'qlar noto'g'ri chiqadi.

$(2;\ -1)$ emas, $\frac{1}{\sqrt5}(2;\ -1)$!
:::

## Eski koordinatalarga qaytish

Kanonik sistemadagi elementlarni (fokus, uchlar) dastlabki koordinatalarda topish:
$$\begin{pmatrix} x\\ y\end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} x_0\\ y_0\end{pmatrix} + T\begin{pmatrix} X\\ Y\end{pmatrix}.$$

1-misol: ellips uchi $X = 3$, $Y = 0$ $\Rightarrow$ $(x;\ y) = 3\cdot\frac{1}{\sqrt5}(2;\ -1) = \left(\frac{6}{\sqrt5};\ -\frac{3}{\sqrt5}\right)$.

---

## Tatbiq: orbitalar

Teleskop sun'iy yo'ldosh yoki asteroidning 5+ pozitsiyasini o'lchaydi. Ularga umumiy tenglama moslashtiriladi, keyin:
1. markaz — orbita markazi;
2. xos vektorlar — orbita o'qlari;
3. kanonik ko'rinish — yarim o'qlar va ekssentrisitet.

---

## Tatbiq: kuchlanish ellipsi

Materialshunoslikda nuqtadagi kuchlanish holati $\begin{pmatrix} \sigma_x & \tau\\ \tau & \sigma_y\end{pmatrix}$ simmetrik matritsa bilan beriladi. Xos sonlar — **bosh kuchlanishlar**, xos vektorlar — bosh yo'nalishlar. Detal aynan shu yo'nalishlarda sinadi.

---

## Tipik xatolar

- Avval burib, keyin markazni eski koordinatalarda izlash (aralashtirish).
- $c'$ ni $a_{33}$ deb qoldirish.
- Xos sonlar tartibi va o'qlarni chalkashtirish.
- Natijani $-c'$ ga bo'lishda ishorani yo'qotish.

---

## Xulosa

Markaziy holat ($\delta \ne 0$):
1. markaz — chiziqli sistema;
2. ko'chirish: $c' = \frac\Delta\delta$;
3. burish: $\lambda^2 - I_1\lambda + \delta = 0$, ortonormal xos vektorlar;
4. $\lambda_1X^2 + \lambda_2Y^2 + c' = 0$ — ellips yoki giperbola.

---
