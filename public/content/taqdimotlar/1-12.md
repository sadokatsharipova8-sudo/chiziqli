## Muammo

Ortonormal bazisda hisoblash juda oson (11-mavzu). Lekin bizga odatda **istalgan** bazis berilgan bo'ladi.

Savol: istalgan bazisdan ortonormal bazis qanday yasaladi?

Javob — **Gram–Shmidt jarayoni**.

---

## Vektorga proyeksiya

$\mathbf a$ ning $\mathbf b$ yo'nalishidagi ortogonal proyeksiyasi:
$$\text{pr}_{\mathbf b}\mathbf a = \frac{(\mathbf a, \mathbf b)}{(\mathbf b, \mathbf b)}\,\mathbf b.$$

Qolgan qism $\mathbf a - \text{pr}_{\mathbf b}\mathbf a$ — $\mathbf b$ ga **perpendikulyar**:
$$\left(\mathbf a - \tfrac{(\mathbf a, \mathbf b)}{(\mathbf b, \mathbf b)}\mathbf b,\ \mathbf b\right) = (\mathbf a, \mathbf b) - (\mathbf a, \mathbf b) = 0.$$

---

## Proyeksiya: misol

$\mathbf a = (3, 4)$, $\mathbf b = (1, 0)$:
$$\text{pr}_{\mathbf b}\mathbf a = \frac{3}{1}(1, 0) = (3, 0),\qquad \mathbf a - \text{pr} = (0, 4) \perp \mathbf b.$$

Bu — Gram–Shmidtning bitta qadami!

---

## G'oya

1. Birinchi vektorni olamiz.
2. Ikkinchisidan birinchisi bo'yicha proyeksiyani ayiramiz.
3. Uchinchisidan oldingi **ikkalasi** bo'yicha proyeksiyalarni ayiramiz.
4. …

Har qadamda «yangi» vektorning faqat «yangi yo'nalishdagi» qismi qoladi.

---

## Gram–Shmidt formulalari

$$\mathbf b_1 = \mathbf a_1$$
$$\mathbf b_2 = \mathbf a_2 - \frac{(\mathbf a_2, \mathbf b_1)}{(\mathbf b_1, \mathbf b_1)}\mathbf b_1$$
$$\mathbf b_3 = \mathbf a_3 - \frac{(\mathbf a_3, \mathbf b_1)}{(\mathbf b_1, \mathbf b_1)}\mathbf b_1 - \frac{(\mathbf a_3, \mathbf b_2)}{(\mathbf b_2, \mathbf b_2)}\mathbf b_2$$
$$\mathbf b_k = \mathbf a_k - \sum_{i<k}\frac{(\mathbf a_k, \mathbf b_i)}{(\mathbf b_i, \mathbf b_i)}\mathbf b_i$$

So'ng: $\mathbf e_i = \mathbf b_i/|\mathbf b_i|$.

---

## Teorema

:::teorema
$\mathbf a_1, \dots, \mathbf a_k$ chiziqli erkli bo'lsa, Gram–Shmidt jarayoni ortogonal $\mathbf b_1, \dots, \mathbf b_k$ beradi, hammasi noldan farqli va har bir $j$ uchun
$$L(\mathbf b_1, \dots, \mathbf b_j) = L(\mathbf a_1, \dots, \mathbf a_j).$$
:::

Isbot — induksiya bilan: $\mathbf b_k$ barcha oldingi $\mathbf b_i$ ga ortogonal (proyeksiya xossasi).

---

## Misol: 1-qadam

$\mathbf a_1 = (1, 1, 0)$, $\mathbf a_2 = (2, 0, -1)$, $\mathbf a_3 = (1, -1, 1)$.

$$\mathbf b_1 = (1, 1, 0),\qquad (\mathbf b_1, \mathbf b_1) = 2.$$

---

## Misol: 2-qadam

$(\mathbf a_2, \mathbf b_1) = 2$:
$$\mathbf b_2 = (2, 0, -1) - \frac22(1, 1, 0) = (1, -1, -1).$$

Tekshirish: $(\mathbf b_1, \mathbf b_2) = 1 - 1 + 0 = 0$ ✓. $(\mathbf b_2, \mathbf b_2) = 3$.

---

## Misol: 3-qadam

$(\mathbf a_3, \mathbf b_1) = 0$, $(\mathbf a_3, \mathbf b_2) = 1 + 1 - 1 = 1$:
$$\mathbf b_3 = (1, -1, 1) - 0 - \frac13(1, -1, -1) = \left(\frac23, -\frac23, \frac43\right) \parallel (1, -1, 2).$$

Tekshirish: $(\mathbf b_1, \mathbf b_3) = 0$, $(\mathbf b_2, \mathbf b_3) = 1 + 1 - 2 = 0$ ✓

---

## Misol: normallash

$$\mathbf e_1 = \frac{1}{\sqrt2}(1, 1, 0),\quad \mathbf e_2 = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(1, -1, -1),\quad \mathbf e_3 = \frac{1}{\sqrt6}(1, -1, 2).$$

$\mathbb R^3$ ning ortonormal bazisi tayyor.

:::eslatma
Hisobni soddalashtirish uchun $\mathbf b_i$ ni istalgan noldan farqli songa ko'paytirish mumkin (yo'nalish o'zgarmaydi).
:::

---

## Mashq: proyeksiya koeffitsiyenti

:::savol
$\mathbf a_1 = (1, 1)$, $\mathbf a_2 = (1, 0)$. $\mathbf b_2$ = ?
+ $\left(\frac12, -\frac12\right)$
- $(1, 0)$
- $(0, 1)$
- $(1, -1)$
> $\mathbf b_2 = (1, 0) - \frac12(1, 1)$.
:::

## Agar $\mathbf b_k = \mathbf 0$ chiqsa?

Bu $\mathbf a_k$ oldingi vektorlar orqali ifodalanadi degani — sistema **chiziqli bog'liq**.

Gram–Shmidt chiziqli bog'liqlikni tekshirishning ham usuli: nol vektorni tashlab, davom etamiz — natija chiziqli qobiqning ortogonal bazisi.

---

## Natija

:::teorema
Har qanday chekli o'lchamli Yevklid fazosida **ortonormal bazis** mavjud.
:::

Isbot: istalgan bazisga Gram–Shmidtni qo'llab, normallaymiz.

Matritsa shaklida — **QR-yoyilma**: $A = QR$, $Q$ — ortonormal ustunli, $R$ — yuqori uchburchak.

## Ortogonal to'ldiruvchi

:::tarif
$$L^\perp = \{\mathbf x : (\mathbf x, \mathbf y) = 0\ \ \forall\mathbf y \in L\}.$$
:::

- $L^\perp$ — qism fazo.
- $\mathbb R^3$ da: to'g'ri chiziqning to'ldiruvchisi — unga perpendikulyar tekislik; tekislikniki — normal to'g'ri chiziq.

---

## Asosiy teorema

:::teorema
$$V = L \oplus L^\perp,\qquad \dim L + \dim L^\perp = \dim V,\qquad (L^\perp)^\perp = L.$$
:::

Isbot g'oyasi: $L$ ning ortonormal bazisini $V$ ning ortonormal bazisigacha to'ldiramiz (Gram–Shmidt); qo'shilgan vektorlar — $L^\perp$ bazisi.

---

## To'ldiruvchini topish

$L = L(\mathbf a_1, \dots, \mathbf a_k)$ bo'lsa:
$$\mathbf x \in L^\perp \iff (\mathbf x, \mathbf a_i) = 0,\ i = 1, \dots, k.$$

Bu — $\mathbf a_i$ lar satr bo'lgan **bir jinsli sistema**; $L^\perp$ — uning yechimlar fazosi (FYS).

---

## To'ldiruvchi: misol

$L = L\big((1, 1, 0)\big) \subset \mathbb R^3$:
$$x_1 + x_2 = 0.$$

FYS: $(-1, 1, 0)$, $(0, 0, 1)$. $L^\perp$ — tekislik, $\dim = 2$; $1 + 2 = 3$ ✓

---

## Mashq: o'lcham

:::savol
$\mathbb R^5$ da $\dim L = 2$. $\dim L^\perp$?
- 2
+ 3
- 5
- 7
:::

---

## Ortogonal proyeksiya

Har bir $\mathbf x$:
$$\mathbf x = \mathbf x_L + \mathbf x_\perp,\qquad \mathbf x_L \in L,\ \mathbf x_\perp \in L^\perp.$$

- $\mathbf x_L$ — ortogonal proyeksiya; ortonormal bazisda $\mathbf x_L = \sum(\mathbf x, \mathbf e_i)\mathbf e_i$.
- $\mathbf x_L$ — $L$ dagi $\mathbf x$ ga **eng yaqin** vektor.
- $|\mathbf x_\perp|$ — $\mathbf x$ dan $L$ gacha masofa.

---

## Proyeksiya: misol

$\mathbf x = (1, 2, 3)$, $L = L\big((1, 1, 0)\big)$:
$$\mathbf x_L = \frac{3}{2}(1, 1, 0),\qquad \mathbf x_\perp = \left(-\tfrac12, \tfrac12, 3\right).$$

Tekshirish: $(\mathbf x_\perp, (1, 1, 0)) = 0$ ✓. Masofa: $\sqrt{\frac14 + \frac14 + 9} = \sqrt{9{,}5}$.

---

## Nega eng yaqin?

$\mathbf y \in L$ — istalgan. $\mathbf x - \mathbf y = \mathbf x_\perp + (\mathbf x_L - \mathbf y)$, ikkinchi qism $L$ da:
$$|\mathbf x - \mathbf y|^2 = |\mathbf x_\perp|^2 + |\mathbf x_L - \mathbf y|^2 \ge |\mathbf x_\perp|^2.$$

(Pifagor teoremasi.) Tenglik faqat $\mathbf y = \mathbf x_L$ da.

---

## Eng kichik kvadratlar usuli

Tajriba nuqtalari $(t_i, y_i)$ ga eng yaxshi to'g'ri chiziq $y = a + bt$:
- $\mathbf y = (y_1, \dots, y_n)$ — o'lchashlar;
- $L = L\big((1, \dots, 1), (t_1, \dots, t_n)\big)$ — barcha mumkin bo'lgan to'g'ri chiziqlar;
- eng yaxshi chiziq — $\mathbf y$ ning $L$ ga **ortogonal proyeksiyasi**.

Regressiya, GPS, mashinali o'rganish — hammasi shu g'oyada.

## Mashq: masofa

:::savol
$\mathbb R^3$ da $\mathbf x = (1, 2, 3)$ dan $Oxy$ tekisligigacha (qism fazo $z = 0$) masofa?
- 1
- 2
+ 3
- $\sqrt5$
> $\mathbf x_L = (1, 2, 0)$, $\mathbf x_\perp = (0, 0, 3)$.
:::

## Tipik xatolar

- $\mathbf a_3$ ni $\mathbf a_1$, $\mathbf a_2$ ga proyeksiyalash ($\mathbf b_1$, $\mathbf b_2$ ga kerak).
- $(\mathbf b_i, \mathbf b_i)$ ga bo'lishni unutish.
- Normallashni har qadamda bajarib, ildizlar bilan chalkashish.
- $L^\perp$ ni topishda sistemani noto'g'ri tuzish.

---

## Xulosa

- $\text{pr}_{\mathbf b}\mathbf a = \frac{(\mathbf a, \mathbf b)}{(\mathbf b, \mathbf b)}\mathbf b$.
- Gram–Shmidt: proyeksiyalarni ketma-ket ayirish → ortogonal bazis.
- Har qanday Yevklid fazosida ortonormal bazis bor.
- $V = L \oplus L^\perp$; $\mathbf x_L$ — eng yaqin vektor.

---
