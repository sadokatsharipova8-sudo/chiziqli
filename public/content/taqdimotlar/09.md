## Nima yetishmaydi?

Chiziqli fazoda vektorlarni qo'shish va cho'zish mumkin, lekin:
- **uzunlik** yo'q,
- **burchak** yo'q,
- **perpendikulyarlik** yo'q.

Bularni bitta yangi amal — **skalyar ko'paytma** — beradi.

---

## Skalyar ko'paytma

:::tarif
Haqiqiy chiziqli fazoda har bir juftga $(\mathbf x, \mathbf y) \in \mathbb R$ mos qo'yilgan va:
1. $(\mathbf x, \mathbf y) = (\mathbf y, \mathbf x)$
2. $(\mathbf x + \mathbf y, \mathbf z) = (\mathbf x, \mathbf z) + (\mathbf y, \mathbf z)$
3. $(\lambda\mathbf x, \mathbf y) = \lambda(\mathbf x, \mathbf y)$
4. $(\mathbf x, \mathbf x) > 0$, agar $\mathbf x \ne \mathbf 0$

Bunday fazo — **Yevklid fazosi**.
:::

---

## Misollar

- $\mathbb R^n$: $(\mathbf x, \mathbf y) = x_1y_1 + \dots + x_ny_n$ — standart.
- $\mathbb R^2$: $(\mathbf x, \mathbf y) = 2x_1y_1 + 3x_2y_2$ — ham skalyar ko'paytma.
- $C[a, b]$: $(f, g) = \displaystyle\int_a^b f(t)g(t)\,dt$.
- Matritsalar: $(A, B) = \operatorname{tr}(A^TB)$.

Bitta fazoda turli skalyar ko'paytmalar bo'lishi mumkin.

---

## Uzunlik

:::tarif
$$|\mathbf x| = \sqrt{(\mathbf x, \mathbf x)}$$
— vektor **uzunligi** (normasi). $|\mathbf x| = 1$ — **normallangan** vektor.
:::

Normallash: $\mathbf x^0 = \dfrac{\mathbf x}{|\mathbf x|}$.

:::misol
$\mathbf x = (1, 2, 2)$: $|\mathbf x| = 3$, $\mathbf x^0 = \left(\frac13, \frac23, \frac23\right)$.
:::

---

## Koshi–Bunyakovskiy tengsizligi

:::teorema
$$|(\mathbf x, \mathbf y)| \le |\mathbf x|\cdot|\mathbf y|.$$
Tenglik $\iff$ $\mathbf x$ va $\mathbf y$ chiziqli bog'liq.
:::

$\mathbb R^n$ da:
$$(x_1y_1 + \dots + x_ny_n)^2 \le (x_1^2 + \dots + x_n^2)(y_1^2 + \dots + y_n^2).$$

---

## Isbot g'oyasi

Istalgan $t \in \mathbb R$ uchun:
$$0 \le (\mathbf x - t\mathbf y, \mathbf x - t\mathbf y) = |\mathbf x|^2 - 2t(\mathbf x, \mathbf y) + t^2|\mathbf y|^2.$$

$t$ ga nisbatan kvadrat uchhad doim nomanfiy $\Rightarrow$ diskriminant $\le 0$:
$$4(\mathbf x, \mathbf y)^2 - 4|\mathbf x|^2|\mathbf y|^2 \le 0.$$ ∎

---

## Natijalar

**Burchak** aniqlanadi (chunki $|\cos\varphi| \le 1$):
$$\cos\varphi = \frac{(\mathbf x, \mathbf y)}{|\mathbf x|\,|\mathbf y|}.$$

**Uchburchak tengsizligi:**
$$|\mathbf x + \mathbf y| \le |\mathbf x| + |\mathbf y|.$$

---

## Burchak: misol

$\mathbb R^4$ da $\mathbf x = (1, 1, 1, 1)$, $\mathbf y = (1, 0, 0, 1)$:
$$\cos\varphi = \frac{1 + 0 + 0 + 1}{2\cdot\sqrt2} = \frac{1}{\sqrt2},\qquad \varphi = 45^\circ.$$

4 o'lchovli fazoda ham burchak — xuddi tekislikdagidek.

---

## Ortogonallik

:::tarif
$(\mathbf x, \mathbf y) = 0$ bo'lsa, vektorlar **ortogonal**: $\mathbf x \perp \mathbf y$.
:::

**Pifagor teoremasi:** $\mathbf x \perp \mathbf y \Rightarrow |\mathbf x + \mathbf y|^2 = |\mathbf x|^2 + |\mathbf y|^2$.

:::savol
$\mathbf x = (1, 2, -1, 3)$, $\mathbf y = (2, 1, a, 0)$ qaysi $a$ da ortogonal?
- $a = -4$
+ $a = 4$
- $a = 0$
- $a = 2$
> $2 + 2 - a = 0$.
:::

---

## Ortogonal sistema erkli

:::teorema
Noldan farqli o'zaro ortogonal vektorlar chiziqli erkli.
:::

Isbot: $\sum\lambda_i\mathbf e_i = \mathbf 0$ ni $\mathbf e_k$ ga skalyar ko'paytiramiz:
$$\lambda_k(\mathbf e_k, \mathbf e_k) = 0 \Rightarrow \lambda_k = 0.$$ ∎

---

## Ortonormal bazis

:::tarif
$(\mathbf e_i, \mathbf e_j) = \delta_{ij} = \begin{cases} 1, & i = j\\ 0, & i \ne j\end{cases}$ — **ortonormal** bazis.
:::

Ortonormal bazisda hamma narsa oddiy:
- $(\mathbf x, \mathbf y) = x_1y_1 + \dots + x_ny_n$
- $|\mathbf x|^2 = x_1^2 + \dots + x_n^2$
- Koordinatalar: $x_i = (\mathbf x, \mathbf e_i)$ — **Furye koeffitsiyentlari**.

---

## Furye koeffitsiyentlari: misol

Ortonormal bazis: $\mathbf e_1 = \frac{1}{\sqrt2}(1, 1)$, $\mathbf e_2 = \frac{1}{\sqrt2}(1, -1)$.

$\mathbf x = (5, 1)$:
$$x_1 = (\mathbf x, \mathbf e_1) = \frac{6}{\sqrt2} = 3\sqrt2,\qquad x_2 = (\mathbf x, \mathbf e_2) = \frac{4}{\sqrt2} = 2\sqrt2.$$

Sistema yechish shart emas — faqat skalyar ko'paytma!

---

## Gram–Shmidt jarayoni

:::teorema
Har qanday chiziqli erkli $\mathbf a_1, \dots, \mathbf a_k$ dan **bir xil chiziqli qobiqli** ortogonal sistema qurish mumkin.
:::

Natija: har qanday chekli o'lchovli Yevklid fazosida **ortonormal bazis mavjud**.

---

## Gram–Shmidt formulalari

$$\mathbf b_1 = \mathbf a_1$$
$$\mathbf b_2 = \mathbf a_2 - \frac{(\mathbf a_2, \mathbf b_1)}{(\mathbf b_1, \mathbf b_1)}\mathbf b_1$$
$$\mathbf b_3 = \mathbf a_3 - \frac{(\mathbf a_3, \mathbf b_1)}{(\mathbf b_1, \mathbf b_1)}\mathbf b_1 - \frac{(\mathbf a_3, \mathbf b_2)}{(\mathbf b_2, \mathbf b_2)}\mathbf b_2$$

So'ng normallash: $\mathbf e_i = \mathbf b_i/|\mathbf b_i|$.

---

## G'oya: proyeksiyani ayirish

$\dfrac{(\mathbf a, \mathbf b)}{(\mathbf b, \mathbf b)}\mathbf b$ — $\mathbf a$ ning $\mathbf b$ yo'nalishidagi **ortogonal proyeksiyasi**.

Har qadamda $\mathbf a_k$ dan oldingi barcha $\mathbf b_i$ bo'yicha proyeksiyalarni ayiramiz — qolgan qism ularning hammasiga **perpendikulyar**.

---

## Gram–Shmidt: misol (1)

$\mathbf a_1 = (1, 1, 0)$, $\mathbf a_2 = (2, 0, -1)$, $\mathbf a_3 = (1, -1, 1)$.

**1-qadam:** $\mathbf b_1 = (1, 1, 0)$, $(\mathbf b_1, \mathbf b_1) = 2$.

**2-qadam:** $(\mathbf a_2, \mathbf b_1) = 2$:
$$\mathbf b_2 = (2, 0, -1) - \tfrac22(1, 1, 0) = (1, -1, -1),\quad (\mathbf b_2, \mathbf b_2) = 3.$$

---

## Gram–Shmidt: misol (2)

**3-qadam:** $(\mathbf a_3, \mathbf b_1) = 0$, $(\mathbf a_3, \mathbf b_2) = 1 + 1 - 1 = 1$:
$$\mathbf b_3 = (1, -1, 1) - 0 - \tfrac13(1, -1, -1) = \left(\tfrac23, -\tfrac23, \tfrac43\right) \parallel (1, -1, 2).$$

Tekshirish: $(\mathbf b_1, \mathbf b_2) = 0$, $(\mathbf b_1, \mathbf b_3) = 0$, $(\mathbf b_2, \mathbf b_3) = \frac23 + \frac23 - \frac43 = 0$ ✓

---

## Gram–Shmidt: misol (3)

Normallaymiz:
$$\mathbf e_1 = \frac{1}{\sqrt2}(1, 1, 0),\quad \mathbf e_2 = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(1, -1, -1),\quad \mathbf e_3 = \frac{1}{\sqrt6}(1, -1, 2).$$

$\mathbb R^3$ ning ortonormal bazisi tayyor.

---

## Mashq: Gram–Shmidt

:::savol
$\mathbf a_1 = (1, 0)$, $\mathbf a_2 = (3, 4)$. $\mathbf b_2$ = ?
- $(3, 4)$
+ $(0, 4)$
- $(3, 0)$
- $(0, 1)$
> $\mathbf b_2 = (3, 4) - 3(1, 0)$.
:::

## Ortogonal to'ldiruvchi

:::tarif
$L^\perp = \{\mathbf x : (\mathbf x, \mathbf y) = 0\ \ \forall\mathbf y \in L\}$ — $L$ ning **ortogonal to'ldiruvchisi**.
:::

:::teorema
$$V = L \oplus L^\perp,\qquad \dim L + \dim L^\perp = \dim V.$$
:::

$\mathbb R^3$ da: to'g'ri chiziqning to'ldiruvchisi — unga perpendikulyar tekislik.

---

## Ortogonal proyeksiya

Har bir $\mathbf x$ yagona usulda:
$$\mathbf x = \mathbf x_L + \mathbf x_\perp,\qquad \mathbf x_L \in L,\ \mathbf x_\perp \in L^\perp.$$

- $\mathbf x_L$ — $L$ dagi $\mathbf x$ ga **eng yaqin** vektor.
- $|\mathbf x_\perp|$ — $\mathbf x$ dan $L$ gacha **masofa**.
- Ortonormal bazisda: $\mathbf x_L = \sum (\mathbf x, \mathbf e_i)\mathbf e_i$.

Bu **eng kichik kvadratlar usuli**ning asosi.

---

## Mashq: to'ldiruvchi o'lchovi

:::savol
$\mathbb R^5$ da $\dim L = 2$. $\dim L^\perp$ = ?
- 2
+ 3
- 5
- 7
:::

---

## To'ldiruvchini topish: misol

$L = L\big((1, 1, 0)\big) \subset \mathbb R^3$. $L^\perp$:
$$(\mathbf x, (1, 1, 0)) = 0 \iff x_1 + x_2 = 0.$$

Bu bir jinsli sistema; FYS: $(-1, 1, 0)$, $(0, 0, 1)$. Demak $L^\perp$ — 2-o'lchovli tekislik.

---

## Kompleks Yevklid (unitar) fazo

Kompleks fazoda simmetriklik o'rniga:
$$(\mathbf x, \mathbf y) = \overline{(\mathbf y, \mathbf x)}.$$

$\mathbb C^n$ da: $(\mathbf x, \mathbf y) = x_1\bar y_1 + \dots + x_n\bar y_n$.

Shu tufayli $(\mathbf x, \mathbf x) = |x_1|^2 + \dots + |x_n|^2 \ge 0$ — uzunlik haqiqiy.

---

## Tatbiqlar

- **Eng kichik kvadratlar:** tajriba nuqtalariga eng yaxshi to'g'ri chiziq.
- **Furye qatorlari:** $1, \cos t, \sin t, \cos2t, \dots$ — $C[-\pi, \pi]$ dagi ortogonal sistema.
- **QR-yoyilma:** $A = QR$ — Gram–Shmidtning matritsaviy shakli, sonli usullarda.
- **Signallarni siqish** (JPEG, MP3): ortogonal bazisdagi koeffitsiyentlar.

---

## Tipik xatolar

- Proyeksiyani ayirishda $(\mathbf b, \mathbf b)$ ga bo'lishni unutish.
- $\mathbf b_3$ da faqat $\mathbf b_2$ bo'yicha proyeksiyani ayirish.
- Gram–Shmidtda $\mathbf a_i$ ga emas, $\mathbf b_i$ ga proyeksiyalash kerakligini unutish.
- Normallashni oxirida emas, oraliqda qilib, ildizlar bilan chalkashish.

---

## Xulosa

- Skalyar ko'paytma: uzunlik, burchak, ortogonallik.
- Koshi–Bunyakovskiy: $|(\mathbf x, \mathbf y)| \le |\mathbf x||\mathbf y|$.
- Ortonormal bazisda koordinatalar — skalyar ko'paytmalar.
- Gram–Shmidt: proyeksiyalarni ketma-ket ayirish.
- $V = L \oplus L^\perp$.
