## Ikkinchi tartibli chiziq

:::tarif
Dekart koordinatalarida ikkinchi darajali tenglama bilan beriladigan chiziq:
$$a_{11}x^2 + 2a_{12}xy + a_{22}y^2 + 2a_{13}x + 2a_{23}y + a_{33} = 0.$$
:::

Bu mavzudan 15-mavzugacha shu chiziqlarni o'rganamiz.

---

## Konus kesimlari

Doiraviy konusni tekislik bilan kesganda:
- o'qqa perpendikulyar — **aylana**;
- qiya — **ellips**;
- yasovchiga parallel — **parabola**;
- ikkala pallani kesadi — **giperbola**.

Apolloniy (mil. avv. III asr) ularni sakkiz kitobda o'rgangan.

---

## Aylana

$$(x - a)^2 + (y - b)^2 = R^2$$
— markaz $(a;\ b)$, radius $R$. Umumiy tenglamada $a_{11} = a_{22}$, $a_{12} = 0$.

Markaz va radiusni topish — to'liq kvadrat ajratish.

---

## Aylana: misol

$$x^2 + y^2 + 6x - 2y = 6 \Rightarrow (x + 3)^2 - 9 + (y - 1)^2 - 1 = 6 \Rightarrow (x + 3)^2 + (y - 1)^2 = 16.$$

Markaz $(-3;\ 1)$, $R = 4$.

:::savol
$x^2 + y^2 - 4x + 2y - 4 = 0$ radiusi?
- 2
+ 3
- 4
- 9
> $(x - 2)^2 + (y + 1)^2 = 9$.
:::

## Ellips: ta'rif

:::tarif
Ikki nuqtagacha (**fokuslar** $F_1$, $F_2$) masofalar yig'indisi o'zgarmas va $2a$ ga teng ($2a > |F_1F_2|$) nuqtalar to'plami — **ellips**:
$$|MF_1| + |MF_2| = 2a.$$
:::

**«Bog'bon usuli»:** ikki qoziq, ip va qalam bilan ellips chiziladi.

---

## Kanonik tenglama: hosil qilish (1)

$F_{1,2}(\mp c;\ 0)$, $|F_1F_2| = 2c$:
$$\sqrt{(x + c)^2 + y^2} + \sqrt{(x - c)^2 + y^2} = 2a.$$

Bitta ildizni o'ngga o'tkazib, kvadratga ko'taramiz:
$$a\sqrt{(x - c)^2 + y^2} = a^2 - cx.$$

---

## Kanonik tenglama: hosil qilish (2)

Yana kvadratga ko'taramiz:
$$(a^2 - c^2)x^2 + a^2y^2 = a^2(a^2 - c^2).$$

$b^2 = a^2 - c^2$ deb belgilab, $a^2b^2$ ga bo'lamiz:

:::teorema
$$\frac{x^2}{a^2} + \frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1,\qquad b^2 = a^2 - c^2.$$
:::

---

## Ellips shakli

- Simmetriya o'qlari — $Ox$, $Oy$; markazi — $O$.
- Uchlari: $(\pm a;\ 0)$, $(0;\ \pm b)$.
- $a$ — **katta yarim o'q**, $b$ — **kichik yarim o'q**.
- Ellips $|x| \le a$, $|y| \le b$ to'g'ri to'rtburchak ichida.
- $a = b$ — aylana.

---

## Parametrik tenglama

$$x = a\cos t,\qquad y = b\sin t,\qquad 0 \le t < 2\pi.$$

Tekshirish: $\frac{a^2\cos^2t}{a^2} + \frac{b^2\sin^2t}{b^2} = 1$ ✓

**Siqish ma'nosi:** ellips — $x^2 + y^2 = a^2$ aylanani $Oy$ bo'ylab $\frac ba$ marta siqish natijasi. Yuzi: $\pi ab$.

---

## Misol: elementlarni topish

$\dfrac{x^2}{25} + \dfrac{y^2}{16} = 1$:
- $a = 5$, $b = 4$;
- $c = \sqrt{25 - 16} = 3$, fokuslar $(\pm3;\ 0)$;
- katta o'q uzunligi $10$, kichik — $8$.

---

## Ekssentrisitet

:::tarif
$$e = \frac ca,\qquad 0 \le e < 1.$$
:::

- $e = 0$ — aylana ($c = 0$);
- $e \to 1$ — ellips «yassilanadi»;
- $\frac ba = \sqrt{1 - e^2}$.

Yuqoridagi misolda $e = \frac35$.

---

## Fokal radiuslar

$M(x;\ y)$ ellipsda:
$$r_1 = |MF_1| = a + ex,\qquad r_2 = |MF_2| = a - ex.$$

$r_1 + r_2 = 2a$ ✓. Masalan, $\frac{x^2}{25} + \frac{y^2}{16} = 1$ da $M(5;\ 0)$ uchun $r_1 = 8$, $r_2 = 2$.

---

## Direktrisalar

:::tarif
$x = \pm\dfrac ae$ to'g'ri chiziqlar — **direktrisalar**.
:::

:::teorema
Ellipsning har bir nuqtasi uchun $\dfrac{r_i}{d_i} = e$, $d_i$ — mos direktrisagacha masofa.
:::

Misolda: $x = \pm\frac{25}{3}$.

---

## Mashq: ekssentrisitet

:::savol
$\frac{x^2}{100} + \frac{y^2}{64} = 1$ ekssentrisiteti?
+ $\frac35$
- $\frac45$
- $\frac{8}{10}$
- $\frac{6}{8}$
> $c = \sqrt{100 - 64} = 6$, $e = \frac{6}{10}$.
:::

## Markazi siljigan ellips

$$\frac{(x - x_0)^2}{a^2} + \frac{(y - y_0)^2}{b^2} = 1.$$

:::misol
$25x^2 - 50x + 4y^2 + 16y - 59 = 0$:
$$25(x - 1)^2 + 4(y + 2)^2 = 100 \Rightarrow \frac{(x - 1)^2}{4} + \frac{(y + 2)^2}{25} = 1.$$
Markaz $(1;\ -2)$, katta o'q **vertikal**, $a = 5$, $b = 2$, $c = \sqrt{21}$.
:::

---

## Vertikal ellips

Agar $y^2$ ostidagi son katta bo'lsa — katta o'q $Oy$ bo'ylab, fokuslar $(0;\ \pm c)$.

:::diqqat
Doim **kattaroq maxrajni** $a^2$ deb oling; fokuslar o'sha o'qda.
:::

## Optik xossa

:::teorema
Bir fokusdan chiqqan nur ellipsdan qaytib, **ikkinchi fokusdan** o'tadi.
:::

Sabab: urinma $F_1M$ va $F_2M$ orasidagi burchakni teng ikkiga bo'luvchi tashqi bissektrisa.

## Ellipsni nuqtalar orqali tuzish

Ellips $\frac{x^2}{a^2} + \frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1$ $(4;\ 0)$ va $(0;\ 3)$ dan o'tadi $\Rightarrow$ $a = 4$, $b = 3$.

Fokuslari $(\pm3;\ 0)$, katta o'qi 10 bo'lgan ellips: $a = 5$, $b^2 = 25 - 9 = 16$:
$$\frac{x^2}{25} + \frac{y^2}{16} = 1.$$

---

## Nuqta ellipsga nisbatan

$M(x_0;\ y_0)$ uchun $\frac{x_0^2}{a^2} + \frac{y_0^2}{b^2}$:
- $< 1$ — ichkarida;
- $= 1$ — ellipsda;
- $> 1$ — tashqarida.

Kompyuter o'yinlarida «obyekt zonaga kirdimi?» shunday tekshiriladi.

## Mashq: fokuslar

:::savol
$4x^2 + 9y^2 = 36$ fokuslari?
+ $(\pm\sqrt5;\ 0)$
- $(\pm\sqrt{13};\ 0)$
- $(\pm3;\ 0)$
- $(0;\ \pm\sqrt5)$
> $\frac{x^2}{9} + \frac{y^2}{4} = 1$, $c^2 = 9 - 4 = 5$.
:::

## Tipik xatolar

- Ellipsda $c^2 = a^2 + b^2$ deb yozish (bu giperbola!).
- $a$ ni kichik maxrajdan olish.
- To'liq kvadrat ajratishda koeffitsiyentni qavsdan chiqarmaslik: $25x^2 - 50x = 25(x - 1)^2 - 25$.
- Markazi siljiganda fokuslarni $(\pm c;\ 0)$ deb qoldirish.

---

## Xulosa

- Ellips: $|MF_1| + |MF_2| = 2a$.
- $\frac{x^2}{a^2} + \frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1$, $c^2 = a^2 - b^2$.
- $e = \frac ca < 1$; $r_{1,2} = a \pm ex$; direktrisalar $x = \pm\frac ae$.
- Optik xossa: fokusdan fokusga.
