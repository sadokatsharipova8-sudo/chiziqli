## Savollar

Operator $\mathcal A$ berilgan:
- Qaysi vektorlar **nolga** o'tadi? (Ma'lumot qayerda yo'qoladi?)
- Qaysi vektorlar **natija** sifatida chiqishi mumkin?
- Bazisni qanday tanlasak, matritsa **eng sodda** bo'ladi?

Javoblar — yadro, obraz va o'xshash matritsalar.

---

## Ta'riflar

:::tarif
- $\operatorname{Im}\mathcal A = \{\mathcal A\mathbf x : \mathbf x \in V\}$ — **obraz**;
- $\ker\mathcal A = \{\mathbf x : \mathcal A\mathbf x = \mathbf 0\}$ — **yadro**.
:::

- $\dim\operatorname{Im}\mathcal A$ — operator **rangi**;
- $\dim\ker\mathcal A$ — operator **defekti**.

---

## Qism fazo ekani

:::teorema
$\operatorname{Im}\mathcal A$ va $\ker\mathcal A$ — qism fazolar.
:::

**Yadro:** $\mathcal A\mathbf x = \mathcal A\mathbf y = \mathbf 0 \Rightarrow \mathcal A(\alpha\mathbf x + \beta\mathbf y) = \alpha\mathbf 0 + \beta\mathbf 0 = \mathbf 0$.

**Obraz:** $\alpha\mathcal A\mathbf x + \beta\mathcal A\mathbf y = \mathcal A(\alpha\mathbf x + \beta\mathbf y) \in \operatorname{Im}\mathcal A$.

---

## Geometrik misollar

| Operator ($\mathbb R^2$) | $\ker$ | $\operatorname{Im}$ |
|---|---|---|
| $Ox$ ga proyeksiya | $Oy$ o'qi | $Ox$ o'qi |
| burish | $\{\mathbf 0\}$ | $\mathbb R^2$ |
| nol operator | $\mathbb R^2$ | $\{\mathbf 0\}$ |
| $(x, y) \mapsto (x + y, x + y)$ | $y = -x$ | $y = x$ |

---

## Hisoblash

- $\operatorname{Im}\mathcal A = L(A$ ning ustunlari$)$ $\Rightarrow$ $\operatorname{rang}\mathcal A = r(A)$.
- $\ker\mathcal A$ — $AX = O$ ning yechimlar fazosi; bazisi — **FYS** (8-mavzu).

---

## Misol: matritsa

$\mathcal A\mathbf x = (x_1 + x_2;\ x_2 + x_3;\ x_1 - x_3)$:
$$A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & 0\\ 0 & 1 & 1\\ 1 & 0 & -1\end{pmatrix},\qquad \det A = 0.$$

Pog'onasimon ko'rinish: $r(A) = 2$.

---

## Misol: yadro

$AX = O$:
$$x_1 + x_2 = 0,\quad x_2 + x_3 = 0\ \Rightarrow\ x_1 = -x_2,\ x_3 = -x_2.$$

$\ker\mathcal A = L\big((1, -1, 1)\big)$ — to'g'ri chiziq, defekt 1.

Tekshirish: $\mathcal A(1, -1, 1) = (0, 0, 0)$ ✓

---

## Misol: obraz

$\operatorname{Im}\mathcal A$ — ustunlar qobig'i: $(1, 0, 1)$, $(1, 1, 0)$, $(0, 1, -1)$.

Uchinchisi $= $ ikkinchisi $-$ birinchisi; bazis — birinchi ikki ustun. Obraz — tekislik, rang 2.

$2 + 1 = 3 = \dim\mathbb R^3$ ✓

---

## Rang + defekt teoremasi

:::teorema
$$\operatorname{rang}\mathcal A + \operatorname{def}\mathcal A = \dim V.$$
:::

Bu 8-mavzudagi «FYS da $n - r$ ta vektor» faktining operator tilidagi ifodasi.

Ma'nosi: qancha o'lcham «yo'qolsa» (yadroga ketsa), obraz shuncha kichrayadi.

---

## Mashq: defekt

:::savol
$\mathbb R^3$ da operator rangi 2. Defekti?
- 2
+ 1
- 3
- 0
:::

---

## Mashq: matritsadan

:::savol
$A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3\\ 2 & 4 & 6\\ 3 & 6 & 9\end{pmatrix}$ operatorining defekti?
- 1
+ 2
- 3
- 0
> $r(A) = 1$, $3 - 1 = 2$.
:::

## Differensiallash misoli

$D: P_n \to P_n$:
- $\ker D$ — o'zgarmaslar, $\dim = 1$;
- $\operatorname{Im}D = P_{n-1}$, $\dim = n$;
- $1 + n = n + 1 = \dim P_n$ ✓

Integral hisobdagi «$+C$» — aynan yadro!

---

## Xosmaslik kriteriylari

:::xossa
Quyidagilar teng kuchli:
1. $\mathcal A$ xosmas ($\det A \ne 0$);
2. $\ker\mathcal A = \{\mathbf 0\}$ (inyektiv);
3. $\operatorname{Im}\mathcal A = V$ (syurektiv);
4. $\mathcal A^{-1}$ mavjud;
5. $AX = B$ istalgan $B$ da yagona yechimli.
:::

---

## Proyeksiya: to'g'ri yig'indi

Proyeksiya $\mathcal P$ ($\mathcal P^2 = \mathcal P$) uchun:
$$V = \operatorname{Im}\mathcal P \oplus \ker\mathcal P.$$

Har bir vektor = «proyeksiya qilinadigan qism» + «yo'qoladigan qism». Masalan, $Ox$ ga proyeksiyada $\mathbb R^2 = Ox \oplus Oy$.

---

## Bazis almashganda

$T$ — eski bazisdan yangisiga o'tish matritsasi: $X = TX'$, $Y = TY'$.

$Y = AX \Rightarrow TY' = ATX' \Rightarrow Y' = T^{-1}ATX'$.

:::teorema
$$A' = T^{-1}AT.$$
:::

---

## Misol: diagonal bo'ldi!

$A = \begin{pmatrix} 4 & 1\\ 2 & 3\end{pmatrix}$, yangi bazis $\mathbf e'_1 = (1, -2)$, $\mathbf e'_2 = (1, 1)$:
$$T = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1\\ -2 & 1\end{pmatrix},\qquad T^{-1} = \frac13\begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1\\ 2 & 1\end{pmatrix}.$$
$$A' = T^{-1}AT = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 0\\ 0 & 5\end{pmatrix}.$$

Yangi bazisda operator shunchaki o'qlar bo'yicha cho'zish!

---

## Nega diagonal chiqdi?

$A\mathbf e'_1 = \begin{pmatrix} 4 - 2\\ 2 - 6\end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 2\\ -4\end{pmatrix} = 2\mathbf e'_1$, $\quad A\mathbf e'_2 = \begin{pmatrix} 5\\ 5\end{pmatrix} = 5\mathbf e'_2$.

Yangi bazis vektorlari operator ta'sirida faqat cho'ziladi — ular **xos vektorlar** (18-mavzu).

---

## O'xshash matritsalar

:::tarif
$B = T^{-1}AT$ ($\det T \ne 0$) — $A$ va $B$ **o'xshash**: bitta operator, turli bazislar.
:::

O'xshashlik — ekvivalentlik munosabati: refleksiv, simmetrik, tranzitiv.

---

## Invariantlar

:::xossa
O'xshash matritsalarda bir xil:
- $\det(T^{-1}AT) = \det T^{-1}\det A\det T = \det A$;
- $\operatorname{tr}(T^{-1}AT) = \operatorname{tr}A$;
- rang;
- xarakteristik ko'phad $\det(A - \lambda E)$.
:::

Misolda: $\det A = 10 = 2\cdot5$, $\operatorname{tr}A = 7 = 2 + 5$ ✓

---

## Mashq: iz

:::savol
$\operatorname{tr}A = 6$, $A' = T^{-1}AT$. $\operatorname{tr}A'$?
- 0
+ 6
- $6\det T$
- aniqlab bo'lmaydi
:::

---

## Iz invariantligi isboti

$\operatorname{tr}(XY) = \operatorname{tr}(YX)$ (2-mavzu). Shuning uchun:
$$\operatorname{tr}(T^{-1}AT) = \operatorname{tr}\big((T^{-1}A)T\big) = \operatorname{tr}\big(T(T^{-1}A)\big) = \operatorname{tr}A.$$

---

## Operator vs forma

| | Bazis almashganda | Invariant |
|---|---|---|
| operator | $T^{-1}AT$ | det, iz, xos sonlar |
| bichiziqli forma | $T^TBT$ | rang, inersiya indekslari |

Ortogonal $T$ da ikkalasi bir xil.

---

## Tatbiq: siqish va rang

- Rasmni siqish: past rangli operatorga yaqinlashtirish — yadro katta, obraz kichik.
- Mashinali o'rganishda «o'lchamni kamaytirish»: ma'lumotni obrazi kichik operator bilan proyeksiyalash.
- Kriptografiyada: shifrlash operatori **xosmas** bo'lishi shart — aks holda yadro bor va xabarni tiklab bo'lmaydi.

---

## Mashq: o'xshash matritsalar

:::savol
$A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2\\ 3 & 4\end{pmatrix}$ va $B$ o'xshash. $\det B$ = ?
- 10
+ −2
- 5
- aniqlab bo'lmaydi
> $\det B = \det A = 4 - 6 = -2$.
:::

## Tipik xatolar

- Obraz o'lchamini satrlar soni deb olish (rang kerak).
- Yadro bazisini noto'g'ri FYS bilan topish.
- $A' = TAT^{-1}$ deb yozish (tartib!).
- O'xshash matritsalarning elementlari teng deb o'ylash (faqat invariantlar teng).

---

## Xulosa

- $\operatorname{Im}\mathcal A$ — ustunlar qobig'i; $\ker\mathcal A$ — $AX = O$ yechimlari.
- Rang + defekt = $\dim V$.
- $A' = T^{-1}AT$; o'xshash matritsalar.
- Invariantlar: det, iz, rang, xarakteristik ko'phad.
