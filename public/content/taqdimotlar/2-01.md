## Analitik geometriya

**G'oya** (R. Dekart, P. Ferma, XVII asr): geometrik obyektlarni **sonlar va tenglamalar** orqali o'rganish.

- nuqta $\leftrightarrow$ koordinatalar;
- chiziq, sirt $\leftrightarrow$ tenglama;
- geometrik masala $\leftrightarrow$ algebraik masala.

Asosiy vosita — **vektorlar**: ular 1-semestrdagi chiziqli algebrani geometriyaga bog'laydi.

---

## Hayotda vektorlar

- **Fizika:** kuch, tezlik, tezlanish, impuls — yo'nalishga ega kattaliklar.
- **Navigatsiya:** samolyot tezligi + shamol tezligi = haqiqiy yo'l.
- **Kompyuter o'yinlari:** obyekt harakati, yorug'lik yo'nalishi.
- **Meteorologiya:** shamol xaritasi — har nuqtada vektor.

---

## Vektor

:::tarif
Yo'nalgan kesma — **vektor**: $\overrightarrow{AB}$ yoki $\mathbf a$. $A$ — boshi, $B$ — oxiri, $|\mathbf a|$ — uzunligi (moduli).
:::

**Skalyar** kattalik — faqat son (massa, harorat). **Vektor** kattalik — son + yo'nalish.

---

## Vektorlar tengligi

:::tarif
Uzunliklari teng va yo'nalishlari bir xil vektorlar **teng**.
:::

- Parallel ko'chirishda vektor o'zgarmaydi — **erkin vektorlar**.
- **Nol vektor** $\mathbf 0$ — boshi va oxiri ustma-ust.
- **Birlik vektor** (ort): $|\mathbf a| = 1$.
- **Qarama-qarshi** vektor $-\mathbf a$.

---

## Kollinear va komplanar

:::tarif
- **Kollinear** — bitta to'g'ri chiziqda yoki parallel chiziqlarda: $\mathbf a \parallel \mathbf b$.
- **Komplanar** — bitta tekislikda yoki parallel tekisliklarda.
:::

- Istalgan ikki vektor komplanar.
- Nol vektor har qanday vektorga kollinear.

---

## Qo'shish

**Uchburchak qoidasi:** $\mathbf b$ ning boshini $\mathbf a$ ning oxiriga qo'yamiz:
$$\overrightarrow{AB} + \overrightarrow{BC} = \overrightarrow{AC}.$$

**Parallelogramm qoidasi:** umumiy boshdan qo'yilgan $\mathbf a$, $\mathbf b$ — parallelogramm diagonali.

**Ko'pburchak qoidasi:** $\overrightarrow{AB} + \overrightarrow{BC} + \overrightarrow{CD} = \overrightarrow{AD}$.

---

## Ayirish

$$\mathbf a - \mathbf b = \mathbf a + (-\mathbf b).$$

Parallelogrammda: bir diagonal — $\mathbf a + \mathbf b$, ikkinchisi — $\mathbf a - \mathbf b$.

$\overrightarrow{AB} = \overrightarrow{OB} - \overrightarrow{OA}$ — «oxiridan boshi».

---

## Songa ko'paytirish

$\lambda\mathbf a$:
- uzunligi $|\lambda|\,|\mathbf a|$;
- $\lambda > 0$ — yo'nalish bir xil; $\lambda < 0$ — teskari;
- $\lambda = 0$ — nol vektor.

Ort: $\mathbf a^0 = \dfrac{\mathbf a}{|\mathbf a|}$.

---

## Amallar xossalari

:::xossa
$\mathbf a + \mathbf b = \mathbf b + \mathbf a$; $(\mathbf a + \mathbf b) + \mathbf c = \mathbf a + (\mathbf b + \mathbf c)$; $\mathbf a + \mathbf 0 = \mathbf a$; $\mathbf a + (-\mathbf a) = \mathbf 0$; $1\cdot\mathbf a = \mathbf a$; $\lambda(\mu\mathbf a) = (\lambda\mu)\mathbf a$; $(\lambda + \mu)\mathbf a = \lambda\mathbf a + \mu\mathbf a$; $\lambda(\mathbf a + \mathbf b) = \lambda\mathbf a + \lambda\mathbf b$.
:::

Bu — 1-semestrdagi chiziqli fazo aksiomalari: geometrik vektorlar — **chiziqli fazo** $V_3$.

---

## Mediana vektori

Uchburchak $ABC$ da $M$ — $BC$ ning o'rtasi:
$$\overrightarrow{AM} = \overrightarrow{AB} + \overrightarrow{BM} = \overrightarrow{AB} + \tfrac12\overrightarrow{BC} = \tfrac12\big(\overrightarrow{AB} + \overrightarrow{AC}\big).$$

Vektorlar bilan geometrik isbotlar ancha qisqa bo'ladi.

---

## Misol: uchburchak o'rta chizig'i

$M$, $N$ — $AB$, $AC$ ning o'rtalari:
$$\overrightarrow{MN} = \overrightarrow{AN} - \overrightarrow{AM} = \tfrac12\overrightarrow{AC} - \tfrac12\overrightarrow{AB} = \tfrac12\overrightarrow{BC}.$$

Demak $MN \parallel BC$ va $MN = \frac12 BC$ — o'rta chiziq teoremasi bir qatorda!

---

## Chiziqli bog'liqlik: geometriya

:::teorema
1. $\mathbf a \ne \mathbf 0$ va $\mathbf b$ kollinear $\iff \mathbf b = \lambda\mathbf a$.
2. Uch vektor komplanar $\iff$ chiziqli bog'liq.
3. Fazoda istalgan 4 ta vektor chiziqli bog'liq.
:::

Natija: $\dim V_2 = 2$, $\dim V_3 = 3$.

---

## Bazis

- Tekislikda: istalgan ikki **nokollinear** vektor — bazis.
- Fazoda: istalgan uch **nokomplanar** vektor — bazis.

Har bir vektor bazis bo'yicha yagona yoyiladi: $\mathbf x = x_1\mathbf e_1 + x_2\mathbf e_2 + x_3\mathbf e_3$.

---

## Dekart bazisi

$\mathbf i, \mathbf j, \mathbf k$ — o'zaro perpendikulyar birlik vektorlar:
$$\mathbf a = a_x\mathbf i + a_y\mathbf j + a_z\mathbf k = (a_x;\ a_y;\ a_z).$$

Nuqta $M(x;\ y;\ z)$ — radius-vektori $\overrightarrow{OM} = (x;\ y;\ z)$.

---

## Koordinatalarda amallar

:::xossa
- $\overrightarrow{AB} = (x_2 - x_1;\ y_2 - y_1;\ z_2 - z_1)$
- $\mathbf a \pm \mathbf b = (a_x \pm b_x;\ a_y \pm b_y;\ a_z \pm b_z)$
- $\lambda\mathbf a = (\lambda a_x;\ \lambda a_y;\ \lambda a_z)$
- $|\mathbf a| = \sqrt{a_x^2 + a_y^2 + a_z^2}$
:::

---

## Misol: chiziqli kombinatsiya

$\mathbf a = (1;\ -1;\ 1)$, $\mathbf b = (-2;\ -1;\ 1)$:
$$-\mathbf a - 2\mathbf b = (-1 + 4;\ 1 + 2;\ -1 - 2) = (3;\ 3;\ -3).$$

Mashqlar bo'limidagi «Vektorlarning chiziqli kombinatsiyasi» masalalari shunday.

---

## Mashq: koordinatalar

:::savol
$A(1;\ 2)$, $B(4;\ 6)$. $\overrightarrow{AB}$?
- $(5;\ 8)$
+ $(3;\ 4)$
- $(-3;\ -4)$
- $(4;\ 3)$
:::

---

## Uzunlik va masofa

:::misol
$\mathbf a = (2;\ -1;\ 2)$: $|\mathbf a| = \sqrt{4 + 1 + 4} = 3$, ort: $\mathbf a^0 = \left(\frac23;\ -\frac13;\ \frac23\right)$.

$A(1;\ -2;\ 3)$, $B(3;\ 0;\ 2)$: $|AB| = \sqrt{4 + 4 + 1} = 3$.
:::

---

## Kollinearlik sharti

$$\mathbf a \parallel \mathbf b \iff \frac{a_x}{b_x} = \frac{a_y}{b_y} = \frac{a_z}{b_z}.$$

:::savol
$\mathbf a = (2;\ -4;\ 6)$ va $\mathbf b = (-1;\ m;\ -3)$ qaysi $m$ da kollinear?
+ $m = 2$
- $m = -2$
- $m = 4$
- hech qachon
> Nisbat $-2$: $\frac{-4}{m} = -2$.
:::

---

## Kesmani nisbatda bo'lish

$M$ nuqta $AB$ ni $AM : MB = \lambda$ nisbatda bo'lsa:
$$\overrightarrow{AM} = \lambda\overrightarrow{MB} \Rightarrow M = \frac{A + \lambda B}{1 + \lambda}.$$

Koordinatalarda:
$$x_M = \frac{x_1 + \lambda x_2}{1 + \lambda},\quad y_M = \frac{y_1 + \lambda y_2}{1 + \lambda},\quad z_M = \frac{z_1 + \lambda z_2}{1 + \lambda}.$$

---

## Xususiy hollar

- **O'rta nuqta** ($\lambda = 1$): $M = \frac{A + B}{2}$.
- **Og'irlik markazi** (medianalar kesishishi): $G = \frac{A + B + C}{3}$ — medianani $2:1$ nisbatda bo'ladi.

:::misol
$A(1;\ 3)$, $B(7;\ -3)$, $\lambda = 2$: $M\left(\frac{1 + 14}{3};\ \frac{3 - 6}{3}\right) = (5;\ -1)$.
:::

---

## Misol: parallelogramm

Uchlari $A(1;\ 2)$, $B(5;\ 3)$, $C(6;\ 7)$. $D$?

$\overrightarrow{AD} = \overrightarrow{BC} = (1;\ 4)$, demak $D = (2;\ 6)$.

Tekshirish: diagonallar o'rtalari $AC$: $(3{,}5;\ 4{,}5)$, $BD$: $(3{,}5;\ 4{,}5)$ ✓

:::savol
$A(2;\ -1;\ 4)$, $B(0;\ 5;\ -2)$. $AB$ o'rtasi?
+ $(1;\ 2;\ 1)$
- $(2;\ 4;\ 2)$
- $(-1;\ 3;\ -3)$

## Tatbiq: navigatsiya

Samolyot havoga nisbatan $\mathbf v = (500;\ 0)$ km/soat tezlikda sharqqa uchmoqda, shamol $\mathbf w = (0;\ 50)$ km/soat shimolga.

Yerga nisbatan tezlik: $\mathbf v + \mathbf w = (500;\ 50)$, moduli $\approx 502{,}5$ km/soat — samolyot biroz shimolga «suriladi». Uchuvchi kursni tuzatadi.

---

## Tatbiq: kuchlar muvozanati

Jismga $\mathbf F_1 = (3;\ 4)$, $\mathbf F_2 = (-5;\ 1)$ N kuchlar ta'sir qilmoqda. Muvozanat uchun uchinchi kuch:
$$\mathbf F_3 = -(\mathbf F_1 + \mathbf F_2) = (2;\ -5)\ \text{N}.$$

Ko'priklar, kranlar hisobida aynan shu.

---

## Tipik xatolar

- $\overrightarrow{AB}$ da boshidan oxirini ayirish.
- Vektor uzunligini koordinatalar yig'indisi deb olish.
- Kollinearlikda nol komponentaga bo'lish.
- Bo'lish formulasida $\lambda$ ni $MB : AM$ deb olish.

---

## Xulosa

- Vektor — yo'nalgan kesma; erkin vektorlar chiziqli fazo.
- Kollinear $\iff$ proporsional; komplanar $\iff$ uch vektor bog'liq.
- Dekart bazisi: $\mathbf a = (a_x;\ a_y;\ a_z)$, amallar — koordinatalar bo'yicha.
- $M = \frac{A + \lambda B}{1 + \lambda}$.
