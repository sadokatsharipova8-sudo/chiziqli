## Kesishishni tekshirish

To'g'ri chiziq $x = x_0 + lt$, $y = y_0 + mt$ ni $F(x, y) = 0$ ga qo'yamiz:
$$\varphi(l, m)\,t^2 + 2(F_1l + F_2m)\,t + F(x_0, y_0) = 0,$$
$$\varphi(l, m) = a_{11}l^2 + 2a_{12}lm + a_{22}m^2,$$
$F_1 = a_{11}x_0 + a_{12}y_0 + a_{13}$, $F_2 = a_{12}x_0 + a_{22}y_0 + a_{23}$.

---

## 1-hol: $\varphi(l, m) \ne 0$

Kvadrat tenglama, diskriminant $D$:
- $D > 0$ — ikki nuqta: **kesuvchi**;
- $D = 0$ — bitta ikki karrali nuqta: **urinma**;
- $D < 0$ — umumiy nuqta yo'q.

---

## 2-hol: $\varphi(l, m) = 0$

Tenglama chiziqli: $2(F_1l + F_2m)t + F(x_0, y_0) = 0$:
- bitta nuqta;
- yoki umumiy nuqta yo'q;
- yoki to'g'ri chiziq butunlay chiziqda yotadi.

Bunday $(l : m)$ — **asimptotik yo'nalish**.

---

## Asimptotik yo'nalishlar

$\varphi(l, m) = a_{11}l^2 + 2a_{12}lm + a_{22}m^2 = 0$ — yechimlar soni $\delta$ ga bog'liq:

| Chiziq | $\delta$ | Asimptotik yo'nalishlar |
|---|---|---|
| ellips | $> 0$ | yo'q |
| giperbola | $< 0$ | ikkita (asimptotalar) |
| parabola | $= 0$ | bitta (o'q) |

---

## Misol: parabola

$y^2 = 4x$: $\varphi(l, m) = m^2 = 0 \Rightarrow m = 0$ — $Ox$ yo'nalishi.

$Ox$ ga parallel to'g'ri chiziq $y = c$: $c^2 = 4x \Rightarrow x = \frac{c^2}{4}$ — faqat **bitta** nuqta.

Parabola o'qiga parallel nur paraboladan faqat bir marta qaytadi (optik xossa!).

---

## Misol: giperbola

$x^2 - y^2 = 1$: $\varphi = l^2 - m^2 = 0 \Rightarrow l = \pm m$ — asimptotalar yo'nalishi $y = \pm x$.

$y = x + 1$ bilan: $x^2 - (x + 1)^2 = 1 \Rightarrow -2x - 1 = 1 \Rightarrow x = -1$. Bitta nuqta $(-1;\ 0)$.

---

## Mashq: asimptotik yo'nalish

:::savol
Ellipsning nechta asimptotik yo'nalishi bor?
+ Yo'q
- Bitta
- Ikkita
- Cheksiz ko'p
:::

---

## Kesishish nuqtalarini topish

Amalda: to'g'ri chiziqdan bir o'zgaruvchini ifodalab, chiziq tenglamasiga qo'yamiz.

:::misol
$x^2 + y^2 = 100$ va $2x + y + 10 = 0$: $y = -2x - 10$,
$$x^2 + (2x + 10)^2 = 100 \Rightarrow 5x^2 + 40x = 0 \Rightarrow x_1 = -8,\ x_2 = 0.$$
Nuqtalar $(-8;\ 6)$ va $(0;\ -10)$.
:::

---

## Tekshirish

- $(-8)^2 + 6^2 = 64 + 36 = 100$ ✓, $\ 2(-8) + 6 + 10 = 0$ ✓
- $0 + 100 = 100$ ✓, $\ 0 - 10 + 10 = 0$ ✓

Mashqlar bo'limidagi «To'g'ri chiziq va aylana kesishishi» masalalari shunday.

---

## Misol: ellips va to'g'ri chiziq

$\frac{x^2}{4} + y^2 = 1$ va $y = x - 1$:
$$x^2 + 4(x - 1)^2 = 4 \Rightarrow 5x^2 - 8x = 0 \Rightarrow x = 0,\ x = \tfrac85.$$
Nuqtalar $(0;\ -1)$ va $\left(\frac85;\ \frac35\right)$.

---

## Parametrli masala

$\frac{x^2}{4} + y^2 = 1$ va $y = x + m$:
$$5x^2 + 8mx + 4m^2 - 4 = 0,\qquad \frac D4 = 16m^2 - 5(4m^2 - 4) = 20 - 4m^2.$$

- $|m| < \sqrt5$ — ikki nuqta;
- $m = \pm\sqrt5$ — urinadi;
- $|m| > \sqrt5$ — kesishmaydi.

---

## Mashq: diskriminant

:::savol
To'g'ri chiziq va ellips tenglamalaridan hosil bo'lgan kvadrat tenglama diskriminanti nol. Bu nimani bildiradi?
- Kesishmaydi
+ To'g'ri chiziq urinma
- Ikki nuqtada kesishadi
- Chiziq ellipsda yotadi
:::

---

## Urinma

:::tarif
$\varphi(l, m) \ne 0$ va $D = 0$ bo'lsa, to'g'ri chiziq **urinma**: chiziq bilan bitta (ikki karrali) umumiy nuqtaga ega.
:::

Parabolada o'qqa parallel chiziq ham bitta nuqtada kesadi — lekin bu urinma **emas** (asimptotik yo'nalish).

---

## Urinma tenglamasi

:::teorema
$M_0(x_0;\ y_0)$ chiziqda bo'lsa (markaz bo'lmasa), urinma:
$$F_1(x - x_0) + F_2(y - y_0) = 0,$$
$F_1 = a_{11}x_0 + a_{12}y_0 + a_{13}$, $F_2 = a_{12}x_0 + a_{22}y_0 + a_{23}$.
:::

Normal vektor $(F_1;\ F_2) = \frac12\nabla F$ — gradient!

---

## «Ikkilantirish» qoidasi

Kanonik chiziqlar uchun: $x^2 \to xx_0$, $y^2 \to yy_0$, $y \to \frac{y + y_0}{2}$:

$$\frac{xx_0}{a^2} + \frac{yy_0}{b^2} = 1\quad(\text{ellips}),$$
$$\frac{xx_0}{a^2} - \frac{yy_0}{b^2} = 1\quad(\text{giperbola}),$$
$$yy_0 = p(x + x_0)\quad(\text{parabola}).$$

---

## Urinma: misol 1

$x^2 + y^2 = 25$, $M_0(3;\ 4)$:
$$3x + 4y = 25.$$

Tekshirish: markazdan masofa $\frac{25}{\sqrt{9 + 16}} = 5 = R$ ✓

---

## Urinma: misol 2

$\frac{x^2}{8} + \frac{y^2}{2} = 1$, $M_0(2;\ 1)$ (tekshirish: $\frac48 + \frac12 = 1$ ✓):
$$\frac{2x}{8} + \frac{y}{2} = 1 \Rightarrow x + 2y = 4.$$

---

## Mashq: urinma

:::savol
$y^2 = 4x$ ga $(1;\ 2)$ nuqtadagi urinma?
+ $y = x + 1$
- $y = 2x$
- $y = x - 1$
- $x + y = 3$
> $2y = 2(x + 1)$, $p = 2$.
:::

---

## Tashqi nuqtadan urinmalar

$M(x_1;\ y_1)$ chiziqdan tashqarida. Urinmani $y - y_1 = k(x - x_1)$ ko'rinishda izlaymiz va $D = 0$ shartidan $k$ ni topamiz.

:::misol
$(0;\ 2)$ dan $x^2 + y^2 = 1$ ga: $y = kx + 2$, $x^2 + (kx + 2)^2 = 1$, $(1 + k^2)x^2 + 4kx + 3 = 0$, $D/4 = 4k^2 - 3(1 + k^2) = k^2 - 3 = 0 \Rightarrow k = \pm\sqrt3$.
:::

---

## Diametrlar

:::tarif
Berilgan yo'nalishdagi parallel vatarlarning o'rtalari bitta to'g'ri chiziqda yotadi — **qo'shma diametr**.
:::

- Markaziy chiziqlarda barcha diametrlar markazdan o'tadi.
- Parabolada diametrlar o'qqa parallel.

---

## Tatbiq: optika

Nur ko'zgu-chiziqqa $M_0$ nuqtada tushsa:
1. urinma va normalni topamiz;
2. qaytish burchagi = tushish burchagi (normalga nisbatan).

Parabolik va ellips ko'zgularning optik xossalari aynan shu hisobdan kelib chiqadi.

---

## Urinma va normal

$M_0$ nuqtadagi **normal** — urinmaga perpendikulyar to'g'ri chiziq:
$$\frac{x - x_0}{F_1} = \frac{y - y_0}{F_2}.$$

:::misol
$x^2 + y^2 = 25$, $(3;\ 4)$: normal $\frac{x - 3}{3} = \frac{y - 4}{4}$, ya'ni $y = \frac43x$ — markazdan o'tadi (aylana uchun har doim).
:::

---

## Mashq: kesishish

:::savol
$x^2 + y^2 = 4$ va $y = 3$ nechta nuqtada kesishadi?
+ 0
- 1
- 2

> $x^2 = -5$ — yechim yo'q.
:::

## Tipik xatolar

- Asimptotik yo'nalishdagi bitta kesishishni urinma deb atash.
- Kvadrat tenglamani tuzishda $(kx + b)^2$ ni noto'g'ri ochish.
- Urinmada «ikkilantirish» qoidasini markaz uchun qo'llash.
- Topilgan nuqtalarni ikkala tenglamaga qo'yib tekshirmaslik.

---

## Xulosa

- To'g'ri chiziqni qo'yish → kvadrat tenglama: $D > 0$, $= 0$, $< 0$.
- $\varphi(l, m) = 0$ — asimptotik yo'nalish: ellipsda yo'q, giperbolada 2, parabolada 1.
- Urinma: $F_1(x - x_0) + F_2(y - y_0) = 0$; «ikkilantirish» qoidasi.
- Diametr — parallel vatarlar o'rtalari.
