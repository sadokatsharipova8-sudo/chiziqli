## Asosiy savol

17-mavzuda $A = \begin{pmatrix} 4 & 1\\ 2 & 3\end{pmatrix}$ maxsus bazisda $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 0\\ 0 & 5\end{pmatrix}$ ga aylandi.

Bunday bazisni qanday topamiz? Javob: operator ta'sirida **burilmaydigan** yo'nalishlarni izlaymiz.

---

## Invariant qism fazo

:::tarif
$\mathcal A L \subset L$ — **invariant qism fazo**: $L$ dagi vektorlar $L$ dan chiqmaydi.
:::

Misollar: $\{\mathbf 0\}$, $V$, $\ker\mathcal A$, $\operatorname{Im}\mathcal A$; fazoni $Oz$ atrofida burishda — $Oz$ o'qi va $Oxy$ tekisligi.

---

## Nega muhim?

$V = L_1 \oplus L_2$, ikkalasi invariant bo'lsa, mos bazisda:
$$A = \begin{pmatrix} A_1 & O\\ O & A_2\end{pmatrix}.$$

Murakkab operator — kichik bloklarga ajraladi. Eng sodda bloklar — **$1\times1$**, ya'ni bir o'lchamli invariant qism fazolar.

---

## Xos son va xos vektor

:::tarif
$$\mathcal A\mathbf x = \lambda\mathbf x,\qquad \mathbf x \ne \mathbf 0.$$
$\lambda$ — **xos son**, $\mathbf x$ — **xos vektor**.
:::

$L(\mathbf x)$ — bir o'lchamli invariant qism fazo. Geometrik: operator xos vektorni **burmaydi**, faqat $\lambda$ marta cho'zadi ($\lambda < 0$ — teskari tomonga).

---

## Geometrik misollar

| Operator | Xos vektorlar | Xos sonlar |
|---|---|---|
| $Ox$ ga proyeksiya | $\mathbf i$, $\mathbf j$ | $1$, $0$ |
| $Ox$ ga nisbatan simmetriya | $\mathbf i$, $\mathbf j$ | $1$, $-1$ |
| $k$ marta gomotetiya | hamma vektorlar | $k$ |
| $90^\circ$ ga burish | haqiqiy yo'q | $\pm i$ |

## Xarakteristik tenglama

$\mathcal A\mathbf x = \lambda\mathbf x \iff (A - \lambda E)X = O$ — bir jinsli sistema.

Notrivial yechim $\iff$
$$\det(A - \lambda E) = 0.$$

$\det(A - \lambda E)$ — $n$-darajali **xarakteristik ko'phad**. O'xshash matritsalarda bir xil — operatorning invarianti.

---

## Vieta munosabatlari

:::xossa
$$\lambda_1 + \dots + \lambda_n = \operatorname{tr}A,\qquad \lambda_1\cdots\lambda_n = \det A.$$
:::

$2\times2$: $\lambda^2 - (\operatorname{tr}A)\lambda + \det A = 0$.

Natija: $\det A = 0 \iff \lambda = 0$ xos son ($\ker\mathcal A$ — xos vektorlar fazosi).

---

## Algoritm

1. $\det(A - \lambda E) = 0$ ni yechib, $\lambda_1, \lambda_2, \dots$ ni topish.
2. Har bir $\lambda_i$ uchun $(A - \lambda_iE)X = O$ ning **FYS**i.
3. FYS vektorlari va ularning noldan farqli kombinatsiyalari — xos vektorlar.

---

## Misol: xos sonlar

$$A = \begin{pmatrix} 4 & 1\\ 2 & 3\end{pmatrix}:\qquad \begin{vmatrix} 4 - \lambda & 1\\ 2 & 3 - \lambda\end{vmatrix} = \lambda^2 - 7\lambda + 10 = 0.$$

$\lambda_1 = 2$, $\lambda_2 = 5$. Tekshirish: $2 + 5 = 7 = \operatorname{tr}A$, $2\cdot5 = 10 = \det A$ ✓

---

## Misol: xos vektorlar

$\lambda_1 = 2$: $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1\\ 2 & 1\end{pmatrix}X = O \Rightarrow 2x_1 + x_2 = 0 \Rightarrow X_1 = c(1;\ -2)$.

$\lambda_2 = 5$: $\begin{pmatrix} -1 & 1\\ 2 & -2\end{pmatrix}X = O \Rightarrow x_1 = x_2 \Rightarrow X_2 = c(1;\ 1)$.

Tekshirish: $A(1, 1)^T = (5, 5)^T$ ✓

## 3×3 misol

$A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 0 & 0\\ 1 & 3 & 0\\ 4 & 5 & 1\end{pmatrix}$ — uchburchak matritsa:
$$\det(A - \lambda E) = (2 - \lambda)(3 - \lambda)(1 - \lambda).$$

Xos sonlar — diagonal elementlari: $1, 2, 3$.

:::xossa
Uchburchak (xususan diagonal) matritsaning xos sonlari — diagonal elementlari.
:::

---

## Kompleks xos sonlar

Xarakteristik ko'phad $\lambda^3 - 5\lambda^2 + 17\lambda - 13$:
- $\lambda = 1$ — ildiz ($1 - 5 + 17 - 13 = 0$);
- $\lambda - 1$ ga bo'lsak: $\lambda^2 - 4\lambda + 13 = 0$;
- $\lambda_{2,3} = 2 \pm 3i$.

Haqiqiy matritsaning kompleks xos sonlari **qo'shma juft** bo'lib keladi (1-mavzu).

---

## Burish: kompleks xos sonlar

$R_{90^\circ} = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & -1\\ 1 & 0\end{pmatrix}$: $\lambda^2 + 1 = 0$, $\lambda = \pm i$.

$\mathbb C^2$ da xos vektor: $\lambda = i$ uchun $(1;\ -i)$: $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & -1\\ 1 & 0\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} 1\\ -i\end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} i\\ 1\end{pmatrix} = i\begin{pmatrix} 1\\ -i\end{pmatrix}$ ✓

Kompleks fazoda (15-mavzu) har doim xos vektor bor!

## Diagonallashtirish

:::teorema
Har xil xos sonlarga mos xos vektorlar chiziqli erkli.
:::

Agar xos vektorlardan bazis tuzish mumkin bo'lsa:
$$T^{-1}AT = D = \operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1, \dots, \lambda_n),$$
$T$ ustunlari — xos vektorlar. Yetarli shart: $n$ ta **har xil** xos son.

---

## Diagonallashtirish: misol

$A = \begin{pmatrix} 4 & 1\\ 2 & 3\end{pmatrix}$, $T = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1\\ -2 & 1\end{pmatrix}$:
$$T^{-1}AT = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 0\\ 0 & 5\end{pmatrix}.$$

17-mavzudagi «sirli» bazis — xos vektorlar edi!

---

## Tatbiq: darajalar

$A = TDT^{-1} \Rightarrow A^k = TD^kT^{-1}$, $D^k = \operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1^k, \dots, \lambda_n^k)$.

:::misol
Fibonachchi sonlari: $\begin{pmatrix} F_{n+1}\\ F_n\end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1\\ 1 & 0\end{pmatrix}^n\begin{pmatrix} 1\\ 0\end{pmatrix}$. Xos sonlar $\frac{1 \pm \sqrt5}{2}$ — oltin nisbat paydo bo'ladi: $F_n \approx \frac{1}{\sqrt5}\left(\frac{1 + \sqrt5}{2}\right)^n$.
:::

---

## Diagonallashmaydigan matritsa

$$A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1\\ 0 & 1\end{pmatrix}:\quad \lambda = 1\ (\text{ikki karrali}).$$

$(A - E)X = O \Rightarrow x_2 = 0$ — faqat bitta yo'nalish $(1, 0)$. Bazis tuzib bo'lmaydi — **siljish** diagonallashmaydi.

---

## Simmetrik operatorlar

:::tarif
$(\mathcal A\mathbf x, \mathbf y) = (\mathbf x, \mathcal A\mathbf y)$ — **o'z-o'ziga qo'shma** (simmetrik); ortonormal bazisda $A^T = A$ (kompleksda $A^* = A$).
:::

---

## Asosiy teorema

:::teorema
O'z-o'ziga qo'shma operator uchun:
1. barcha xos sonlar **haqiqiy**;
2. har xil xos sonlarga mos xos vektorlar **ortogonal**;
3. xos vektorlardan **ortonormal bazis** tuzish mumkin: $Q^TAQ = D$.
:::

Bu — 14-mavzudagi ortogonal usul va 2-semestrda chiziq va sirtlarni soddalashtirishning asosi.

---

## Isbot g'oyasi (1-band)

$A\mathbf x = \lambda\mathbf x$, $\mathbf x \ne \mathbf 0$ (kompleks fazoda):
$$\lambda(\mathbf x, \mathbf x) = (A\mathbf x, \mathbf x) = (\mathbf x, A\mathbf x) = \bar\lambda(\mathbf x, \mathbf x).$$

$(\mathbf x, \mathbf x) > 0 \Rightarrow \lambda = \bar\lambda$, ya'ni $\lambda$ haqiqiy. ∎

## Tipik xatolar

- Xos vektor sifatida $\mathbf 0$ ni olish.
- $\det(A - \lambda E)$ da $\lambda$ ni diagonaldan tashqari elementlardan ham ayirish.
- Har qanday matritsa diagonallashadi deb o'ylash.
- Xos vektorlar ustunlarini $T$ ga emas, $T^{-1}$ ga yozish.

---

## Xulosa

- $\mathcal A\mathbf x = \lambda\mathbf x$, $\mathbf x \ne \mathbf 0$; $\det(A - \lambda E) = 0$.
- $\sum\lambda_i = \operatorname{tr}A$, $\prod\lambda_i = \det A$.
- Xos vektorlar bazisi — diagonal matritsa: $T^{-1}AT = D$.
- Simmetrik matritsa: haqiqiy xos sonlar, ortonormal xos vektorlar.
