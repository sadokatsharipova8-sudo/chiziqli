## Nega kompleks fazo?

- Haqiqiy matritsaning xos sonlari kompleks bo'lishi mumkin: $90^\circ$ burish — $\lambda = \pm i$.
- **Elektrotexnika:** o'zgaruvchan tok $I = I_0e^{i\omega t}$, impedans $Z = R + iX$.
- **Signallar:** Furye almashtirishi $e^{ikt}$ bazisida.
- **Kvant mexanikasi:** holat — kompleks fazoning birlik vektori.

## Kompleks chiziqli fazo

Chiziqli fazoning 8 aksiomasi (9-mavzu) — faqat sonlar **kompleks**.

Misollar:
- $\mathbb C^n$ — kompleks sonlar qatorlari;
- kompleks matritsalar $M_{m\times n}(\mathbb C)$;
- kompleks koeffitsiyentli ko'phadlar.

$\dim_{\mathbb C}\mathbb C^n = n$ (bazis $\mathbf e_1, \dots, \mathbf e_n$).

---

## Muammo

Haqiqiy formula $(\mathbf x, \mathbf y) = \sum x_iy_i$ ni $\mathbb C$ da qo'llasak:
$$\mathbf x = (i):\quad (\mathbf x, \mathbf x) = i^2 = -1 < 0!$$
$$\mathbf x = (1, i):\quad (\mathbf x, \mathbf x) = 1 + i^2 = 0,\ \text{lekin } \mathbf x \ne \mathbf 0.$$

Uzunlik ma'nosini yo'qotadi. Yechim — **qo'shma son**.

---

## Kompleks sonlarni eslaymiz

- $\bar z = x - iy$ — qo'shma;
- $z\bar z = |z|^2 = x^2 + y^2 \ge 0$;
- $\overline{z_1z_2} = \bar z_1\bar z_2$.

G'oya: $(\mathbf x, \mathbf x) = \sum x_i\bar x_i = \sum|x_i|^2$ — har doim nomanfiy haqiqiy son.

---

## Ermit skalyar ko'paytma

:::tarif
Kompleks fazoda $(\mathbf x, \mathbf y) \in \mathbb C$ va:
1. $(\mathbf x, \mathbf y) = \overline{(\mathbf y, \mathbf x)}$ — **ermit simmetriyasi**
2. $(\mathbf x + \mathbf y, \mathbf z) = (\mathbf x, \mathbf z) + (\mathbf y, \mathbf z)$
3. $(\lambda\mathbf x, \mathbf y) = \lambda(\mathbf x, \mathbf y)$
4. $(\mathbf x, \mathbf x) > 0$, $\mathbf x \ne \mathbf 0$

Bunday fazo — **kompleks Yevklid** (unitar) fazo.
:::

---

## Aksiomalardan natijalar

- $(\mathbf x, \mathbf x) = \overline{(\mathbf x, \mathbf x)}$ — haqiqiy son.
- Ikkinchi argument bo'yicha **qo'shma chiziqlilik**:
$$(\mathbf x, \lambda\mathbf y) = \overline{(\lambda\mathbf y, \mathbf x)} = \bar\lambda(\mathbf x, \mathbf y).$$

:::diqqat
$(\mathbf x, \mathbf y)$ va $(\mathbf y, \mathbf x)$ — **qo'shma** sonlar, teng emas!
:::

---

## Standart ko'paytma $\mathbb C^n$ da

$$(\mathbf x, \mathbf y) = x_1\bar y_1 + x_2\bar y_2 + \dots + x_n\bar y_n.$$

$$|\mathbf x| = \sqrt{|x_1|^2 + \dots + |x_n|^2}.$$

$\mathbb R^n$ ga kelsak ($y_i$ haqiqiy) — oddiy skalyar ko'paytma. Demak bu — umumlashma.

---

## Misol: ko'paytma

$\mathbf x = (1 + i;\ 2)$, $\mathbf y = (i;\ 1 - i)$:
$$(\mathbf x, \mathbf y) = (1 + i)\cdot\bar i + 2\cdot\overline{(1 - i)} = (1 + i)(-i) + 2(1 + i)$$
$$= (-i + 1) + (2 + 2i) = 3 + i.$$

$(\mathbf y, \mathbf x) = 3 - i$ — qo'shma ✓

---

## Misol: uzunlik

$\mathbf x = (1 + i;\ 2)$:
$$|\mathbf x|^2 = |1 + i|^2 + |2|^2 = 2 + 4 = 6,\quad |\mathbf x| = \sqrt6.$$

$\mathbf x = (1, i)$: $|\mathbf x|^2 = 1 + 1 = 2$ — endi ma'noli ✓

---

---

## Misol: generator uslubida

$\mathbf x = (-3 - 3i;\ 2 - 2i)$, $\mathbf y = (1 + 3i;\ -2)$:
- $x_1\bar y_1 = (-3 - 3i)(1 - 3i) = -3 + 9i - 3i + 9i^2 = -12 + 6i$;
- $x_2\bar y_2 = (2 - 2i)(-2) = -4 + 4i$;
- $(\mathbf x, \mathbf y) = -16 + 10i$.

Mashqlar bo'limidagi «Kompleks skalyar ko'paytma» masalalari shunday.

---

## Saqlanadigan xossalar

:::xossa
- Koshi–Bunyakovskiy: $|(\mathbf x, \mathbf y)| \le |\mathbf x||\mathbf y|$
- Uchburchak tengsizligi
- Ortogonallik: $(\mathbf x, \mathbf y) = 0 \iff (\mathbf y, \mathbf x) = 0$
- Ortogonal sistema erkli
- Gram–Shmidt, ortonormal bazis, ortogonal to'ldiruvchi
:::

«Burchak» odatda ishlatilmaydi — $(\mathbf x, \mathbf y)$ kompleks.

---

## Ortonormal bazis: misol

$\mathbb C^2$ da:
$$\mathbf e_1 = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt2}(1;\ i),\qquad \mathbf e_2 = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt2}(1;\ -i).$$

$(\mathbf e_1, \mathbf e_2) = \frac12\big(1 + i\cdot\overline{(-i)}\big) = \frac12(1 + i\cdot i) = 0$ ✓

$(\mathbf e_1, \mathbf e_1) = \frac12(1 + i\cdot\bar i) = \frac12(1 + 1) = 1$ ✓

---

## Furye koeffitsiyentlari

Ortonormal bazisda $\mathbf x = \sum(\mathbf x, \mathbf e_k)\mathbf e_k$ — xuddi haqiqiy holdagidek.

:::misol
$\mathbf x = (1;\ 0)$, bazis yuqoridagi: $(\mathbf x, \mathbf e_1) = \frac{1}{\sqrt2}$, $(\mathbf x, \mathbf e_2) = \frac{1}{\sqrt2}$:
$$\mathbf x = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt2}\mathbf e_1 + \tfrac{1}{\sqrt2}\mathbf e_2 = \tfrac12(1, i) + \tfrac12(1, -i) = (1, 0)\ ✓$$
:::

---

## Gram–Shmidt kompleks holda

$$\mathbf b_2 = \mathbf a_2 - \frac{(\mathbf a_2, \mathbf b_1)}{(\mathbf b_1, \mathbf b_1)}\mathbf b_1$$

— formula o'zgarmaydi; faqat **tartib muhim**: $(\mathbf a_2, \mathbf b_1)$, $(\mathbf b_1, \mathbf a_2)$ emas.

Tekshirish: $(\mathbf b_2, \mathbf b_1) = (\mathbf a_2, \mathbf b_1) - \frac{(\mathbf a_2, \mathbf b_1)}{(\mathbf b_1, \mathbf b_1)}(\mathbf b_1, \mathbf b_1) = 0$ ✓

---

## Ermit qo'shma matritsa

:::tarif
$$A^* = \bar A^T\quad(\text{transponirlash + qo'shma}).$$
:::

$$A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & i\\ 2 & 3 - i\end{pmatrix} \Rightarrow A^* = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2\\ -i & 3 + i\end{pmatrix}.$$

Asosiy xossa: $(A\mathbf x, \mathbf y) = (\mathbf x, A^*\mathbf y)$.

---

## Ermit matritsalar

:::tarif
$A^* = A$ — **ermit** (o'z-o'ziga qo'shma) matritsa: $a_{ij} = \bar a_{ji}$, diagonal haqiqiy.
:::

$$\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 - i\\ 1 + i & 3\end{pmatrix}$$ — ermit.

:::teorema
Ermit matritsaning barcha xos sonlari **haqiqiy** (18-mavzu).
:::

Kvant mexanikasida o'lchanadigan kattaliklar — ermit operatorlar; o'lchash natijalari (xos sonlar) haqiqiy!

---

## Unitar matritsalar

:::tarif
$U^*U = E$ — **unitar** matritsa.
:::

:::xossa
- Ustunlari ortonormal;
- uzunlikni saqlaydi: $|U\mathbf x| = |\mathbf x|$;
- $|\det U| = 1$;
- haqiqiy holda — ortogonal matritsa.
:::

$U = \frac{1}{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1\\ i & -i\end{pmatrix}$ — unitar (ustunlari $\mathbf e_1, \mathbf e_2$).

## Tatbiq: kvant hisoblash

Kubit — $\mathbb C^2$ dagi birlik vektor: $|\psi\rangle = \alpha|0\rangle + \beta|1\rangle$, $|\alpha|^2 + |\beta|^2 = 1$.

- $|\alpha|^2$, $|\beta|^2$ — o'lchash ehtimolliklari (shuning uchun uzunlik **modul kvadratlari** bilan!).
- Kvant «ventillar» — **unitar** matritsalar, masalan Adamar vertili $\frac{1}{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1\\ 1 & -1\end{pmatrix}$.

---

## Tatbiq: elektrotexnika

Tarmoqdagi kuchlanish va toklar kompleks amplitudalar $\mathbf V, \mathbf I \in \mathbb C^n$.
- O'rtacha quvvat: $P = \operatorname{Re}(\mathbf V, \mathbf I)$ — ermit skalyar ko'paytma!
- Qo'shma olish — fazalar farqini hisobga olish.

---

## Koshi–Bunyakovskiy: misol

$\mathbf x = (1 + i;\ 2)$, $\mathbf y = (i;\ 1 - i)$: $(\mathbf x, \mathbf y) = 3 + i$, $|(\mathbf x, \mathbf y)| = \sqrt{10}$.

$|\mathbf x| = \sqrt6$, $|\mathbf y| = \sqrt{1 + 2} = \sqrt3$, $|\mathbf x||\mathbf y| = \sqrt{18}$.

$\sqrt{10} \le \sqrt{18}$ ✓

+ $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & i\\ -i & 2\end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} i & 1\\ 1 & i\end{pmatrix}$
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2\\ 3 & 4\end{pmatrix}$

## Tipik xatolar

- Qo'shma olishni unutish: $\sum x_iy_i$ deb hisoblash.
- Qo'shmani birinchi ko'paytuvchiga qo'llash (bizning kelishuvda — ikkinchisiga).
- $(\mathbf x, \mathbf y) = (\mathbf y, \mathbf x)$ deb yozish.
- $|1 + i|^2 = 2$ o'rniga $(1 + i)^2 = 2i$ deb olish.

---

## Xulosa

- Kompleks fazoda $(\mathbf x, \mathbf y) = \overline{(\mathbf y, \mathbf x)}$.
- $\mathbb C^n$: $(\mathbf x, \mathbf y) = \sum x_i\bar y_i$, $|\mathbf x|^2 = \sum|x_i|^2$.
- Koshi–Bunyakovskiy, Gram–Shmidt, ortonormal bazis saqlanadi.
- $A^* = \bar A^T$; ermit ($A^* = A$) va unitar ($U^*U = E$) matritsalar.
