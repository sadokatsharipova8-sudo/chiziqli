## Nega?

- **Radar** obyektni masofa va burchak bilan ko'rsatadi — qutb koordinatalari.
- **Quvur, sim, kabel** — silindrik simmetriya.
- **Yer, sayyoralar, antenna nurlanishi** — sferik simmetriya.

Dekart sistemasida aylana $x^2 + y^2 = 25$, qutbda — shunchaki $r = 5$.

---

## Qutb koordinatalari

:::tarif
Tekislikda $O$ — **qutb**, $Ox$ — **qutb o'qi**. $M$ ning koordinatalari:
- $r = |OM| \ge 0$ — qutb radiusi;
- $\varphi$ — qutb burchagi ($0 \le \varphi < 2\pi$ yoki $-\pi < \varphi \le \pi$).
:::

Qutbning o'zi uchun $r = 0$, $\varphi$ aniqlanmagan.

---

## Bog'lanish formulalari

$$x = r\cos\varphi,\qquad y = r\sin\varphi;$$
$$r = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2},\qquad \operatorname{tg}\varphi = \frac yx.$$

:::diqqat
$\operatorname{tg}\varphi$ bir xil bo'lgan ikki burchak $\pi$ ga farq qiladi — **chorakni** tekshiring.
:::

---

## Misol: Dekart → qutb

$M(-1;\ \sqrt3)$: $r = \sqrt{1 + 3} = 2$; II chorak; $\operatorname{tg}\varphi = -\sqrt3$ $\Rightarrow$ $\varphi = \frac{2\pi}{3}$.

$M(-6;\ -8)$: $r = 10$; III chorak; $\varphi = \pi + \operatorname{arctg}\frac43$.

---

## Misol: qutb → Dekart

$r = 2$, $\varphi = \frac\pi2$: $M(0;\ 2)$.

$r = 4$, $\varphi = \frac{5\pi}{6}$: $x = 4\cdot\left(-\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\right) = -2\sqrt3$, $y = 4\cdot\frac12 = 2$.

## Qutb tenglamalari

| Tenglama | Chiziq |
|---|---|
| $r = a$ | aylana, markazi $O$ |
| $\varphi = \varphi_0$ | nur |
| $r = 2a\cos\varphi$ | aylana, markazi $(a;\ 0)$ |
| $r = 2a\sin\varphi$ | aylana, markazi $(0;\ a)$ |
| $r = a\varphi$ | Arximed spirali |
| $r = a(1 + \cos\varphi)$ | kardioida |

---

## O'tkazish: misol 1

$x^2 + y^2 = 2ax$:
$$r^2 = 2ar\cos\varphi \Rightarrow r = 2a\cos\varphi.$$

Qutb tenglamasi ancha sodda.

---

## O'tkazish: misol 2

$r = 4\sin\varphi$:
$$r^2 = 4r\sin\varphi \Rightarrow x^2 + y^2 = 4y \Rightarrow x^2 + (y - 2)^2 = 4.$$

Markazi $(0;\ 2)$, radiusi 2 bo'lgan aylana.

---

## Arximed spirali

$r = a\varphi$: har aylanishda radius $2\pi a$ ga oshadi.

Hayotda: vinil plastinka yo'lagi, o'ralgan arqon, prujina, ba'zi nasoslar (Arximed vinti).

---

## Kardioida va «gullar»

- $r = a(1 + \cos\varphi)$ — kardioida («yurak»); mikrofon yo'nalganlik diagrammasi!
- $r = a\cos2\varphi$ — to'rt yaproqli gul.
- $r = a\cos3\varphi$ — uch yaproqli gul.

Qutb koordinatalarida murakkab chiziqlar oddiy tenglamalarga ega.

---

## Konus kesimlari

Fokus — qutbda bo'lsa:
$$r = \frac{p}{1 - e\cos\varphi}.$$

$e < 1$ — ellips, $e = 1$ — parabola, $e > 1$ — giperbola (10–11-mavzular). Sayyora va kometa orbitalari shu ko'rinishda yoziladi.

---

## Kompleks sonlar bilan bog'liqlik

$$z = r(\cos\varphi + i\sin\varphi) = re^{i\varphi}$$
— aynan $(x;\ y)$ nuqtaning qutb koordinatalari (1-semestr 1-mavzu).

Ko'paytirish: radiuslar ko'paytiriladi, burchaklar qo'shiladi.

---

## Silindrik koordinatalar

$(r, \varphi, z)$ — $Oxy$ dagi qutb koordinatalari + balandlik:
$$x = r\cos\varphi,\qquad y = r\sin\varphi,\qquad z = z.$$

---

| Tenglama | Sirt |
|---|---|

| $\varphi = \varphi_0$ | yarim tekislik |
| $z = c$ | gorizontal tekislik |
| $z = r$ | konus |

## Sferik koordinatalar

$(\rho, \theta, \varphi)$:
- $\rho = |OM|$;
- $\theta$ — $Oz$ dan og'ish burchagi, $0 \le \theta \le \pi$;
- $\varphi$ — azimut, $0 \le \varphi < 2\pi$.

$$x = \rho\sin\theta\cos\varphi,\quad y = \rho\sin\theta\sin\varphi,\quad z = \rho\cos\theta.$$

---

## Sferik: misol

$\rho = 2$, $\theta = \frac\pi2$, $\varphi = 0$: $M(2;\ 0;\ 0)$ — «ekvator»da.

$M(1;\ 1;\ \sqrt2)$: $\rho = \sqrt{1 + 1 + 2} = 2$, $\cos\theta = \frac{\sqrt2}{2}$ $\Rightarrow$ $\theta = \frac\pi4$, $\varphi = \frac\pi4$.

---

## Sferik: sirtlar

| Tenglama | Sirt |
|---|---|
| $\rho = R$ | sfera |
| $\theta = \theta_0$ | konus |
| $\varphi = \varphi_0$ | yarim tekislik |

---

## Geografik koordinatalar

- Kenglik $= 90^\circ - \theta$ (ekvatordan shimol/janubga);
- Uzunlik $= \varphi$ (Grinvichdan sharq/g'arbga).

Toshkent: $\approx 41{,}3^\circ$ sh.k., $69{,}3^\circ$ sh.u. GPS aynan sferik (aniqrog'i — ellipsoidal) koordinatalarda ishlaydi.

## Qaysi sistema qachon?

| Masala | Qulay sistema |
|---|---|
| ko'pburchaklar, to'g'ri chiziqlar | Dekart |
| aylana, spiral, markaziy kuch, radar | qutb |
| quvur, sim, o'q atrofida aylanish | silindrik |
| shar, sayyora, nurlanish | sferik |

2-kursda integrallarni hisoblashda mos sistema integralni keskin soddalashtiradi.

---

## Tatbiq: radar va sonar

Radar ekranida obyekt: masofa $r$ va azimut $\varphi$. Ikki obyekt orasidagi masofa — kosinuslar teoremasi:
$$d^2 = r_1^2 + r_2^2 - 2r_1r_2\cos(\varphi_1 - \varphi_2).$$

Havo harakati boshqaruvi har soniyada shunday hisoblaydi.

---

## Tipik xatolar

- Qutb burchagini chorakni hisobga olmay topish.
- $r$ ni manfiy olish.
- Sferik koordinatalarda $\theta$ ni kenglik bilan chalkashtirish.
- $x = \rho\cos\theta\cos\varphi$ deb yozish (to'g'risi $\sin\theta$).

---

## Xulosa

- Qutb: $x = r\cos\varphi$, $y = r\sin\varphi$; chorakka e'tibor.
- Aylana, spiral, kardioida — sodda qutb tenglamalari.
- Silindrik: qutb + $z$; sferik: $\rho, \theta, \varphi$.
- Sistema masala simmetriyasiga qarab tanlanadi.

---
