## Tekislikdagi bilim → fazoga

| Tekislikda (15-mavzu) | Fazoda |
|---|---|
| $Ax + By + C = 0$ — to'g'ri chiziq | $Ax + By + Cz + D = 0$ — **tekislik** |
| $\mathbf n = (A;\ B)$ | $\mathbf n = (A;\ B;\ C)$ |
| nuqtadan chiziqqacha masofa | nuqtadan tekislikkacha masofa |

Fazodagi to'g'ri chiziq esa — ikki tekislik kesishmasi.

---

## Tekislikning umumiy tenglamasi

:::teorema
Fazoda har qanday tekislik birinchi darajali tenglama bilan beriladi:
$$Ax + By + Cz + D = 0,$$
$\mathbf n = (A;\ B;\ C)$ — tekislikka perpendikulyar **normal vektor**.
:::

---

## Xususiy hollar

| Shart | Tekislik |
|---|---|
| $D = 0$ | koordinata boshidan o'tadi |
| $C = 0$ | $Oz$ o'qiga parallel |
| $B = C = 0$ | $Oyz$ ga parallel: $x = \text{const}$ |
| $D = C = 0$ | $Oz$ o'qidan o'tadi |
| $z = 0$ | $Oxy$ koordinata tekisligi |

---

## Nuqta va normal

$M_0(x_0;\ y_0;\ z_0)$, $\mathbf n = (A;\ B;\ C)$:
$$A(x - x_0) + B(y - y_0) + C(z - z_0) = 0.$$

:::misol
$M_0(1;\ -2;\ 3)$, $\mathbf n = (2;\ 1;\ -1)$:
$$2(x - 1) + (y + 2) - (z - 3) = 0 \Rightarrow 2x + y - z + 3 = 0.$$
:::

---

## Uch nuqta orqali

$M, M_1, M_2, M_3$ bitta tekislikda $\iff$ $\overrightarrow{M_1M}$, $\overrightarrow{M_1M_2}$, $\overrightarrow{M_1M_3}$ komplanar (13-mavzu):
$$\begin{vmatrix} x - x_1 & y - y_1 & z - z_1\\ x_2 - x_1 & y_2 - y_1 & z_2 - z_1\\ x_3 - x_1 & y_3 - y_1 & z_3 - z_1\end{vmatrix} = 0.$$

---

## Uch nuqta orqali: misol

$M_1(1;\ 1;\ 1)$, $M_2(2;\ 3;\ 1)$, $M_3(1;\ 2;\ 3)$:
$$\begin{vmatrix} x - 1 & y - 1 & z - 1\\ 1 & 2 & 0\\ 0 & 1 & 2\end{vmatrix} = 4(x - 1) - 2(y - 1) + (z - 1) = 0.$$

$4x - 2y + z - 3 = 0$. Tekshirish: $M_2$: $8 - 6 + 1 - 3 = 0$ ✓, $M_3$: $4 - 4 + 3 - 3 = 0$ ✓

---

## Kesmalarda va normal tenglama

**Kesmalarda:** $\dfrac{x}{a} + \dfrac{y}{b} + \dfrac{z}{c} = 1$ — o'qlarni $(a;0;0)$, $(0;b;0)$, $(0;0;c)$ da kesadi.

**Normal:** $x\cos\alpha + y\cos\beta + z\cos\gamma - p = 0$, $p$ — boshdan masofa.

:::savol
$2x - 3y + 6z - 12 = 0$ tekislik $Oz$ dan qanday kesma ajratadi?
- 6
+ 2
- −12
- 12
> $\frac{x}{6} + \frac{y}{-4} + \frac{z}{2} = 1$.
:::

---

## Tekisliklar orasidagi burchak

Normal vektorlar orasidagi burchak:
$$\cos\varphi = \frac{|A_1A_2 + B_1B_2 + C_1C_2|}{\sqrt{A_1^2 + B_1^2 + C_1^2}\,\sqrt{A_2^2 + B_2^2 + C_2^2}}.$$

:::misol
$x + 2y + 2z - 3 = 0$ va $6x + 2y - 3z + 1 = 0$:
$$\cos\varphi = \frac{|6 + 4 - 6|}{3\cdot 7} = \frac{4}{21}.$$
:::

---

## Parallellik va perpendikulyarlik

:::xossa
- **Parallel:** $\dfrac{A_1}{A_2} = \dfrac{B_1}{B_2} = \dfrac{C_1}{C_2}$ (normallar kollinear)
- **Perpendikulyar:** $A_1A_2 + B_1B_2 + C_1C_2 = 0$ (normallar perpendikulyar)
:::

:::savol
$2x - y + 3z = 1$ va $x + 5y + kz = 0$ qaysi $k$ da perpendikulyar?
+ $k = 1$
- $k = -1$
- $k = 3$
- $k = 0$
> $2 - 5 + 3k = 0$.
:::

---

## Nuqtadan tekislikkacha masofa

$$d = \frac{|Ax_0 + By_0 + Cz_0 + D|}{\sqrt{A^2 + B^2 + C^2}}.$$

:::misol
- $M(1;\ 2;\ 3)$ dan $2x - y + 2z + 5 = 0$ gacha: $d = \dfrac{|2 - 2 + 6 + 5|}{3} = \dfrac{11}{3}$.
- $(1;\ 1;\ 1)$ dan $2x + 2y + z - 2 = 0$ gacha: $d = \dfrac{|2 + 2 + 1 - 2|}{3} = 1$.
:::

---

## Fazoda to'g'ri chiziq

$M_0(x_0;\ y_0;\ z_0)$ va **yo'naltiruvchi** vektor $\mathbf s = (l;\ m;\ n)$:

**Kanonik:**
$$\frac{x - x_0}{l} = \frac{y - y_0}{m} = \frac{z - z_0}{n}.$$

**Parametrik:**
$$x = x_0 + lt,\quad y = y_0 + mt,\quad z = z_0 + nt.$$

---

## Ikki nuqta orqali

$$\frac{x - x_1}{x_2 - x_1} = \frac{y - y_1}{y_2 - y_1} = \frac{z - z_1}{z_2 - z_1}.$$

:::misol
$A(1;\ 0;\ 2)$, $B(3;\ 1;\ 5)$: $\ \mathbf s = (2;\ 1;\ 3)$,
$$\frac{x - 1}{2} = \frac{y}{1} = \frac{z - 2}{3}.$$
:::

---

## Mashq: yo'naltiruvchi vektor

:::savol
$\frac{x - 1}{2} = \frac{y + 1}{-1} = \frac{z}{3}$ ning yo'naltiruvchi vektori?
- $(1;\ -1;\ 0)$
+ $(2;\ -1;\ 3)$
- $(-1;\ 1;\ 0)$
- $(2;\ 1;\ 3)$
:::

---

## Umumiy tenglama: ikki tekislik kesishmasi

$$\begin{cases} A_1x + B_1y + C_1z + D_1 = 0\\ A_2x + B_2y + C_2z + D_2 = 0\end{cases}$$

Kanonik ko'rinishga o'tish:
1. $\mathbf s = \mathbf n_1 \times \mathbf n_2$ (ikkala normalga perpendikulyar);
2. $M_0$ — sistemaning biror yechimi (masalan, $z = 0$ deb).

---

## Kesishma: misol

$\begin{cases} x + 2y - z = 3\\ 2x - y + z = 1\end{cases}$

$$\mathbf s = \begin{vmatrix} \mathbf i & \mathbf j & \mathbf k\\ 1 & 2 & -1\\ 2 & -1 & 1\end{vmatrix} = (2 - 1;\ -(1 + 2);\ -1 - 4) = (1;\ -3;\ -5).$$

$z = 0$: $x + 2y = 3$, $2x - y = 1$ $\Rightarrow$ $x = 1$, $y = 1$.

$$\frac{x - 1}{1} = \frac{y - 1}{-3} = \frac{z}{-5}.$$

---

## To'g'ri chiziq va tekislik orasidagi burchak

$\mathbf s$ va $\mathbf n$ orasidagi burchak $\psi$ bo'lsa, $\varphi = 90^\circ - \psi$:
$$\sin\varphi = \frac{|Al + Bm + Cn|}{\sqrt{A^2 + B^2 + C^2}\,\sqrt{l^2 + m^2 + n^2}}.$$

:::diqqat
Bu yerda **sinus** — chunki $\mathbf n$ tekislikka perpendikulyar.
:::

---

## To'g'ri chiziq va tekislik: shartlar

:::xossa
- **Parallel** (yoki tekislikda yotadi): $\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf s = Al + Bm + Cn = 0$
- **Perpendikulyar:** $\mathbf n \parallel \mathbf s$: $\dfrac{A}{l} = \dfrac{B}{m} = \dfrac{C}{n}$
:::

Parallel holda: $M_0$ tekislikda yotsa — to'g'ri chiziq butunlay tekislikda.

---

## Mashq: chiziq va tekislik

:::savol
$\frac{x}{1} = \frac{y}{2} = \frac{z}{-1}$ va $x + y + 3z - 5 = 0$ qanday joylashgan?
+ Parallel
- Perpendikulyar
- Chiziq tekislikda yotadi
- $45^\circ$ ostida kesishadi
> $\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf s = 1 + 2 - 3 = 0$; $O$ nuqta tekislikda emas.
:::

## Kesishish nuqtasi

Parametrik tenglamani tekislik tenglamasiga qo'yamiz va $t$ ni topamiz.

:::misol
$x = 1 + t$, $y = 2t$, $z = -1 + t$ va $x + y + z - 6 = 0$:
$$(1 + t) + 2t + (-1 + t) - 6 = 0 \Rightarrow 4t = 6 \Rightarrow t = \tfrac32.$$
Nuqta: $\left(\frac52;\ 3;\ \frac12\right)$.
:::

---

## Nuqtaning tekislikdagi proyeksiyasi

$M(4;\ 3;\ 2)$ ni $x + y + z - 3 = 0$ ga proyeksiyalaymiz.

1. Perpendikulyar: $\mathbf s = \mathbf n = (1;\ 1;\ 1)$: $x = 4 + t$, $y = 3 + t$, $z = 2 + t$.
2. Tekislikka qo'yamiz: $9 + 3t - 3 = 0 \Rightarrow t = -2$.
3. Proyeksiya: $K(2;\ 1;\ 0)$.

Masofa: $|MK| = \sqrt{4 + 4 + 4} = 2\sqrt3$. Formula bilan: $\frac{|4 + 3 + 2 - 3|}{\sqrt3} = \frac{6}{\sqrt3} = 2\sqrt3$ ✓

---

## Simmetrik nuqta

$M$ ga tekislikka nisbatan simmetrik nuqta $M'$: $K$ — $MM'$ ning o'rtasi.
$$M' = 2K - M = 2(2;\ 1;\ 0) - (4;\ 3;\ 2) = (0;\ -1;\ -2).$$

---

## Ikki to'g'ri chiziq

$L_1$: $M_1$, $\mathbf s_1$; $\quad L_2$: $M_2$, $\mathbf s_2$.

| Shart | Joylashuv |
|---|---|
| $\mathbf s_1 \parallel \mathbf s_2$ | parallel (yoki ustma-ust) |
| $\overrightarrow{M_1M_2}\,\mathbf s_1\,\mathbf s_2 = 0$, $\mathbf s_1 \nparallel \mathbf s_2$ | kesishadi |
| $\overrightarrow{M_1M_2}\,\mathbf s_1\,\mathbf s_2 \ne 0$ | **ayqash** |

Tekislikda «ayqash» holat yo'q — bu fazoviy hodisa.

---

## Ayqash to'g'ri chiziqlar orasidagi masofa

$$d = \frac{|\overrightarrow{M_1M_2}\,\mathbf s_1\,\mathbf s_2|}{|\mathbf s_1 \times \mathbf s_2|}.$$

Ma'nosi: parallelepiped hajmi ÷ asos yuzi = balandlik.

---

## Ayqash: misol

$L_1$: $O(0;\ 0;\ 0)$, $\mathbf s_1 = (1;\ 0;\ 0)$ — $Ox$ o'qi.
$L_2$: $M_2(0;\ 1;\ 2)$, $\mathbf s_2 = (0;\ 1;\ 0)$.

$$\begin{vmatrix} 0 & 1 & 2\\ 1 & 0 & 0\\ 0 & 1 & 0\end{vmatrix} = 2 \ne 0\ \text{— ayqash.}$$

$\mathbf s_1 \times \mathbf s_2 = \mathbf k$, $|\mathbf k| = 1$, $\ d = 2$.

---

## Tipik xatolar

- Chiziq va tekislik burchagida $\cos$ ishlatish ($\sin$ kerak).
- Kanonik tenglamada nuqta koordinatalari ishorasini chalkashtirish: $\frac{y + 1}{-1}$ da $y_0 = -1$.
- Ikki tekislik kesishmasida $\mathbf s = \mathbf n_1 + \mathbf n_2$ deb olish.
- Ayqash va parallel to'g'ri chiziqlarni farqlamaslik.

---

## Xulosa

- Tekislik: $Ax + By + Cz + D = 0$, $\mathbf n = (A;\ B;\ C)$.
- $d = \dfrac{|Ax_0 + By_0 + Cz_0 + D|}{\sqrt{A^2 + B^2 + C^2}}$.
- To'g'ri chiziq: $\dfrac{x - x_0}{l} = \dfrac{y - y_0}{m} = \dfrac{z - z_0}{n}$.
- Kesishma yo'nalishi: $\mathbf n_1 \times \mathbf n_2$.
- Ayqash chiziqlar: aralash ko'paytma $\ne 0$.
