## Giperbola: ta'rif

:::tarif
Fokuslargacha masofalar **ayirmasining moduli** o'zgarmas va $2a$ ga teng ($2a < |F_1F_2| = 2c$) nuqtalar to'plami — **giperbola**:
$$\big||MF_1| - |MF_2|\big| = 2a.$$
:::

---

## Kanonik tenglama

Ellipsdagi kabi hisoblab ($F_{1,2}(\mp c;\ 0)$):

:::teorema
$$\frac{x^2}{a^2} - \frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1,\qquad b^2 = c^2 - a^2.$$
:::

:::diqqat
Ellipsda $c^2 = a^2 - b^2$, giperbolada $c^2 = a^2 + b^2$.
:::

---

## Giperbola shakli

- **Ikki tarmoq**, $|x| \ge a$;
- uchlari $(\pm a;\ 0)$;
- $2a$ — **haqiqiy o'q**, $2b$ — **mavhum o'q**;
- simmetriya o'qlari — $Ox$, $Oy$, markazi — $O$.

---

## Asimptotalar

:::teorema
$$y = \pm\frac ba x$$
— giperbolaning **asimptotalari**: tarmoqlar ularga cheksiz yaqinlashadi, lekin kesib o'tmaydi.
:::

Chizish: tomonlari $2a$ va $2b$ bo'lgan to'g'ri to'rtburchak diagonallari.

---

## Misol

$\dfrac{x^2}{16} - \dfrac{y^2}{9} = 1$:
- $a = 4$, $b = 3$, $c = \sqrt{16 + 9} = 5$;
- fokuslar $(\pm5;\ 0)$;
- asimptotalar $y = \pm\frac34x$.

---

## Mashq: $c^2$

:::savol
Giperbola uchun $c^2$?
- $a^2 - b^2$
+ $a^2 + b^2$
- $b^2 - a^2$
- $2ab$
:::

---

## Ekssentrisitet va direktrisalar

:::xossa
- $e = \dfrac ca > 1$; $e$ katta — tarmoqlar «ochiqroq»;
- $\frac ba = \sqrt{e^2 - 1}$;
- direktrisalar $x = \pm\dfrac ae$, $\frac{r_i}{d_i} = e$;
- fokal radiuslar $r_{1,2} = |ex \pm a|$.
:::

Misolda: $e = \frac54$, direktrisalar $x = \pm\frac{16}{5}$.

---

## Mashq: ekssentrisitet

:::savol
$\frac{x^2}{16} - \frac{y^2}{9} = 1$ ekssentrisiteti?
- $\frac34$
+ $\frac54$
- $\frac53$
- $\frac{\sqrt7}{4}$
:::

---

## Teng tomonli giperbola

$a = b$: $x^2 - y^2 = a^2$.
- Asimptotalar $y = \pm x$ — perpendikulyar;
- $e = \sqrt2$.

$45^\circ$ ga burilgan holda: $xy = k$ (5-mavzu). **Boyl–Mariott qonuni** $pV = \text{const}$ — teng tomonli giperbola!

---

## Qo'shma giperbola

$$\frac{x^2}{a^2} - \frac{y^2}{b^2} = -1$$
— tarmoqlari $Oy$ bo'ylab, xuddi shu asimptotalar bilan.

Birinchi giperbola bilan birga asimptotalar orasidagi barcha «choraklarni» to'ldiradi.

---

## Markazi siljigan giperbola

$$4x^2 - 9y^2 - 24x - 36y - 36 = 0.$$
$$4(x - 3)^2 - 36 - 9(y + 2)^2 + 36 - 36 = 0 \Rightarrow \frac{(x - 3)^2}{9} - \frac{(y + 2)^2}{4} = 1.$$

Markaz $(3;\ -2)$, $a = 3$, $b = 2$, $c = \sqrt{13}$. Asimptotalar: $y + 2 = \pm\frac23(x - 3)$.

---

## Parabola: ta'rif

:::tarif
Berilgan nuqta (**fokus** $F$) va to'g'ri chiziqdan (**direktrisa** $d$) **teng uzoqlikdagi** nuqtalar to'plami — **parabola**.
:::

$|MF| = d(M, d)$, ya'ni $e = 1$.

---

## Kanonik tenglama

$F\left(\frac p2;\ 0\right)$, $d$: $x = -\frac p2$:
$$\sqrt{\left(x - \tfrac p2\right)^2 + y^2} = x + \tfrac p2 \Rightarrow$$

:::teorema
$$y^2 = 2px,\qquad p > 0\ \text{— parametr}.$$
:::

$p$ — fokusdan direktrisagacha masofa.

---

## Parabola shakli

- Uchi — $O$, o'qi — $Ox$, o'ngga ochilgan;
- $x^2 = 2py$ — o'qi $Oy$, yuqoriga ochilgan;
- $y^2 = -2px$ — chapga ochilgan;
- fokal radius: $r = x + \frac p2$.

---

## Misollar

- $y^2 = 12x$: $2p = 12$, $p = 6$, fokus $(3;\ 0)$, direktrisa $x = -3$.
- $x^2 = 4y$: $p = 2$, fokus $(0;\ 1)$, direktrisa $y = -1$.
- $y^2 - 2y - 8x + 9 = 0 \Rightarrow (y - 1)^2 = 8(x - 1)$: uchi $(1;\ 1)$, $p = 4$.

---

## Mashq: direktrisa

:::savol
$y^2 = 8x$ direktrisasi?
- $x = 2$
+ $x = -2$
- $x = -4$
- $y = -2$
:::

---

## Mashq: fokus

:::savol
$y^2 = 12x$ fokusi?
- $(0;\ 3)$
+ $(3;\ 0)$
- $(6;\ 0)$
- $(12;\ 0)$
:::

## Parabolaning optik xossasi

:::teorema
Fokusdan chiqqan nurlar paraboladan qaytib, **o'qqa parallel** ketadi (va aksincha: o'qqa parallel nurlar fokusda yig'iladi).
:::

- Projektor, avtomobil farasi, cho'ntak fonari;
- sun'iy yo'ldosh antennasi, radioteleskop;
- quyosh konsentratori — fokusda harorat $1000^\circ$C dan oshadi.

---

## Parabola tabiatda

- **Otilgan jism trayektoriyasi** (havo qarshiligisiz): $y = x\operatorname{tg}\alpha - \frac{gx^2}{2v_0^2\cos^2\alpha}$.
- Favvora oqimi, basketbol to'pi.
- Osma ko'prik troslari (taxminan parabola).

---

## Yagona ta'rif

:::teorema
Fokusgacha masofaning direktrisagacha masofaga nisbati o'zgarmas $e$ bo'lgan chiziq:
- $e < 1$ — **ellips**;
- $e = 1$ — **parabola**;
- $e > 1$ — **giperbola**.
:::

---

## Qutb tenglamasi

Fokus — qutbda:
$$r = \frac{p}{1 - e\cos\varphi}.$$

Bitta formula — uchala chiziq! Osmon mexanikasida orbitalar shunday yoziladi: $e < 1$ — yopiq orbita (sayyora), $e \ge 1$ — ochiq (kometa Quyosh tizimini tark etadi).

---

## Giperbola hayotda

- **Kometalar** ochiq orbitalari ($e > 1$): Oumuamua (2017) — $e \approx 1{,}2$.
- **Navigatsiya (LORAN):** ikki stansiyadan signal kelish vaqtlari farqi o'zgarmas nuqtalar — giperbola; ikki juft stansiya — kesishish nuqtasi.
- **Sovitish minoralari** kesimi.

---

## Uchala chiziqni solishtirish

| | Ellips | Giperbola | Parabola |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ta'rif | $r_1 + r_2 = 2a$ | $\lvert r_1 - r_2\rvert = 2a$ | $r = d$ |
| Tenglama | $\frac{x^2}{a^2} + \frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1$ | $\frac{x^2}{a^2} - \frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1$ | $y^2 = 2px$ |
| $c^2$ | $a^2 - b^2$ | $a^2 + b^2$ | — |
| $e$ | $< 1$ | $> 1$ | $= 1$ |

## Mashq: asimptota

:::savol
$\frac{x^2}{9} - \frac{y^2}{4} = 1$ asimptotalari?
+ $y = \pm\frac23x$
- $y = \pm\frac32x$
- $y = \pm\frac49x$
- $y = \pm x$
:::

## Tipik xatolar

- Giperbolada $c^2 = a^2 - b^2$ deb yozish.
- Asimptotalarni $y = \pm\frac abx$ deb yozish.
- Parabolada fokusni $(p;\ 0)$ deb olish (to'g'risi $\frac p2$).
- $y^2 = 2px$ dan $p$ ni topishda 2 ga bo'lishni unutish.

---

## Xulosa

- Giperbola: $\frac{x^2}{a^2} - \frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1$, $c^2 = a^2 + b^2$, asimptotalar $y = \pm\frac bax$, $e > 1$.
- Parabola: $y^2 = 2px$, $F\left(\frac p2;\ 0\right)$, $d$: $x = -\frac p2$, $e = 1$.
- Uchala chiziq — $e$ bilan yagona ta'rif.
