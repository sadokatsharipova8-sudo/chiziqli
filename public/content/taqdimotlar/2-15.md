## Nomarkaziy hol

$$\delta = a_{11}a_{22} - a_{12}^2 = 0.$$

- Markaz sistemasi determinanti nol — markaz yo'q yoki cheksiz ko'p.
- Xos sonlar: $\lambda_1 = 0$, $\lambda_2 = I_1$.
- Natija: parabola yoki to'g'ri chiziqlar juftligi.

---

## Bosh qism — to'liq kvadrat

$\delta = 0$ bo'lsa:
$$a_{11}x^2 + 2a_{12}xy + a_{22}y^2 = \pm(\alpha x + \beta y)^2.$$

:::misol
$x^2 + 4xy + 4y^2 = (x + 2y)^2$;
$9x^2 - 12xy + 4y^2 = (3x - 2y)^2$.
:::

---

## Mashq: δ = 0

:::savol
Qaysi bosh qism to'liq kvadrat ($\delta = 0$)?
- $x^2 + 2xy + 2y^2$
+ $4x^2 + 4xy + y^2$
- $x^2 - y^2$
- $x^2 + y^2$
> $(2x + y)^2$.
:::

---

## Nega avval burish?

Markaziy holda: ko'chirish → burish.

Nomarkaziy holda markaz yo'q — ko'chirish uchun «nuqta» yo'q. Shuning uchun:
1. **avval burish** — $xy$ hadni yo'qotamiz, bosh qism $I_1Y^2$ ga aylanadi;
2. **keyin to'liq kvadrat** — $Y$ bo'yicha, va ko'chirish.

---

## Algoritm

1. $\lambda_1 = 0$, $\lambda_2 = I_1$ ga mos ortonormal xos vektorlar $\mathbf e_1$, $\mathbf e_2$.
2. $x$, $y$ ni $X$, $Y$ orqali ifodalab qo'yish:
$$I_1Y^2 + 2b_1X + 2b_2Y + a_{33} = 0.$$
3. $Y$ bo'yicha to'liq kvadrat, $X$ bo'yicha ko'chirish.
4. $b_1 \ne 0$ — **parabola** $Y'^2 = 2pX'$; $b_1 = 0$ — to'g'ri chiziqlar.

---

## 1-misol: burishsiz

$$y^2 - 2y - 8x + 9 = 0\quad(xy\ \text{yo'q},\ \delta = 0).$$

To'liq kvadrat:
$$(y - 1)^2 - 1 - 8x + 9 = 0 \Rightarrow (y - 1)^2 = 8(x - 1).$$

---

## 1-misol: natija

Ko'chirish $X = x - 1$, $Y = y - 1$:
$$Y^2 = 8X.$$

Parabola: $2p = 8$, $p = 4$; uchi $(1;\ 1)$; fokus $X = 2$, ya'ni $(3;\ 1)$; direktrisa $x = -1$.

---

## 2-misol: tenglama

$$x^2 + 4xy + 4y^2 - 20x + 10y = 0.$$

$\delta = 4 - 4 = 0$; bosh qism $(x + 2y)^2$; $I_1 = 5$.

---

## 2-misol: xos vektorlar

- $\lambda_1 = 0$: $x + 2y = 0 \Rightarrow (2;\ -1)$;
- $\lambda_2 = 5$: $(1;\ 2)$.

Normallab:
$$x = \frac{2X + Y}{\sqrt5},\qquad y = \frac{-X + 2Y}{\sqrt5}.$$

---

## 2-misol: bosh qism

$$x + 2y = \frac{2X + Y - 2X + 4Y}{\sqrt5} = \frac{5Y}{\sqrt5} = \sqrt5\,Y.$$
$$(x + 2y)^2 = 5Y^2.$$

$xy$ had yo'qoldi ✓

---

## 2-misol: chiziqli qism

$$-20x + 10y = \frac{-20(2X + Y) + 10(-X + 2Y)}{\sqrt5} = \frac{-50X}{\sqrt5} = -10\sqrt5\,X.$$

---

## 2-misol: natija

$$5Y^2 - 10\sqrt5\,X = 0 \Rightarrow Y^2 = 2\sqrt5\,X.$$

**Parabola**, $p = \sqrt5$, uchi koordinata boshida, o'qi $(2;\ -1)$ yo'nalishida.

---

## Invariant formula

Parabola parametri:
$$p = \sqrt{-\frac{\Delta}{I_1^3}}.$$

2-misolda:
$$\Delta = \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 2 & -10\\ 2 & 4 & 5\\ -10 & 5 & 0\end{vmatrix} = -625,\qquad p = \sqrt{\frac{625}{125}} = \sqrt5\ ✓$$

## 3-misol: generator uslubida

$$y^2 - 2y - 8x + 9 = 0 \to (y - y_0)^2 = 2p(x - x_0).$$

Mashqlar bo'limidagi «Parabolaning uchi va parametri» masalalarida $x_0$, $y_0$, $p$ ni topasiz: $y$ bo'yicha to'liq kvadrat va o'ng tomonni guruhlash.

---

## Aynigan holatlar

$b_1 = 0$ (ya'ni $\Delta = 0$):

:::misol
- $x^2 + 4xy + 4y^2 - 1 = 0 \Rightarrow (x + 2y)^2 = 1$ — ikki **parallel** to'g'ri chiziq $x + 2y = \pm1$.
- $(x + 2y)^2 = 0$ — **ustma-ust** to'g'ri chiziqlar.
- $(x + 2y)^2 + 1 = 0$ — **mavhum** parallel to'g'ri chiziqlar.
:::

---

## Markazlar to'g'ri chizig'i

$(x + 2y)^2 = 1$ da markaz sistemasi:
$$x + 2y = 0,\quad 2x + 4y = 0$$
— cheksiz ko'p yechim: $x + 2y = 0$ — **markazlar to'g'ri chizig'i** (ikki parallel chiziq orasidagi simmetriya o'qi).

---

## Yakuniy jadval (12–15-mavzular)

| $\delta$ | $\Delta \ne 0$ | $\Delta = 0$ |
|---|---|---|
| $> 0$ | ellips (haqiqiy/mavhum) | nuqta |
| $< 0$ | giperbola | ikki kesishuvchi to'g'ri chiziq |
| $= 0$ | parabola | parallel / ustma-ust / mavhum to'g'ri chiziqlar |

Istalgan ikkinchi tartibli chiziq — shu **9 tur**dan biri!

## Tatbiq: ballistika

Snaryad trayektoriyasi og'ma koordinatalarda $x^2 + 2xy + y^2 + \dots = 0$ ko'rinishida chiqishi mumkin ($\delta = 0$). Kanonik ko'rinishga keltirib:
- uchi — eng yuqori nuqta;
- $p$ — trayektoriya «kengligi»;
- fokus va o'q — tushish nuqtasini hisoblash.

---

## Tatbiq: antenna sozlash

Parabolik antenna kesimi o'lchanganda tenglama aralash ko'rinishda chiqadi. Nomarkaziy algoritm:
- o'q yo'nalishi — signal manbaiga yo'naltirish;
- fokus — qabul qilgich (konvertor) o'rnatiladigan nuqta.

---

## Fokus va direktrisani topish

Kanonik sistemada $Y^2 = 2pX$: fokus $X = \frac p2$, direktrisa $X = -\frac p2$.

2-misolda $p = \sqrt5$: fokus $X = \frac{\sqrt5}{2}$, $Y = 0$. Eski koordinatalarda:
$$(x;\ y) = \frac{\sqrt5}{2}\cdot\frac{1}{\sqrt5}(2;\ -1) = \left(1;\ -\tfrac12\right).$$

---

## Tipik xatolar

- Nomarkaziy holda markazni izlab vaqt sarflash.
- Burishdan keyin chiziqli hadlarni hisoblashda $\sqrt5$ ni unutish.
- $Y^2 = 2pX$ dan $p$ ni topishda 2 ga bo'lmaslik.
- $\Delta = 0$ holatini parabola deb hisoblash.

---

## Xulosa

Nomarkaziy holat ($\delta = 0$):
1. bosh qism — to'liq kvadrat, $\lambda_1 = 0$, $\lambda_2 = I_1$;
2. **avval burish** (xos vektorlar), keyin to'liq kvadrat va ko'chirish;
3. $\Delta \ne 0$ — parabola, $p = \sqrt{-\Delta/I_1^3}$;
4. $\Delta = 0$ — parallel/ustma-ust/mavhum to'g'ri chiziqlar.
