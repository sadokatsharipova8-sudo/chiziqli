## Ta'rif

:::tarif
$$\mathbf a\mathbf b\mathbf c = (\mathbf a \times \mathbf b)\cdot\mathbf c$$
— **aralash ko'paytma**. Natija — **son**.
:::

Nomi: ikki xil ko'paytma «aralashgan» — vektor va skalyar.

---

## Koordinatalardagi formula

:::teorema
$$\mathbf a\mathbf b\mathbf c = \begin{vmatrix} a_x & a_y & a_z\\ b_x & b_y & b_z\\ c_x & c_y & c_z\end{vmatrix}.$$
:::

Isbot: $\mathbf a \times \mathbf b$ koordinatalari — 2-tartibli minorlar; $\mathbf c$ ga skalyar ko'paytirish — 3-satr bo'yicha yoyilma.

---

## Misol

$\mathbf a = (1;\ 2;\ 3)$, $\mathbf b = (0;\ 1;\ 1)$, $\mathbf c = (2;\ 1;\ 0)$:
$$\mathbf a\mathbf b\mathbf c = \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3\\ 0 & 1 & 1\\ 2 & 1 & 0\end{vmatrix} = 1(0 - 1) - 2(0 - 2) + 3(0 - 2) = -1 + 4 - 6 = -3.$$

---

## Geometrik ma'no

:::teorema
$|\mathbf a\mathbf b\mathbf c|$ — $\mathbf a, \mathbf b, \mathbf c$ ga qurilgan **parallelepiped hajmi**.
:::

Isbot: $V = S_{\text{asos}}\cdot h$; $S_{\text{asos}} = |\mathbf a \times \mathbf b|$, $h = |\text{pr}_{\mathbf a\times\mathbf b}\mathbf c|$:
$$V = |\mathbf a \times \mathbf b|\cdot\frac{|(\mathbf a \times \mathbf b)\cdot\mathbf c|}{|\mathbf a \times \mathbf b|} = |\mathbf a\mathbf b\mathbf c|.$$

---

## Ishora

- $\mathbf a\mathbf b\mathbf c > 0$ — $\mathbf c$ va $\mathbf a \times \mathbf b$ bir tomonda — **o'ng** uchlik.
- $\mathbf a\mathbf b\mathbf c < 0$ — **chap** uchlik.
- $\mathbf a\mathbf b\mathbf c = 0$ — komplanar.

Yuqoridagi misol: $-3$ — chap uchlik, hajm 3.

---

## Mashq: hajm

:::savol
$\mathbf a = (2;\ 0;\ 0)$, $\mathbf b = (0;\ 3;\ 0)$, $\mathbf c = (0;\ 0;\ 4)$ ga qurilgan parallelepiped hajmi?
- 9
+ 24
- 4
- 12
> To'g'ri burchakli parallelepiped: $2\cdot3\cdot4$.
:::

## Xossalar (1)

:::xossa
1. **Doiraviy almashtirishda o'zgarmaydi:** $\mathbf a\mathbf b\mathbf c = \mathbf b\mathbf c\mathbf a = \mathbf c\mathbf a\mathbf b$.
2. **Ikki ko'paytuvchi almashsa — ishora o'zgaradi:** $\mathbf b\mathbf a\mathbf c = -\mathbf a\mathbf b\mathbf c$.
:::

Sabab: determinantda satrlarni almashtirish (1-semestr 3-mavzu). Doiraviy almashtirish = ikki marta almashtirish.

---

## Xossalar (2)

:::xossa
3. $(\mathbf a \times \mathbf b)\cdot\mathbf c = \mathbf a\cdot(\mathbf b \times \mathbf c)$ — «$\times$» va «$\cdot$» o'rnini almashtirish mumkin.
4. Chiziqlilik: $(\lambda\mathbf a + \mu\mathbf a')\mathbf b\mathbf c = \lambda\,\mathbf a\mathbf b\mathbf c + \mu\,\mathbf a'\mathbf b\mathbf c$.
5. Ikkita ko'paytuvchi kollinear bo'lsa — nol.
:::

## Komplanarlik sharti

:::teorema
$$\mathbf a, \mathbf b, \mathbf c\ \text{komplanar} \iff \mathbf a\mathbf b\mathbf c = 0.$$
:::

Geometrik: parallelepiped «yassilanib» qoladi — hajm nol.

Algebraik: uch vektor chiziqli bog'liq $\iff$ determinant nol.

---

## To'rt nuqta bitta tekislikdami?

$A(1;\ 2;\ -1)$, $B(0;\ 1;\ 5)$, $C(-1;\ 2;\ 1)$, $D(2;\ 1;\ 3)$:
$$\overrightarrow{AB} = (-1;\ -1;\ 6),\ \overrightarrow{AC} = (-2;\ 0;\ 2),\ \overrightarrow{AD} = (1;\ -1;\ 4).$$
$$\begin{vmatrix} -1 & -1 & 6\\ -2 & 0 & 2\\ 1 & -1 & 4\end{vmatrix} = 0 - 2 + 12 - 0 - 8 - 2 = 0.$$

Ha — bitta tekislikda.

---

## Mashq: parametr

:::savol
$\mathbf a = (1;\ 0;\ 0)$, $\mathbf b = (0;\ 2;\ 0)$, $\mathbf c = (1;\ 1;\ k)$ qaysi $k$ da komplanar?
+ $k = 0$
- $k = 1$
- $k = 2$
- hech qanday $k$ da
> $\mathbf a\mathbf b\mathbf c = 2k$.
:::

## Tetraedr hajmi

Tetraedr (uchburchakli piramida) — parallelepipedning $\frac16$ qismi:
$$V = \frac16|\overrightarrow{AB}\,\overrightarrow{AC}\,\overrightarrow{AD}|.$$

(Parallelepiped → 2 ta prizma → har biri 3 ta piramida.)

---

## Tetraedr: misol

$A(1;\ 1;\ 1)$, $B(3;\ 1;\ 1)$, $C(1;\ 4;\ 1)$, $D(1;\ 1;\ 5)$:
$$\overrightarrow{AB} = (2;\ 0;\ 0),\ \overrightarrow{AC} = (0;\ 3;\ 0),\ \overrightarrow{AD} = (0;\ 0;\ 4).$$

Aralash ko'paytma $= 24$, $V = \frac{24}{6} = 4$.

---

## Piramida balandligi

$$V = \frac13S_{\text{asos}}\cdot h \Rightarrow h = \frac{3V}{S_{\text{asos}}}.$$

$S_{ABC} = \frac12|\overrightarrow{AB} \times \overrightarrow{AC}| = \frac12|(0;\ 0;\ 6)| = 3$.

$h = \frac{3\cdot4}{3} = 4$ — $D$ dan $ABC$ tekisligigacha masofa.

---

## Umumiy misol

$A(0;\ 0;\ 0)$, $B(1;\ 2;\ 0)$, $C(0;\ 1;\ 3)$, $D(2;\ 0;\ 1)$:
$$\begin{vmatrix} 1 & 2 & 0\\ 0 & 1 & 3\\ 2 & 0 & 1\end{vmatrix} = 1(1 - 0) - 2(0 - 6) + 0 = 13.$$

$V = \frac{13}{6}$.

---

## Tatbiq: kompyuter grafikasi

3D sahnada nuqta $P$ uchburchak $ABC$ tekisligining qaysi tomonida?
$$\operatorname{sign}\big(\overrightarrow{AB}\,\overrightarrow{AC}\,\overrightarrow{AP}\big).$$

- Musbat — «old» tomonda (normal tomonida);
- manfiy — «orqa» tomonda.

Ko'rinmas yuzalarni olib tashlash (back-face culling) shunga asoslanadi.

---

## Tatbiq: hajmni hisoblash

Har qanday ko'pyoqni (yoki 3D modelni) tetraedrlarga bo'lib:
$$V = \sum\frac16|\mathbf a_i\mathbf b_i\mathbf c_i|.$$

3D printerlar material sarfini, CAD dasturlar detal massasini shunday hisoblaydi.

---

## Tatbiq: kristallar

Kristall panjara elementar katakchasi $\mathbf a, \mathbf b, \mathbf c$ vektorlarga qurilgan. Katakcha hajmi $V = |\mathbf a\mathbf b\mathbf c|$ — moddaning zichligini hisoblashda ishlatiladi:
$$\rho = \frac{n\cdot m_{\text{atom}}}{V}.$$

---

## Uchta ko'paytmani solishtirish

| | Natija | Formula | Ma'nosi |
|---|---|---|---|
| $\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b$ | son | $\sum a_ib_i$ | proyeksiya, burchak |
| $\mathbf a \times \mathbf b$ | vektor | $\mathbf i, \mathbf j, \mathbf k$ li determinant | yuza, normal |
| $\mathbf a\mathbf b\mathbf c$ | son | $3\times3$ determinant | hajm, komplanarlik |

---

## Mashq: aralash ko'paytma

:::savol
$\mathbf i\,\mathbf j\,\mathbf k$ = ?
+ 1
- 0
- −1
- $\mathbf k$
> $(\mathbf i \times \mathbf j)\cdot\mathbf k = \mathbf k\cdot\mathbf k = 1$.
:::

## Tipik xatolar

- Tetraedr hajmida $\frac16$ o'rniga $\frac13$ olish.
- Hajmni topishda modulni unutish.
- Vektorlarni nuqtalardan noto'g'ri tuzish (hammasi bitta uchdan).
- Ishorani tahlil qilmasdan uchlik yo'nalishi haqida xulosa.

---

## Xulosa

- $\mathbf a\mathbf b\mathbf c = (\mathbf a \times \mathbf b)\cdot\mathbf c$ = $3\times3$ determinant.
- $|\mathbf a\mathbf b\mathbf c|$ — parallelepiped hajmi; tetraedr — $\frac16$.
- Ishora — uchlik yo'nalishi.
- Komplanar $\iff \mathbf a\mathbf b\mathbf c = 0$.
