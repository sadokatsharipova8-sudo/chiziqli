## Matritsa

:::tarif
$m$ ta satr va $n$ ta ustundan iborat to'g'ri burchakli sonlar jadvali — $m\times n$ o'lchamli **matritsa**:
$$A = \begin{pmatrix} a_{11} & a_{12} & \dots & a_{1n}\\ a_{21} & a_{22} & \dots & a_{2n}\\ \vdots & & \ddots & \vdots\\ a_{m1} & a_{m2} & \dots & a_{mn}\end{pmatrix} = (a_{ij}).$$
:::

$a_{ij}$ — $i$-satr va $j$-ustun kesishmasidagi element.

---

## Matritsa turlari (1)

- **Kvadrat** matritsa: $m = n$; $n$ — uning tartibi.
- **Bosh diagonal**: $a_{11}, a_{22}, \dots, a_{nn}$.
- **Satr-matritsa** $1\times n$ va **ustun-matritsa** $m\times 1$.
- **Nol matritsa** $O$: barcha elementlar nol.

---

## Matritsa turlari (2)

$$\underbrace{\begin{pmatrix} 3 & 0 & 0\\ 0 & -1 & 0\\ 0 & 0 & 5\end{pmatrix}}_{\text{diagonal}}\quad \underbrace{\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0\\ 0 & 1 & 0\\ 0 & 0 & 1\end{pmatrix}}_{\text{birlik } E}\quad \underbrace{\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 7 & 1\\ 0 & 4 & 3\\ 0 & 0 & 6\end{pmatrix}}_{\text{yuqori uchburchak}}$$

Diagonal elementlari teng diagonal matritsa — **skalyar** matritsa: $\lambda E$.

---

## Qo'shish va songa ko'paytirish

:::tarif
Bir xil o'lchamli matritsalar uchun:
$$(A + B)_{ij} = a_{ij} + b_{ij},\qquad (\lambda A)_{ij} = \lambda a_{ij}.$$
:::

:::misol
$$2\begin{pmatrix} 1 & -2\\ 0 & 3\end{pmatrix} + \begin{pmatrix} 4 & 1\\ -1 & 0\end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 6 & -3\\ -1 & 6\end{pmatrix}.$$
:::

---

## Chiziqli amallar xossalari

:::xossa
1. $A + B = B + A$
2. $(A + B) + C = A + (B + C)$
3. $A + O = A$, $\ A + (-A) = O$
4. $(\alpha\beta)A = \alpha(\beta A)$
5. $(\alpha + \beta)A = \alpha A + \beta A$
6. $\alpha(A + B) = \alpha A + \alpha B$
:::

Demak $m\times n$ matritsalar **chiziqli fazo** tashkil etadi (9-mavzu).

---

## Matritsalarni ko'paytirish

:::tarif
$A$ — $m\times n$, $B$ — $n\times p$. $C = AB$ — $m\times p$ matritsa:
$$c_{ij} = \sum_{k=1}^{n} a_{ik}b_{kj}.$$
:::

**«Satrni ustunga»:** $c_{ij}$ — $A$ ning $i$-satri va $B$ ning $j$-ustuni elementlari ko'paytmalari yig'indisi.

:::diqqat
Ko'paytma faqat **$A$ ning ustunlari soni = $B$ ning satrlari soni** bo'lganda aniqlangan.
:::

---

## Ko'paytirish: misol

$$\begin{pmatrix} 2 & -1 & 3\\ 0 & 4 & -2\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} 1 & -2\\ -3 & 1\\ 2 & 3\end{pmatrix}$$

- $c_{11} = 2\cdot1 + (-1)(-3) + 3\cdot2 = 11$
- $c_{12} = 2(-2) + (-1)\cdot1 + 3\cdot3 = 4$
- $c_{21} = 0 + 4(-3) + (-2)\cdot2 = -16$
- $c_{22} = 0 + 4\cdot1 + (-2)\cdot3 = -2$

$$AB = \begin{pmatrix} 11 & 4\\ -16 & -2\end{pmatrix}.$$

---

## Mashq: o'lcham

:::savol
$A$ — $2\times 3$, $B$ — $3\times 4$. $AB$ ning o'lchami?
- $3\times 3$
+ $2\times 4$
- $4\times 2$
- aniqlanmagan
:::

---

## Ko'paytma kommutativ emas!

$$\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\3&2\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\1&1\end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix}3&4\\5&8\end{pmatrix},\qquad \begin{pmatrix}1&2\\1&1\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\3&2\end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix}7&6\\4&4\end{pmatrix}.$$

- Umuman $AB \ne BA$.
- $AB$ mavjud bo'lsa ham, $BA$ mavjud bo'lmasligi mumkin.
- $AB = BA$ bo'lsa, $A$ va $B$ **o'rin almashinuvchi** deyiladi.

---

## Yana bir g'alati xossa

Sonlarda $ab = 0 \Rightarrow a = 0$ yoki $b = 0$. Matritsalarda bunday emas:
$$\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1\\ 0 & 0\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1\\ 0 & 0\end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 0\\ 0 & 0\end{pmatrix}.$$

Ya'ni $A \ne O$, lekin $A^2 = O$ (**nilpotent** matritsa).

---

## Ko'paytirish xossalari

:::xossa
1. $(AB)C = A(BC)$ — assotsiativlik
2. $(A + B)C = AC + BC$, $\ C(A + B) = CA + CB$
3. $\lambda(AB) = (\lambda A)B = A(\lambda B)$
4. $AE = EA = A$
:::

Kvadrat matritsa darajasi: $A^k = \underbrace{A\cdot A\cdots A}_{k}$, $A^0 = E$.

---

## Sistemaning matritsaviy yozuvi

$$\begin{cases} 2x_1 - x_2 + 3x_3 = 5\\ x_1 + 4x_2 - x_3 = 2\end{cases} \iff \begin{pmatrix} 2 & -1 & 3\\ 1 & 4 & -1\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} x_1\\ x_2\\ x_3\end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 5\\ 2\end{pmatrix}.$$

Qisqacha: $AX = B$. Bu yozuv 5–8-mavzularda asosiy vosita bo'ladi.

---

## Transponirlash

:::tarif
$A^T$ — satrlari $A$ ning ustunlaridan iborat matritsa: $(A^T)_{ij} = a_{ji}$.
:::

$$A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3\\ 4 & 5 & 6\end{pmatrix}\ \Rightarrow\ A^T = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 4\\ 2 & 5\\ 3 & 6\end{pmatrix}.$$

---

## Transponirlash xossalari

:::xossa
- $(A^T)^T = A$
- $(A + B)^T = A^T + B^T$, $\ (\lambda A)^T = \lambda A^T$
- $(AB)^T = B^TA^T$ — tartib **teskarisiga** o'zgaradi!
:::

:::savol
$(ABC)^T$ nimaga teng?
- $A^TB^TC^T$
+ $C^TB^TA^T$
- $B^TA^TC^T$
- $CBA$
:::

---

## Simmetrik va kososimmetrik matritsalar

- $A^T = A$ — **simmetrik**: $a_{ij} = a_{ji}$.
- $A^T = -A$ — **kososimmetrik**: $a_{ij} = -a_{ji}$, diagonal nol.

Har qanday kvadrat matritsa:
$$A = \underbrace{\tfrac12(A + A^T)}_{\text{simmetrik}} + \underbrace{\tfrac12(A - A^T)}_{\text{kososimmetrik}}.$$

Simmetrik matritsalar kvadratik formalarda (13–14-mavzular) asosiy rol o'ynaydi.

## Elementar almashtirishlar

Matritsa satrlari ustida:
1. ikki satr o'rnini almashtirish;
2. satrni noldan farqli songa ko'paytirish;
3. bir satrga boshqa satrni songa ko'paytirib qo'shish.

Ular **Gauss usuli**, rang va teskari matritsani topishning asosi.

Har bir elementar almashtirish — chapdan maxsus **elementar matritsaga** ko'paytirish.

---

## Elementar almashtirish: misol

$$\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 1\\ 2 & 5 & 4\\ 3 & 7 & 6\end{pmatrix} \xrightarrow[S_3 - 3S_1]{S_2 - 2S_1} \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 1\\ 0 & 1 & 2\\ 0 & 1 & 3\end{pmatrix} \xrightarrow{S_3 - S_2} \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 1\\ 0 & 1 & 2\\ 0 & 0 & 1\end{pmatrix}.$$

Natija — **pog'onasimon** (uchburchak) matritsa.

---

## O'rin almashtirishlar

:::tarif
$1, 2, \dots, n$ sonlarining ma'lum tartibda yozilishi $(n_1, n_2, \dots, n_n)$ — **o'rin almashtirish**.
:::

Jami o'rin almashtirishlar soni: $P_n = n! = 1\cdot 2\cdots n$.

- $n = 3$: $123,\ 132,\ 213,\ 231,\ 312,\ 321$ — 6 ta.
- $n = 5$: $120$ ta, $n = 10$: $3\,628\,800$ ta.

---

## Inversiya

:::tarif
$i < j$, lekin $n_i > n_j$ bo'lsa, $(n_i, n_j)$ juftlik **inversiya** hosil qiladi. $\sigma$ — inversiyalar soni.
:::

:::misol
$(3, 1, 4, 2)$: $(3,1)$, $(3,2)$, $(4,2)$ — $\sigma = 3$.
:::

Hisoblash usuli: har bir son uchun o'ngida turgan kichik sonlarni sanaymiz va yig'amiz.

---

## Juft va toq o'rin almashtirishlar

- $\sigma$ juft — **juft** o'rin almashtirish
- $\sigma$ toq — **toq** o'rin almashtirish

$n = 3$ uchun:

| O'rin almashtirish | $\sigma$ | Tur |
|---|---|---|
| 123, 231, 312 | 0, 2, 2 | juft |
| 132, 213, 321 | 1, 1, 3 | toq |

---

## Transpozitsiya

:::teorema
Ikki elementning o'rnini almashtirish (**transpozitsiya**) o'rin almashtirishning juft-toqligini **o'zgartiradi**.
:::

Natija: $n \ge 2$ da juft va toq o'rin almashtirishlar soni teng — $\dfrac{n!}{2}$ tadan.

Bu fakt 3-mavzuda determinant ta'rifida ishlatiladi: har bir hadning ishorasi $(-1)^\sigma$.

---

## Mashq: inversiyalar

:::savol
$(2, 4, 1, 3)$ da nechta inversiya bor?
- 2
+ 3
- 4
- 1
> $(2,1),\ (4,1),\ (4,3)$.
:::

---

## Mashq: toq o'rin almashtirishlar

:::savol
4 ta elementdan nechta toq o'rin almashtirish bor?
- 24
+ 12
- 6
- 4
> $4!/2 = 12$.
:::

## Ko'paytirishga yana bir misol

$A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1\\ 0 & 1\end{pmatrix}$ uchun $A^n$ ni topamiz:
$$A^2 = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2\\ 0 & 1\end{pmatrix},\quad A^3 = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 3\\ 0 & 1\end{pmatrix},\ \dots\ \Rightarrow\ A^n = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & n\\ 0 & 1\end{pmatrix}.$$

Isbot — matematik induksiya bilan: $A^{n+1} = A^nA$.

---

## Matritsa izi

:::tarif
Kvadrat matritsaning **izi** — bosh diagonal elementlari yig'indisi:
$$\operatorname{tr}A = a_{11} + a_{22} + \dots + a_{nn}.$$
:::

Xossalari: $\operatorname{tr}(A + B) = \operatorname{tr}A + \operatorname{tr}B$, $\ \operatorname{tr}(AB) = \operatorname{tr}(BA)$.

18-mavzuda: iz — xos sonlar yig'indisi.

---

## Tipik xatolar

- O'lchamlarni tekshirmasdan ko'paytirish.
- $(A + B)^2 = A^2 + 2AB + B^2$ deb yozish. To'g'risi: $A^2 + AB + BA + B^2$.
- $(AB)^T = A^TB^T$ deb yozish.
- Satr va ustun indekslarini chalkashtirish: $a_{ij}$ — avval **satr**.

---

## Xulosa

- Matritsa — sonlar jadvali; o'lchami $m\times n$.
- Qo'shish va songa ko'paytirish — elementma-element.
- Ko'paytirish — «satr × ustun»; assotsiativ, lekin kommutativ emas.
- $(AB)^T = B^TA^T$.
- Elementar almashtirishlar — Gauss usulining asosi.
- O'rin almashtirishning juft-toqligi — inversiyalar soni bilan aniqlanadi.
