## Muammo

Kanonik tenglamalarni bilamiz (10–11-mavzular). Ammo amalda tenglama ko'pincha «aralash» ko'rinishda keladi:
$$3x^2 + 10xy + 3y^2 - 2x - 14y - 13 = 0.$$

Savollar: bu qanday chiziq? Markazi qayerda? Qanday soddalashtiriladi?

---

## Umumiy tenglama

$$F(x, y) = a_{11}x^2 + 2a_{12}xy + a_{22}y^2 + 2a_{13}x + 2a_{23}y + a_{33} = 0.$$

- $a_{11}x^2 + 2a_{12}xy + a_{22}y^2$ — **bosh qism** (kvadratik forma, 1-semestr 13-mavzu);
- $2a_{13}x + 2a_{23}y$ — chiziqli qism;
- $a_{33}$ — ozod had.

---

## Matritsalar

$$A = \begin{pmatrix} a_{11} & a_{12}\\ a_{12} & a_{22}\end{pmatrix},\qquad \bar A = \begin{pmatrix} a_{11} & a_{12} & a_{13}\\ a_{12} & a_{22} & a_{23}\\ a_{13} & a_{23} & a_{33}\end{pmatrix}.$$

:::diqqat
$xy$, $x$, $y$ oldidagi koeffitsiyentlar **ikkiga bo'linadi**: $10xy \to a_{12} = 5$, $-2x \to a_{13} = -1$.
:::

---

## Misol: matritsalar

$3x^2 + 10xy + 3y^2 - 2x - 14y - 13 = 0$:
$$A = \begin{pmatrix} 3 & 5\\ 5 & 3\end{pmatrix},\qquad \bar A = \begin{pmatrix} 3 & 5 & -1\\ 5 & 3 & -7\\ -1 & -7 & -13\end{pmatrix}.$$

---

## Mashq: koeffitsiyent

:::savol
$2x^2 - 6xy + y^2 + 8x - 4y + 1 = 0$ da $a_{12}$ va $a_{13}$?
+ $-3$ va $4$
- $-6$ va $8$
- $-3$ va $8$
- $6$ va $-4$
:::

---

## Ko'chirishda nima o'zgaradi?

$x = x' + x_0$, $y = y' + y_0$:
- bosh qism (kvadratik koeffitsiyentlar) — **o'zgarmaydi**;
- chiziqli koeffitsiyentlar: $a'_{13} = a_{11}x_0 + a_{12}y_0 + a_{13}$, $a'_{23} = a_{12}x_0 + a_{22}y_0 + a_{23}$;
- ozod had: $a'_{33} = F(x_0, y_0)$.

---

## Burishda nima o'zgaradi?

$X = TX'$ ($T$ ortogonal):
- bosh qism matritsasi: $A' = T^TAT$ — xos sonlar saqlanadi;
- chiziqli qism ham buriladi;
- ozod had o'zgarmaydi.

---

## Invariantlar

Ko'chirish va burishda o'zgarmaydi:
$$I_1 = a_{11} + a_{22},\qquad \delta = I_2 = \begin{vmatrix} a_{11} & a_{12}\\ a_{12} & a_{22}\end{vmatrix},\qquad \Delta = I_3 = \det\bar A.$$

$I_1 = \lambda_1 + \lambda_2$, $\delta = \lambda_1\lambda_2$ ($\lambda_i$ — $A$ ning xos sonlari).

---

## Invariantlar: misol

$3x^2 + 10xy + 3y^2 - 2x - 14y - 13 = 0$:
- $I_1 = 6$;
- $\delta = 9 - 25 = -16$;
- $\Delta = \det\bar A = 128$.

---

## Mashq: δ

:::savol
$2x^2 - 2xy - 3y^2 - 6y - 3 = 0$ uchun $\delta$?
+ $-7$
- $-5$
- $-6$
- $7$
> $a_{12} = -1$: $\delta = 2\cdot(-3) - 1 = -7$.
:::

---

## Markaz: ta'rif

:::tarif
$S$ — chiziqning **markazi**, agar chiziq $S$ ga nisbatan simmetrik bo'lsa: $M$ chiziqda $\Rightarrow$ $M$ ning $S$ ga simmetrigi ham chiziqda.
:::

- Ellips, giperbola — markazi bor.
- Parabola — markaz yo'q.
- Ikki parallel chiziq — markazlar to'g'ri chizig'i.

---

## Markaz tenglamalari

:::teorema
$S(x_0;\ y_0)$ markaz $\iff$
$$\begin{cases} a_{11}x_0 + a_{12}y_0 + a_{13} = 0\\ a_{12}x_0 + a_{22}y_0 + a_{23} = 0\end{cases}\qquad\left(\frac{\partial F}{\partial x} = \frac{\partial F}{\partial y} = 0\right).$$
:::

Isbot g'oyasi: markazga ko'chirganda chiziq $(x', y') \to (-x', -y')$ ga nisbatan simmetrik — chiziqli hadlar yo'qolishi kerak.

---

## Markaz: misol 1

$3x^2 + 10xy + 3y^2 - 2x - 14y - 13 = 0$:
$$\begin{cases} 3x + 5y - 1 = 0\\ 5x + 3y - 7 = 0\end{cases} \Rightarrow x_0 = 2,\ y_0 = -1.$$

Tekshirish: $6 - 5 - 1 = 0$ ✓, $10 - 3 - 7 = 0$ ✓

---

## Markaz: misol 2

$-3x^2 + 4xy - y^2 - 6x + 6y + 1 = 0$:
$$\begin{cases} -3x + 2y - 3 = 0\\ 2x - y + 3 = 0\end{cases} \Rightarrow x_0 = -3,\ y_0 = -3.$$

Mashqlar bo'limidagi «Chiziq markazi» masalalari shunday.

---

## Mashq: markaz

:::savol
$x^2 + 2y^2 - 4x + 8y + 1 = 0$ markazi?
+ $(2;\ -2)$
- $(-2;\ 2)$
- $(4;\ -8)$
- $(2;\ 2)$
> $x - 2 = 0$, $2y + 4 = 0$.
:::

---

## Markazga ko'chirish

Ko'chirgandan keyin chiziqli hadlar yo'qoladi:
$$a_{11}x'^2 + 2a_{12}x'y' + a_{22}y'^2 + a'_{33} = 0,$$
$$a'_{33} = a_{13}x_0 + a_{23}y_0 + a_{33} = \frac{\Delta}{\delta}.$$

Misol 1: $a'_{33} = -2 + 7 - 13 = -8 = \frac{128}{-16}$ ✓

---

## Markaziy va nomarkaziy

:::tarif
- $\delta \ne 0$ — **markaziy** chiziq: markaz sistemasi yagona yechimli.
- $\delta = 0$ — **nomarkaziy**: markaz yo'q yoki markazlar to'g'ri chizig'i.
:::

$\delta$ — markaz sistemasining determinanti!

---

## Tur bo'yicha tasnif

| $\delta$ | Tur | $\Delta \ne 0$ da |
|---|---|---|
| $> 0$ | elliptik | ellips (haqiqiy yoki mavhum) |
| $< 0$ | giperbolik | giperbola |
| $= 0$ | parabolik | parabola |

Misol 1: $\delta < 0$ — giperbola.

---

## Aynigan chiziqlar ($\Delta = 0$)

| $\delta$ | Chiziq |
|---|---|
| $> 0$ | nuqta ($x^2 + y^2 = 0$) |
| $< 0$ | ikki kesishuvchi to'g'ri chiziq ($x^2 - y^2 = 0$) |
| $= 0$ | parallel, ustma-ust yoki mavhum to'g'ri chiziqlar |

---

## Misollar: aynigan

- $x^2 - y^2 = 0 \Rightarrow (x - y)(x + y) = 0$ — $y = \pm x$.
- $x^2 + y^2 + 1 = 0$ — mavhum ellips (nuqtasi yo'q).
- $(x + 2y)^2 = 1$ — $x + 2y = \pm1$, ikki parallel to'g'ri chiziq.

---

## Mashq: tur

:::savol
$x^2 + 4xy + 4y^2 + x - 1 = 0$?
- Elliptik
- Giperbolik
+ Parabolik
- Aylana
> $\delta = 4 - 4 = 0$.
:::

---

## Mashq: tur 2

:::savol
$5x^2 + 4xy + 8y^2 - 36 = 0$?
+ Ellips
- Giperbola
- Parabola
- Nuqta
> $\delta = 40 - 4 = 36 > 0$, $\Delta \ne 0$, $I_1\Delta < 0$.
:::

---

## Haqiqiy va mavhum ellips

$\delta > 0$, $\Delta \ne 0$ bo'lsa:
- $I_1\cdot\Delta < 0$ — **haqiqiy** ellips;
- $I_1\cdot\Delta > 0$ — **mavhum** ellips (bitta ham nuqta yo'q).

Masalan, $x^2 + y^2 + 1 = 0$: $I_1 = 2$, $\Delta = 1$ — mavhum.

---

## Tatbiq: tasvirlarni tanish

Kamera kadrida dumaloq obyekt (ko'z qorachig'i, g'ildirak, tanga) **ellips** bo'lib ko'rinadi. Dastur:
1. chegara nuqtalariga umumiy tenglamani moslashtiradi (eng kichik kvadratlar);
2. $\delta > 0$ ekanini tekshiradi;
3. markaz va o'qlarni topadi (14-mavzu).

---

## Tipik xatolar

- $xy$, $x$, $y$ koeffitsiyentlarini ikkiga bo'lmaslik.
- $\Delta$ ni $2\times2$ determinant bilan chalkashtirish.
- $\delta = 0$ da ham markaz sistemasini yagona deb hisoblash.
- Mavhum ellipsni haqiqiy deb chizish.

---

## Xulosa

- Umumiy tenglama: $A$ ($2\times2$) va $\bar A$ ($3\times3$) matritsalar.
- Invariantlar: $I_1$, $\delta$, $\Delta$.
- Markaz: $\frac{\partial F}{\partial x} = \frac{\partial F}{\partial y} = 0$; $a'_{33} = \frac\Delta\delta$.
- $\delta \ne 0$ — markaziy, $\delta = 0$ — nomarkaziy; $\delta$ va $\Delta$ bo'yicha tasnif.
