## Qayerda uchraydi?

- Ikkinchi tartibli chiziq va sirtlar: $ax^2 + 2bxy + cy^2 + \dots$
- Ko'p o'zgaruvchili funksiya ekstremumi: ikkinchi differensial $d^2f$.
- Mexanika: kinetik va potensial energiya.
- Statistika: dispersiya, kovariatsiya matritsasi.
- Optimallashtirish: funksiyaning «qavariqligi».

---

## Chiziqli forma

:::tarif
$f: V \to \mathbb R$ funksiya
$$f(\alpha\mathbf x + \beta\mathbf y) = \alpha f(\mathbf x) + \beta f(\mathbf y)$$
shartni qanoatlantirsa — **chiziqli forma**.
:::

Bazisda: $f(\mathbf x) = a_1x_1 + a_2x_2 + \dots + a_nx_n$, bunda $a_i = f(\mathbf e_i)$.

Misol: $f(x, y, z) = 2x - y + 5z$.

---

## Bichiziqli forma

:::tarif
$B(\mathbf x, \mathbf y)$ har bir argumenti bo'yicha chiziqli bo'lsa — **bichiziqli forma**.
:::

Bazisda:
$$B(\mathbf x, \mathbf y) = \sum_{i,j} b_{ij}x_iy_j = X^TBY,\qquad b_{ij} = B(\mathbf e_i, \mathbf e_j).$$

$B(\mathbf x, \mathbf y) = B(\mathbf y, \mathbf x)$ — **simmetrik** forma ($B^T = B$). Skalyar ko'paytma — simmetrik bichiziqli forma.

---

## Bazis almashganda

$X = TX'$, $Y = TY'$ bo'lsa:
$$B(\mathbf x, \mathbf y) = (TX')^TB(TY') = X'^T(T^TBT)Y'.$$

:::xossa
Yangi bazisda bichiziqli forma matritsasi: $B' = T^TBT$.
:::

Solishtiring: chiziqli operator matritsasi $T^{-1}AT$ ko'rinishda o'zgaradi (11-mavzu).

---

## Kvadratik forma

:::tarif
Simmetrik bichiziqli formada $\mathbf y = \mathbf x$:
$$f(\mathbf x) = \sum_{i,j=1}^n a_{ij}x_ix_j = X^TAX,\qquad A^T = A.$$
$A$ — **matritsa**, $r(A)$ — formaning **rangi**.
:::

Ikki o'zgaruvchida: $f = a_{11}x^2 + 2a_{12}xy + a_{22}y^2$.

---

## Matritsani tuzish

:::diqqat
- $x_i^2$ oldidagi koeffitsiyent — diagonalga $a_{ii}$.
- $x_ix_j$ ($i \ne j$) oldidagi koeffitsiyent **ikkiga bo'linib**, $a_{ij}$ va $a_{ji}$ ga yoziladi.
:::

:::misol
$f = 3x_1^2 - 2x_2^2 + x_3^2 + 4x_1x_2 - 6x_2x_3$:
$$A = \begin{pmatrix} 3 & 2 & 0\\ 2 & -2 & -3\\ 0 & -3 & 1\end{pmatrix}.$$
:::

---

## Teskari yo'nalish

Matritsadan formani tiklash:
$$A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -2\\ -2 & 4\end{pmatrix} \Rightarrow f = x^2 - 4xy + 4y^2 = (x - 2y)^2.$$

$\det A = 0$, $r = 1$: forma bitta kvadratga aylanadi.

---

## Kanonik ko'rinish

:::tarif
Faqat kvadratlardan iborat ko'rinish:
$$f = \lambda_1y_1^2 + \lambda_2y_2^2 + \dots + \lambda_ny_n^2$$
— **kanonik ko'rinish**. Koeffitsiyentlari faqat $+1, -1, 0$ — **normal ko'rinish**.
:::

Kanonik ko'rinishda formaning «tuzilishi» darhol ko'rinadi: qaysi yo'nalishda musbat, qaysida manfiy.

---

## Lagranj teoremasi

:::teorema
Har qanday kvadratik formani xosmas chiziqli almashtirish bilan kanonik ko'rinishga keltirish mumkin.
:::

**Lagranj usuli** — to'liq kvadratlarni ketma-ket ajratish:
1. $x_1^2$ qatnashgan barcha hadlarni to'liq kvadratga yig'amiz.
2. Qolgan forma $x_1$ ni o'z ichiga olmaydi — xuddi shunday davom etamiz.

---

## Lagranj usuli: oddiy misol

$f = x^2 + 4xy + 3y^2$.

$$x^2 + 4xy = (x + 2y)^2 - 4y^2 \Rightarrow f = (x + 2y)^2 - y^2.$$

$y_1 = x + 2y$, $y_2 = y$: $\ f = y_1^2 - y_2^2$.

---

## Kvadratlar yo'q bo'lsa

Agar formada birorta ham kvadrat bo'lmasa, avval:
$$x_1 = t_1 + t_2,\qquad x_2 = t_1 - t_2,$$
so'ng $x_1x_2 = t_1^2 - t_2^2$ — kvadratlar paydo bo'ladi.

---

## Lagranj usuli: murakkab misol (1)

$f = 2x_1x_2 - 6x_2x_3 + 2x_3x_1$.

$x_1 = t_1 + t_2$, $x_2 = t_1 - t_2$, $x_3 = t_3$:
$$f = 2(t_1^2 - t_2^2) - 6(t_1 - t_2)t_3 + 2t_3(t_1 + t_2)$$
$$= 2t_1^2 - 2t_2^2 - 4t_1t_3 + 8t_2t_3.$$

---

## Lagranj usuli: murakkab misol (2)

$t_1$ bo'yicha kvadrat:
$$2t_1^2 - 4t_1t_3 = 2(t_1 - t_3)^2 - 2t_3^2.$$

$t_2$ bo'yicha kvadrat:
$$-2t_2^2 + 8t_2t_3 = -2(t_2 - 2t_3)^2 + 8t_3^2.$$

Natija: $f = 2(t_1 - t_3)^2 - 2(t_2 - 2t_3)^2 + 6t_3^2$.

$$\boxed{f = 2y_1^2 - 2y_2^2 + 6y_3^2}$$

---

## Ortogonal almashtirish usuli

:::teorema
Har qanday haqiqiy kvadratik formani **ortogonal** almashtirish bilan
$$f = \lambda_1y_1^2 + \dots + \lambda_ny_n^2$$
ko'rinishga keltirish mumkin; $\lambda_i$ — $A$ ning **xos sonlari**, yangi o'qlar — ortonormal xos vektorlar.
:::

Afzalligi: uzunlik va burchaklar saqlanadi — geometriya uchun zarur (17–18-mavzular).

---

## Ortogonal usul: misol

$f = 5x^2 + 4xy + 8y^2$, $\ A = \begin{pmatrix} 5 & 2\\ 2 & 8\end{pmatrix}$.

$$\det(A - \lambda E) = \lambda^2 - 13\lambda + 36 = 0 \Rightarrow \lambda_1 = 4,\ \lambda_2 = 9.$$

Kanonik ko'rinish: $f = 4x'^2 + 9y'^2$.

Xos vektorlar: $\lambda = 4$ — $(2, -1)$; $\lambda = 9$ — $(1, 2)$ — o'zaro perpendikulyar.

---

## Kanonik ko'rinish yagonami?

Yo'q! Turli almashtirishlar turli koeffitsiyentlar beradi:
- Lagranj: $2y_1^2 - 2y_2^2 + 6y_3^2$
- Boshqa almashtirish: $y_1^2 - 5y_2^2 + 3y_3^2$ va h.k.

Lekin nimadir o'zgarmaydi…

---

## Inersiya qonuni

:::teorema Silvestr inersiya qonuni
Kvadratik formaning qaysi usulda kanonik ko'rinishga keltirilishidan qat'i nazar:
- musbat koeffitsiyentlar soni $p$ (**musbat inersiya indeksi**),
- manfiy koeffitsiyentlar soni $q$ (**manfiy inersiya indeksi**)

o'zgarmaydi. $p + q = r$ — forma rangi.
:::

Yuqoridagi misolda: $p = 2$, $q = 1$, $r = 3$.

## Aniqlanganlik

:::tarif
- Har qanday $\mathbf x \ne \mathbf 0$ da $f(\mathbf x) > 0$ — **musbat aniqlangan**
- $f(\mathbf x) < 0$ — **manfiy aniqlangan**
- har ikki ishorali qiymat — **aniqlanmagan**
:::

Kanonik ko'rinishda: musbat aniqlangan $\iff$ barcha $\lambda_i > 0$ ($p = n$).

Misol: $x^2 + y^2$ — musbat aniqlangan; $x^2 - y^2$ — aniqlanmagan.

---

## Burchak minorlari

$$\Delta_1 = a_{11},\quad \Delta_2 = \begin{vmatrix} a_{11} & a_{12}\\ a_{21} & a_{22}\end{vmatrix},\quad \dots,\quad \Delta_n = \det A.$$

Chap yuqori burchakdan boshlanuvchi ketma-ket minorlar.

---

## Silvestr mezoni

:::teorema Silvestr mezoni
1. Musbat aniqlangan $\iff$ $\Delta_1 > 0,\ \Delta_2 > 0,\ \dots,\ \Delta_n > 0$.
2. Manfiy aniqlangan $\iff$ $\Delta_1 < 0,\ \Delta_2 > 0,\ \Delta_3 < 0,\ \dots$ (ishoralar navbatlashadi).
:::

Kanonik ko'rinishga keltirmasdan, faqat determinantlar bilan tekshirish!

---

## Silvestr mezoni: misol

$f = 2x_1^2 + 2x_2^2 + 2x_3^2 + 2x_1x_2 + 2x_2x_3$:
$$A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 & 0\\ 1 & 2 & 1\\ 0 & 1 & 2\end{pmatrix}.$$

$\Delta_1 = 2 > 0$, $\ \Delta_2 = 4 - 1 = 3 > 0$, $\ \Delta_3 = 2\cdot 3 - 1\cdot 2 + 0 = 4 > 0$.

**Musbat aniqlangan.**

---

## Mashq: Silvestr

:::savol
$f = -x^2 + 2xy - 3y^2$ qanday forma?
- Musbat aniqlangan
+ Manfiy aniqlangan
- Aniqlanmagan
- Aynigan
> $\Delta_1 = -1 < 0$, $\Delta_2 = 3 - 1 = 2 > 0$.
:::

---

## Yakobi usuli

Barcha $\Delta_k \ne 0$ bo'lsa:
$$f = \frac{\Delta_1}{\Delta_0}y_1^2 + \frac{\Delta_2}{\Delta_1}y_2^2 + \dots + \frac{\Delta_n}{\Delta_{n-1}}y_n^2,\qquad \Delta_0 = 1.$$

Yuqoridagi misolda: $f = 2y_1^2 + \frac32y_2^2 + \frac43y_3^2$ — hammasi musbat ✓

---

## Ekstremumga tatbiq

$f(x, y)$ funksiyaning statsionar nuqtasida Gesse matritsasi
$$H = \begin{pmatrix} f_{xx} & f_{xy}\\ f_{xy} & f_{yy}\end{pmatrix}$$
- musbat aniqlangan — **minimum**,
- manfiy aniqlangan — **maksimum**,
- aniqlanmagan — ekstremum yo'q («egar»).

---

## Tipik xatolar

- $x_ix_j$ koeffitsiyentini ikkiga bo'lmaslik.
- Silvestr mezonida manfiy aniqlanganlik uchun barcha $\Delta_k < 0$ deb o'ylash.
- Lagranj usulida almashtirishning xosmasligini tekshirmaslik.
- Kanonik koeffitsiyentlarni yagona deb hisoblash (yagona — faqat $p$ va $q$).

---

## Xulosa

- Kvadratik forma: $f = X^TAX$, $A$ — simmetrik.
- Lagranj usuli — to'liq kvadratlar ajratish.
- Ortogonal usul — xos sonlar orqali.
- Inersiya qonuni: $p$ va $q$ o'zgarmas.
- Silvestr: musbat aniqlangan $\iff$ barcha $\Delta_k > 0$.
