## Koordinatalar usuli

R. Dekart (1637): «Har bir nuqtaga sonlar, har bir chiziqqa tenglama».

Muhim kuzatuv: **bitta** geometrik obyekt turli koordinata sistemalarida turlicha ko'rinadi.
- $xy = 1$ va $\dfrac{x'^2}{2} - \dfrac{y'^2}{2} = 1$ — bitta giperbola!
- $x^2 + y^2 = 4$ va $r = 2$ — bitta aylana!

Maqsad: masalaga **qulay** sistemani tanlash.

---

## Affin koordinatalar sistemasi

:::tarif
$O$ nuqta va $\mathbf e_1, \mathbf e_2, \mathbf e_3$ bazis — **affin koordinatalar sistemasi**. $M$ ning koordinatalari:
$$\overrightarrow{OM} = x\mathbf e_1 + y\mathbf e_2 + z\mathbf e_3.$$
:::

Bazis ortonormal ($\mathbf i, \mathbf j, \mathbf k$) bo'lsa — **to'g'ri burchakli Dekart** sistemasi.

---

## Dekart sistemasi

- $Ox$ — abssissa, $Oy$ — ordinata, $Oz$ — applikata o'qi.
- Koordinata tekisliklari: $Oxy$ ($z = 0$), $Oxz$ ($y = 0$), $Oyz$ ($x = 0$).
- Fazo 8 ta **oktant**ga bo'linadi; tekislik 4 ta **chorak**ka.

O'ng sistema: $\mathbf i \times \mathbf j = \mathbf k$.

---

## Masofa

$$|AB| = \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2 + (z_2 - z_1)^2}.$$

:::savol
$A(1;\ -2;\ 3)$ va $B(3;\ 0;\ 2)$ orasidagi masofa?
+ 3
- 9
- $\sqrt5$
- 5
> $\sqrt{4 + 4 + 1} = 3$.
:::

## Uchburchak yuzi (tekislikda)

$$S = \frac12\left|\begin{vmatrix} x_2 - x_1 & y_2 - y_1\\ x_3 - x_1 & y_3 - y_1\end{vmatrix}\right|.$$

Yoki uchinchi tartibli determinant orqali:
$$S = \frac12\left|\begin{vmatrix} x_1 & y_1 & 1\\ x_2 & y_2 & 1\\ x_3 & y_3 & 1\end{vmatrix}\right|.$$

Determinant nol — uch nuqta bitta to'g'ri chiziqda.

## Parallel ko'chirish

Yangi boshi $O'(a;\ b)$, o'qlar yo'nalishi saqlanadi:
$$x = x' + a,\qquad y = y' + b.$$
$$x' = x - a,\qquad y' = y - b.$$

Fazoda xuddi shunday, $z = z' + c$ qo'shiladi.

---

## Ko'chirish: misol 1

Koordinata boshi $O'(2;\ 3)$ ga ko'chirildi. $M(5;\ 1)$ ning yangi koordinatalari:
$$x' = 5 - 2 = 3,\qquad y' = 1 - 3 = -2.$$

$M'(3;\ -2)$.

---

## Ko'chirish: misol 2

$x^2 + y^2 - 4x + 6y = 3$ — qanday chiziq?

To'liq kvadratlar:
$$(x - 2)^2 - 4 + (y + 3)^2 - 9 = 3 \Rightarrow (x - 2)^2 + (y + 3)^2 = 16.$$

$O'(2;\ -3)$ ga ko'chirsak: $x'^2 + y'^2 = 16$ — markazi $O'$, radiusi 4 bo'lgan aylana.

**Ko'chirish birinchi darajali hadlarni yo'qotadi.**

---

## O'qlarni burish

O'qlar $O$ atrofida $\alpha$ burchakka (soat strelkasiga qarshi) burildi:
$$\begin{cases} x = x'\cos\alpha - y'\sin\alpha\\ y = x'\sin\alpha + y'\cos\alpha\end{cases}$$

Teskari formulalar:
$$\begin{cases} x' = x\cos\alpha + y\sin\alpha\\ y' = -x\sin\alpha + y\cos\alpha\end{cases}$$

---

## Formulaning kelib chiqishi

Yangi bazis: $\mathbf i' = (\cos\alpha;\ \sin\alpha)$, $\mathbf j' = (-\sin\alpha;\ \cos\alpha)$.

$\overrightarrow{OM} = x'\mathbf i' + y'\mathbf j'$ ni koordinatalarda yozamiz — burish formulalari hosil bo'ladi.

Matritsa ko'rinishida (8-mavzu, $X = TX'$):
$$\begin{pmatrix} x\\ y\end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} \cos\alpha & -\sin\alpha\\ \sin\alpha & \cos\alpha\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} x'\\ y'\end{pmatrix}.$$

---

## Burish matritsasi

$$T = \begin{pmatrix} \cos\alpha & -\sin\alpha\\ \sin\alpha & \cos\alpha\end{pmatrix}$$

- **Ortogonal:** $T^TT = E$, $T^{-1} = T^T$.
- $\det T = 1$.
- Masofa va burchaklarni saqlaydi.

Shuning uchun teskari formulalar shunchaki $T^T$ bilan yoziladi.

---

## Burish: misol 1

O'qlar $90^\circ$ ga burildi. $M(3;\ 1)$ ning yangi koordinatalari:
$$x' = 3\cos90^\circ + 1\cdot\sin90^\circ = 1,\qquad y' = -3\sin90^\circ + 1\cdot\cos90^\circ = -3.$$

$M'(1;\ -3)$.

Tekshirish: masofa $\sqrt{9 + 1} = \sqrt{1 + 9}$ ✓

---

## Burish: misol 2 (giperbola)

$xy = 1$. O'qlarni $45^\circ$ ga buramiz:
$$x = \frac{x' - y'}{\sqrt2},\qquad y = \frac{x' + y'}{\sqrt2}.$$

$$xy = \frac{x'^2 - y'^2}{2} = 1 \Rightarrow \frac{x'^2}{2} - \frac{y'^2}{2} = 1.$$

Teng tomonli giperbola, yarim o'qlari $\sqrt2$. **Burish $xy$ hadni yo'qotadi.**

---

## Umumiy almashtirish

Ko'chirish + burish:
$$x = x'\cos\alpha - y'\sin\alpha + a,\qquad y = x'\sin\alpha + y'\cos\alpha + b.$$

Ikkinchi tartibli chiziqni kanonik ko'rinishga keltirish (17-mavzu):
1. burish — $xy$ hadni yo'qotish;
2. ko'chirish — birinchi darajali hadlarni yo'qotish.

---

## Ko'chirish va burish: misol

Boshi $O'(-2;\ 2)$ ga ko'chirildi va o'qlar $180^\circ$ ga burildi. $M(3;\ 4)$:

1. Ko'chirish: $(3 + 2;\ 4 - 2) = (5;\ 2)$.
2. $180^\circ$ burish: $x' = -5$, $y' = -2$.

$M'(-5;\ -2)$.

---

## Qutb koordinatalari

:::tarif
$O$ — qutb, $Ox$ — qutb o'qi. $M$ ning **qutb koordinatalari**:
- $r = |OM| \ge 0$ — qutb radiusi
- $\varphi$ — qutb burchagi ($0 \le \varphi < 2\pi$ yoki $-\pi < \varphi \le \pi$)
:::

$$x = r\cos\varphi,\quad y = r\sin\varphi;\qquad r = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2},\quad \operatorname{tg}\varphi = \frac{y}{x}.$$

---

## Qutb koordinatalari: misollar

:::misol
- $M(-1;\ \sqrt3)$: $r = 2$, II chorak, $\varphi = \frac{2\pi}{3}$.
- $M(-6;\ -8)$: $r = 10$, III chorak.
- $r = 2$, $\varphi = \frac{\pi}{2}$: $M(0;\ 2)$.
:::

:::diqqat
$\operatorname{tg}\varphi$ bir xil bo'lgan ikki burchak $\pi$ ga farq qiladi — **chorakni** tekshiring.
:::

## Qutb tenglamalari

| Tenglama | Chiziq |
|---|---|
| $r = a$ | aylana, markazi $O$ |
| $\varphi = \varphi_0$ | nur |
| $r = 2a\cos\varphi$ | aylana, markazi $(a;\ 0)$ |
| $r = a\varphi$ | Arximed spirali |
| $r = a(1 + \cos\varphi)$ | kardioida |
| $r = \dfrac{p}{1 - e\cos\varphi}$ | konus kesimi (17-mavzu) |

---

## Misol: Dekart → qutb

$x^2 + y^2 = 2ax$ aylana.

$r^2 = 2ar\cos\varphi \Rightarrow r = 2a\cos\varphi$.

Qutb tenglamasi ancha sodda!

Teskari: $r = 4\sin\varphi \Rightarrow r^2 = 4r\sin\varphi \Rightarrow x^2 + y^2 = 4y \Rightarrow x^2 + (y - 2)^2 = 4$.

---

## Kompleks sonlar bilan bog'liqlik

Kompleks sonning trigonometrik shakli (1-mavzu):
$$z = r(\cos\varphi + i\sin\varphi)$$
— aynan $(x;\ y)$ nuqtaning qutb koordinatalari: $r = |z|$, $\varphi = \arg z$.

Kompleks sonlarni ko'paytirish = qutb radiuslarini ko'paytirish + burchaklarni qo'shish.

---

## Silindrik koordinatalar

$(r, \varphi, z)$ — $Oxy$ dagi qutb koordinatalari + applikata:
$$x = r\cos\varphi,\qquad y = r\sin\varphi,\qquad z = z.$$

- $r = R$ — doiraviy **silindr**
- $\varphi = \varphi_0$ — yarim tekislik
- $z = c$ — gorizontal tekislik

Silindrsimon jismlar (quvurlar, simlar) uchun qulay.

---

## Sferik koordinatalar

$(\rho, \theta, \varphi)$:
- $\rho = |OM|$
- $\theta$ — $Oz$ o'qdan og'ish burchagi, $0 \le \theta \le \pi$
- $\varphi$ — uzunlik (azimut) burchagi

$$x = \rho\sin\theta\cos\varphi,\quad y = \rho\sin\theta\sin\varphi,\quad z = \rho\cos\theta.$$

$\rho = R$ — **sfera**.

---

## Sferik koordinatalar: misol

$\rho = 2$, $\theta = \frac{\pi}{2}$, $\varphi = 0$:
$$x = 2\cdot1\cdot1 = 2,\quad y = 0,\quad z = 2\cdot0 = 0.$$

$M(2;\ 0;\ 0)$ — «ekvator»da.

Geografik koordinatalar: kenglik $= 90^\circ - \theta$, uzunlik $= \varphi$.

## Tipik xatolar

- Burish formulalarida $\sin$ oldidagi ishorani chalkashtirish.
- To'g'ri va teskari formulalarni aralashtirib yuborish.
- Qutb burchagini chorakni hisobga olmay topish.
- Sferik koordinatalarda $\theta$ ni kenglik bilan aralashtirish.

---

## Xulosa

- Ko'chirish: $x = x' + a$, $y = y' + b$ — chiziqli hadlarni yo'qotadi.
- Burish: ortogonal matritsa $T$, $X = TX'$ — $xy$ hadni yo'qotadi.
- Qutb: $x = r\cos\varphi$, $y = r\sin\varphi$.
- Silindrik va sferik koordinatalar — fazoviy simmetriyalar uchun.
